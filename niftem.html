About NIFTEM Sonipat

National Institute of Food Technology Entrepreneurship and Management (NIFTEM), Sonipat is a premier autonomous institute under the Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI), Govt. of India. Established in 2012 and granted the status of Institution of National Importance in 2021, NIFTEM is India's leading institution exclusively focused on the global food sector. Located on a 100-acre modern eco-friendly campus in Kundli, Sonipat, it offers specialized UG, PG, Integrated, Executive, and PhD programs in food science, food engineering, agribusiness management, and supply chains. NIFTEM has AICTE approval and Tier-1 International Recognition, attracting students and researchers from across the country.

Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Register on Samarth Portal
Register on the unified Samarth online application portal for NIFTEM. Ensure you have all academic documents ready.
Step 2 — Pay Application Fee
General / OBC / EWS: ₹1,000 | SC / ST / PwD: ₹500. Payment done online through the Samarth portal.
Step 3 — Submit Academic Portfolio
Upload academic portfolio and attach valid national-level entrance scorecards (GATE / CAT / MAT / CMAT / CUET-PG as applicable).
Step 4 — GD & PI (Management Tracks)
Candidates applying for MBA / Executive MBA must attend Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) evaluations.
Step 5 — Merit List & Enrolment
Final selection based on entrance scores and GD/PI performance. Pay fees and complete enrolment formalities on the portal.
Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Appear in JEE Main / CUET-UG
Register and appear for JEE Main (for B.Tech / Integrated B.Tech+MBA) or CUET-UG / Academic Merit (for BBA Hons.) through NTA.
Step 2 — JoSAA / CSAB Counselling (B.Tech)
B.Tech admissions are done exclusively via JoSAA / CSAB centralised counselling. Fill choice-locking as per CRL rank.
Step 3 — Document Verification
Submit 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, JEE scorecard, and photographs at reporting centre.
Step 4 — Fee Payment & Reporting
Pay semester fees (approx. ₹1,22,100/semester for engineering) and report to campus for hostel allocation and orientation.

Placement Notes

NIFTEM's dedicated placement cell facilitates campus recruitment for B.Tech, MBA, and M.Tech students, with a strong focus on the food processing and agribusiness ecosystem. Being India's premier food technology institution under MoFPI, NIFTEM has strategic ties with top food corporations, FMCG majors, and government bodies. Graduates find opportunities in food R&D, food safety, supply chain management, agribusiness consulting, and entrepreneurship. PhD graduates are placed in research institutions and academia.
