About Manav Rachna International Institute of Research and Studies

Manav Rachna International Institute of Research and Studies (MRIIRS) is a prestigious Deemed-to-be-University located in Sector-43, Surajkund Badkhal Road, Faridabad, Haryana. Established in 1997, MRIIRS holds the highest NAAC "A++" accreditation and holds UGC Category-1 Status — a recognition awarded to only the finest universities in India. The university features a sprawling urban infrastructure comprising 14+ dedicated faculties and centres of excellence. MRIIRS offers a wide range of UG, PG, and PhD programs spanning conventional, specialized industry-integrated, and global dual-degree formats, making it a sought-after destination for students across engineering, sciences, management, health sciences, and media studies.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply Online
File your application on the centralized Samarth-linked admission portal at apply.manavrachna.edu.in. Upload all academic dossiers and submit entrance exam scores.
Step 2 — Submit Entrance Scores
Submit valid national test scores: JEE Main / SAT / Uni-Gauge-E for B.Tech; CUET-UG / MRNAT for BCA, B.Sc., B.A., B.Com, B.Des; GATE for M.Tech; CAT / MAT / XAT / CMAT / ATMA for MBA; CUET-PG / MRNAT for M.Sc., M.A., MCA.
Step 3 — Clear MRNAT (if applicable)
Candidates applying without national test scores must clear the internal Manav Rachna National Aptitude Test (MRNAT) evaluation cycles conducted by the university.
Step 4 — Personal Interview / GD (where applicable)
Attend the "Vision & Values" personal interview or corporate group discussions where required, especially for management programs (MBA).
Step 5 — Document Verification & Enrolment
Submit original documents: 10th, 12th / graduation marksheets, category certificates, entrance scorecard, and photographs to confirm admission and complete enrolment.

Placement Notes

MRIIRS maintains an active Training & Placement Cell that facilitates campus recruitment drives across engineering, management, health sciences, and media domains. The university's industry-integrated BCA and global dual-degree programs ensure graduates are job-ready from day one. Engineering & CSE graduates are typically placed in leading IT and tech companies. MBA graduates find roles in top management consultancies and financial institutions. Health Sciences graduates (BPT, Nutrition & Dietetics) are placed in multi-specialty hospitals and wellness chains. PhD scholars are eligible for teaching assistantships and research stipends under university provisions for non-fellowship candidates.
