About DCRUST

Deenbandhu Chhotu Ram University of Science & Technology (DCRUST), Murthal is a premier state university situated on the Delhi–Chandigarh National Highway (NH-44) in Sonepat, Haryana. Originally established as Chhotu Ram State College of Engineering in 1987, it was granted university status in 2006. The university is NAAC 'A' Grade accredited, holds NBA accreditation for 6 B.Tech programmes, and is approved by both UGC under Section 12(B) and AICTE. With a sprawling 273-acre campus and 50+ courses spanning UG, PG, and PhD levels, DCRUST is among Haryana's top technical universities.

Admission Process 2026–27
Step 1 – Appear for Entrance Exam
B.Tech → JEE Main | B.Arch → NATA | M.Tech → GATE | MBA → CMAT/CAT/MAT | M.Sc/MCA → DCRUST Test / CUET-PG
Step 2 – Register on Official Portal
Visit www.dcrustm.org and complete registration with your entrance scores
Step 3 – HSTES Counselling (B.Tech/B.Arch)
Participate in Haryana State Technical Education Society (HSTES) online counselling for seat allotment
Step 4 – Direct Application (PG)
M.Sc, MCA, MBA applicants apply directly via the DCRUST portal
Step 5 – Document Verification & Fee Payment
Submit required documents and pay the admission fee after seat allotment. Application fee: ₹250–₹1,000 (varies by course)
