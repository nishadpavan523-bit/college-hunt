About Sharda University

Sharda University, established in 2009 in Greater Noida, UP, is a premier private university accredited with NAAC "A+" Grade. Recognized by UGC and approved by AICTE, BCI, NMC, PCI and other statutory bodies, it is a fully residential international campus with students from more than 85 countries. It offers UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate and Ph.D. programs in Engineering, Medical Sciences, Management, Law, Architecture, Design, Humanities, Media Studies and Research.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply Online
Apply through the official Sharda University admission portal. Choose your program and fill academic details.
Step 2 — Entrance Exam
Appear in SUAT / JEE Main / NEET-UG / NEET-PG / CAT / CLAT / NATA as per program requirement.
Step 3 — Counselling / Interview
Participate in counselling and interview round if applicable for your chosen program.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester fees online to confirm your seat and complete enrolment formalities.
