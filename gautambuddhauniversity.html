Gautam Buddha University offers undergraduate, postgraduate, integrated, diploma, and doctoral programmes across Engineering & Technology, Management, Science, Law, Pharmacy, and Biotechnology. With a sprawling 511-acre campus in Greater Noida, GBU provides a comprehensive academic ecosystem focused on research, innovation, and industry readiness.

Admission Overview

Admissions at Gautam Buddha University are primarily conducted through the Common University Entrance Test (CUET) for UG and PG programs. M.Tech admissions use GATE scores. Law programs require CLAT. All applications are submitted through the official GBU Admissions Portal.

Program Entrance Exam Conducting Body
B.Tech / BCA / BBA / B.Com / B.Pharmacy CUET UG NTA
BA LLB (Integrated) CLAT Consortium of NLUs
MBA / MCA / M.Sc / M.A / LL.M CUET PG NTA
M.Tech GATE IITs / IISc
PhD Programs GBU Entrance / NET GBU / UGC
Step-by-Step Admission Process
Step 1 – Appear in Entrance Exam
Register and appear for CUET / GATE / CLAT as applicable to your chosen program
Step 2 – Apply on GBU Portal
Fill the application form at gbu.ac.in/admissions with your entrance exam scores and required documents
Step 3 – Merit List & Counselling
GBU releases a merit list; selected candidates are called for document verification and counselling
Step 4 – Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay admission fees online through the portal and complete enrollment formalities to confirm your seat

Placement Overview

GBU actively bridges students with the industry through internships, technical workshops, and industry exposure initiatives. A strong focus on project-based learning and competitive exam preparation (GATE, UPSC, NET) ensures students are well-equipped for both corporate and government sector opportunities.
