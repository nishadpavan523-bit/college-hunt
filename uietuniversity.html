About UIET Chandigarh

UIET Chandigarh (CUH) is a premier central university established in 2002 by an Act of Parliament. Located in Mahendergarh district, Haryana, the university offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across sciences, humanities, management, law, and engineering. CUH is accredited with NAAC "A++" and is known for its research-oriented curriculum, modern infrastructure, and sprawling 484-acre green campus. Being a central university, tuition fees are highly subsidized, making quality education accessible to all.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register for CUET-UG / CUET-PG / JEE Main / NCET as applicable. Deadlines typically Feb–Apr each year.
Step 2 — Appear for Exam
Exams conducted by NTA. CUH accepts scores from all NTA-conducted national entrance tests.
Step 3 — Merit List / Counselling
B.Tech via JoSAA/CSAB counselling. Other programs via CUH's own merit-based counselling.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester fees online via university portal and complete enrolment formalities.

Placement Notes

CUH's placement cell actively facilitates campus recruitment drives for B.Tech students. Companies from IT, ITES, and core engineering domains visit the campus. The average package ranges between ₹8.7 LPA for B.Tech graduates. For PG and PhD students, opportunities exist in research institutions, academia, government jobs, and private enterprises.
