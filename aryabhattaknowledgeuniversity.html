About AKU

Aryabhatta Knowledge University (AKU), Patna is a State Public University established under Bihar Act 24 of 2008, named after the legendary ancient Indian mathematician and astronomer Aryabhatta (476–550 CE). Operational since 26 January 2010, AKU is the apex body governing all technical, medical, management and professional colleges of Bihar, affiliating 114+ colleges across the state. AKU does not directly run colleges — it affiliates, regulates and governs examinations for all affiliated professional institutions in Bihar (except NIT Patna).

Admission Process Overview
Step 1 – Appear in National / State Entrance Exam
JEE Main (B.Tech), NEET-UG (Medical/Pharmacy), GATE (M.Tech), CAT/MAT/SMAT (MBA), Bihar B.Ed CET (B.Ed), GPAT (M.Pharm), BCECEB exams for remaining courses.
Step 2 – BCECEB Counselling Registration
Register on bceceboard.bihar.gov.in. Upload documents: 10+2 marksheet, entrance scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), photo, signature.
Step 3 – Merit List & Seat Allotment
BCECEB releases merit list based on entrance score. Multiple rounds of seat allotment (Round 1, Round 2, Spot Round). Choose preferred college and course from available seats.
Step 4 – Document Verification
Report to allotted college for physical document verification within prescribed date. Carry originals + photocopies of all documents.
Step 5 – Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay college admission fee (online/offline as notified). Collect admission receipt and enrolment number from AKU-affiliated college.
Step 6 – PhD (PAT) – Separate Process
Apply at akubihar.ac.in. PAT-2026 exam: 12 Sep 2026. Shortlisted candidates called for Interview / Research Proposal Presentation. NET/JRF holders exempted from written test.
