About University of Delhi

The University of Delhi (DU), established in 1922, is one of India's most prestigious central universities and an Institution of Eminence (IoE) by UGC. With 91 affiliated colleges, 16 Faculties, 86+ Departments and over 7 lakh students, it is the largest university in India. Accredited with NAAC A++ (CGPA 3.55, valid till 2029), DU is ranked #5 among Universities and #15 Overall (NIRF 2025), and #328 globally (QS 2026). The university spans two main campuses — North Campus (Maurice Nagar) and South Campus (Benito Juarez Marg). With 500+ programs and among the lowest fees of any premier university in India, DU offers an extraordinary academic experience accessible to all.

Admission Process (UG & PG)
Step 1 — Appear for CUET / Other Entrance Exams
Register for CUET-UG (UG) or CUET-PG (PG) on NTA portal. For MBA: CAT; M.Tech: GATE; MBBS/BDS: NEET; LLB: DU LLB Entrance Test.
Step 2 — Register on DU Admission Portal
Visit admission.uod.ac.in and register. Application fee: ₹1,200 (SC/ST/PwD) or ₹1,500 (General/OBC/EWS) via CSAS portal.
Step 3 — Fill Preferences on CSAS Portal
On csas.du.ac.in, fill course and college preferences in order of priority. Seat allotment is merit-based from CUET scores.
Step 4 — Merit List & Seat Allotment (Round-wise)
DU releases seat allotment in multiple rounds. Accept/upgrade your seat on the CSAS portal within the given window.
Step 5 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Verify originals at allotted college: 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, and photographs. Pay fees online to confirm.
