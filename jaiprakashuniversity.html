About Jai Prakash University

Jai Prakash University (JPU) is a prominent state university established in 1990 and located in Chapra, Saran district, Bihar. Named after the legendary freedom fighter and social reformer Jai Prakash Narayan, the university oversees multiple constituent and affiliated colleges across Saran, Siwan, and Gopalganj districts. It is NAAC Accredited and recognized by the UGC. JPU offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, professional, and doctoral programs spanning Arts, Science, Commerce, Management, Education, and Computer Applications, making quality higher education accessible to the students of Bihar.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply via JPU Portal
Register on the JPU admission portal, fill in the application form, and upload required documents.
Step 2 — Merit List / Entrance Result
For most UG/PG programs, admission is merit-based. Professional courses may require a university-conducted entrance test.
Step 3 — Counselling
Attend counselling session as per the schedule. Seat allotment done based on merit and category.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th & 12th marksheets, graduation certificate (PG/PhD), category certificate, ID proof, and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the semester/annual fees and complete enrolment formalities at the respective college or university department.

Placement Notes

JPU's placement cell provides active assistance to students through campus recruitment drives and interview preparation. The major recruiting sectors include IT, Banking, Education, and Sales. The highest package recorded is approximately ₹5–9 LPA while the average package stands at ₹2.5–4 LPA. Internship opportunities are available in selected professional departments, along with dedicated aptitude training and mock interview sessions for final-year students.
