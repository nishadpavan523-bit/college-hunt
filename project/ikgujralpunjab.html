About IKGPTU

I.K. Gujral Punjab Technical University (IKGPTU), formerly known as Punjab Technical University (PTU), was established in 1997 by the Government of Punjab and renamed in honour of former Prime Minister I.K. Gujral in 2015. It is one of Punjab's premier technical and professional universities, recognized by UGC and accredited by NAAC. The university affiliates over 250 institutions across Punjab, making it one of the largest technical university systems in North India. With constituent campuses in Jalandhar, Amritsar, Mohali, and Hoshiarpur, IKGPTU offers programs in Engineering & Technology, Management, Pharmacy, Architecture, Hospitality, Applied Sciences, and Humanities. It also provides flexible Distance Education and online learning programs for working professionals.

Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), NATA (B.Arch), or prepare merit documents for BCA, BBA, B.Com, B.Pharm, BHMCT, and other programs as applicable.
Step 2 — Register via Centralised Counselling / University Portal
B.Tech admissions are conducted through centralized Punjab state counselling. Other programs are admitted through the university's online admission portal.
Step 3 — Merit List and Seat Allotment
Seats are allotted based on entrance exam scores and qualifying marks. Candidates can choose from main campus, constituent campuses, or affiliated colleges.
Step 4 — Document Verification and Fee Payment
Submit 10th and 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, and entrance scorecard. Complete fee payment and enrolment formalities at the allotted institution.
Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Check Eligibility and Program
Confirm eligibility: graduation in a relevant discipline with the minimum qualifying percentage. Prepare national exam scores (GATE / CMAT / CAT / MAT) as applicable.
Step 2 — Apply via University Admission Portal
Register on the IKGPTU admission portal, fill the application form, and pay the registration fee to confirm your application.
Step 3 — Entrance Score Submission / University Criteria
Submit GATE scores for M.Tech, CMAT/CAT/MAT scores for MBA. MCA and M.Sc. admissions are based on graduation merit or university entrance criteria.
Step 4 — Document Verification and Enrolment
Submit degree certificates, marksheets, category certificate, and ID proof. Pay the semester fee to complete enrolment.

Placement Notes

IKGPTU has an active Training & Placement Department that facilitates campus recruitment across its main campus and its vast network of over 250 affiliated institutions. Top IT companies including Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Cognizant, HCL, IBM, and Amazon are regular recruiters for B.Tech, MCA, and BCA graduates. The university provides technical training, placement preparation programs, and industry certification opportunities to bridge academics and industry. The university's extensive Punjab-wide network gives students broad geographic access to job markets in North India.
