About Banaras Hindu University

Banaras Hindu University (BHU), founded on 4 February 1916 by Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya, is a premier central university and Institution of Eminence (GoI). Recognised as an Institution of National Importance, BHU is the largest residential university in Asia, spanning a 1,300-acre campus in Varanasi with an additional 2,700-acre Rajiv Gandhi South Campus in Mirzapur. Upgraded to NAAC A++ in 2025 and ranked #6 University and #10 Overall (NIRF 2025), BHU houses 6 Institutes, 14 Faculties, 140+ Departments, and offers 600+ courses to 35,000+ students from 48+ countries. It is approved by UGC, AICTE, NMC, BCI, PCI, ICAR and AIU.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear for Entrance Exam
Register and appear for CUET-UG / CUET-PG / NEET-UG / NEET-PG / CAT / GATE / JEE Main+Advanced as applicable. Conducted by NTA / IITs.
Step 2 — Apply on BHU Portal
Visit bhuonline.in and fill the online application form after entrance exam results. Select preferred programme and category.
Step 3 — BHU CAP Counselling
Participate in BHU's Combined Allotment Programme (CAP). Seat allotment is merit-based on entrance scores across programmes.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th, 12th, graduation marksheets, category certificate, ID proof and photographs at reporting centre.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allotment
Pay semester/annual fees online. Apply for hostel accommodation. SC/ST/PwD students with income below ₹2.5L get full tuition waiver.
