About Panjab University

Panjab University is one of India's oldest and most prestigious universities. Originally established in Lahore in 1882, it was re-established in Chandigarh after the partition of India. The university consistently ranks among India's top public universities and is known for excellence in academics, research, sports, innovation, and student life. It holds a NAAC A++ Grade — the highest accreditation level in India — and features in QS Asia University Rankings. Spread across a sprawling 550-acre campus, the university covers Engineering, Science, Arts, Commerce, Law, Medical Sciences, and Education.

Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech via JAC Chandigarh), PU BA LLB Entrance (Law), or qualify with merit marks for B.Sc., B.A., B.Com, BBA, BCA programs.
Step 2 — Apply via Official Admission Portal
Register at the official Panjab University admission portal at puchd.ac.in, fill the application form, and pay the registration fee.
Step 3 — Merit List / JAC Counselling
B.Tech admissions via JAC Chandigarh counselling. Other UG programs via PU merit list based on qualifying exam scores and 10+2 marks.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Submit 10th and 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, entrance scorecard, and photographs. Pay semester/annual fees online to complete enrolment.
Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for GATE (M.Tech), PU MET or CAT (MBA), or respective PU entrance tests for MCA, M.Sc., M.A., M.Com, LL.M., and other PG programs.
Step 2 — Apply via Admission Portal
Register at puchd.ac.in, fill the PG application form, and submit the registration fee online through the portal.
Step 3 — Merit List / Interview
MBA shortlisted candidates appear for a Personal Interview round. Other PG programs are offered admission through PU merit / entrance list.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Enrolment
Submit original degree certificates, marksheets, category certificate, and ID proof. Complete fee payment and enrolment formalities to confirm your seat.

Placement Notes

Panjab University's dedicated Training & Placement Cell actively facilitates campus recruitment across its diverse departments. The university's engineering institute UIET attracts top IT companies like Microsoft, Amazon, IBM, and Infosys with competitive packages. MBA graduates find opportunities in management consulting, banking, and finance. The university also offers internship opportunities alongside placements. Its location in Chandigarh — a major administrative and IT hub — gives students excellent access to recruiters from the public and private sectors across North India.
