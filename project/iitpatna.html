About IIT Patna

Indian Institute of Technology Patna (IIT Patna) is a premier Institute of National Importance established in 2008 by an Act of Parliament. Located in Bihta, Patna, Bihar, it offers programs in Engineering, Sciences, Humanities, and Management. The institute is recognized by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, and is ranked among the top engineering institutes in India by NIRF. The campus features modern laboratories, innovation centers, smart classrooms, and research facilities spread across 500+ acres.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for JEE Main
Register for JEE Main conducted by NTA. Top scorers qualify for JEE Advanced registration.
Step 2 — Appear for JEE Advanced
Sit for JEE Advanced exam and secure a rank in the IIT rank list.
Step 3 — JoSAA Counselling
Participate in JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) counselling to select IIT Patna branch.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester fees online via the institute portal and complete enrolment formalities.

Placement Notes

IIT Patna's Training & Placement Cell actively invites top national and international companies. Average CTC for B.Tech graduates ranges between ₹10–16 LPA, with top offers exceeding ₹30+ LPA. Scholarships include Merit-cum-Means, SC/ST schemes, NSP, and the Prime Minister's Research Fellowship (PMRF) for PhD students.
