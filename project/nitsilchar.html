About NIT Silchar

National Institute of Technology Silchar (NIT-S) is one of India's 31 NITs and was declared an Institute of National Importance under the NIT Act, 2007. Founded in 1967 as a Regional Engineering College (REC), it is located in the scenic Barak Valley of Assam, spread across approximately 975 acres of lush green campus. NIT Silchar is recognised by UGC and AICTE, with several engineering programs holding NBA Accreditation. The institute offers programs across Engineering, Science, Management, and Humanities & Social Sciences.

The institute boasts an impressive NIRF 2024 ranking of #68 Overall and #44 in Engineering, and consistently places its graduates at top multinational companies. Its highest placement package stands at ₹42 LPA with a strong median package of ₹9.2 LPA. B.Tech admissions are exclusively via JEE Main score through JoSAA/CSAB Counselling — NIT Silchar being one of the most sought-after NITs in the Northeast region of India.

B.Tech Admission Process (JoSAA)
01Appear in JEE Main
Register and appear for JEE Main (conducted by NTA, usually in January and April). Qualify the exam by securing a valid rank. The NIT Silchar cutoff rank varies by branch — CSE and ECE have the most competitive cutoffs. Both sessions are attempted for a better percentile.
02JoSAA Registration
Post JEE Main results, register on the JoSAA portal (josaa.nic.in). Only candidates with a valid JEE Main rank are eligible. Registration is free. Fill in essential personal, academic, and contact details carefully. Both JEE Main and JEE Advanced rank holders can participate in JoSAA counselling.
03Fill Choice of Institutes & Branches
In the JoSAA Choice Filling window, you can add NIT Silchar as a preferred institute and select up to 25,000 institute-branch combinations in order of preference. Use previous year's opening/closing ranks for each category (General, EWS, OBC-NCL, SC, ST) as a reference to build a balanced choice list.
04Seat Allotment Rounds
JoSAA conducts 5 rounds of seat allotment (extended rounds also exist via CSAB). Check your allotment result on the portal. If you receive a seat at NIT Silchar in your preferred branch, you can accept the seat, freeze it, or float/slide to higher preferences in subsequent rounds. Carefully choose between 'Freeze', 'Float', and 'Slide' options based on your target.
05Online Reporting & Fee Payment
After seat allotment, complete online reporting by paying the seat acceptance fee (₹15,000–₹35,000 depending on category; adjusted against final tuition fee). Upload required documents: Class 10 & 12 Marksheets, JEE scorecard, category certificate, photo ID, passport photos, and medical fitness certificate.
06Physical Reporting at NIT Silchar
After final allotment and fee payment, report physically to NIT Silchar on the designated date for document verification. Original and attested copies of all documents are mandatory. Medical examination may be conducted. Pay the remaining tuition and hostel fees at the accounts section.
07Admission Confirmation & Orientation
After successful document verification and fee payment, your admission to NIT Silchar is confirmed. You receive an enrollment number and details about hostel allotment. Attend the freshers' orientation program organized by the institute, which is followed by commencement of classes in the first semester.

Scholarships & Financial Aid
🎓
Free Studentship (SC/ST/PwD) — Full fee waiver for SC, ST, and PwD students as per GoI norms. No tuition fee charged throughout the B.Tech program.
💰
Merit-cum-Means (MCM) Scholarship — For students with family income below ₹4.5 Lakh/yr; covers tuition fee + ₹1,000/month. Up to 25% of students eligible annually.
🏛️
AICTE Pragati/Saksham/Swanath Scholarships — For girl students, PwD students, and children of armed forces/CAPF. ₹50,000/yr financial assistance.
🔬
GATE Scholarship (M.Tech) — ₹12,400/month stipend for full-time GATE-qualified M.Tech students funded by MHRD/GoI.
📜
State Government Scholarships — Assam and other state government merit scholarships applicable for students domiciled in respective states.
🌐
Prime Minister's Special Scholarship Scheme (PMSSS) — For J&K domicile students. Covers tuition fee up to ₹1.25 Lakh/yr + ₹1 Lakh/yr maintenance allowance.

Placement Notes

NIT Silchar is one of the most reputed NITs in Eastern and Northeast India, with a steadily growing placement record. The Training & Placement Cell (TPC) maintains strong industry ties across sectors. CSE, ECE, MnC, and EE branches see the best placement performance. In 2023–24, over 170 companies visited campus making more than 1,100+ job offers. The institute also has an active internship program, with students interning at major product companies. A strong and growing alumni network across India and abroad (US, Europe, Singapore) actively supports referrals and recruitments every cycle.

🌿 Campus Atmosphere

NIT Silchar's sprawling 975-acre lush green campus in the Barak Valley of Assam offers a serene, nature-rich environment perfect for focused study and holistic development. The campus is surrounded by beautiful hills and tea gardens, offering a unique scenic experience unlike any other NIT. With 13+ hostels, multiple eateries, and a vibrant social scene, the campus life at NIT Silchar is enriching, diverse, and memorable. Students from all parts of India and a few international students call this campus home, making it a truly multicultural community.
