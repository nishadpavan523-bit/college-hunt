About MAHE

Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE), popularly known as Manipal University, is one of India's most prestigious private deemed-to-be universities, established in 1953 with Kasturba Medical College as its founding institution. Granted deemed-university status in 1993, MAHE is accredited by NAAC with A++ grade and holds the coveted Institution of Eminence (IoE) designation from the Government of India — one of only 20 such institutions in the country.

Ranked #3 University and #14 Overall by NIRF 2025, and placed in the QS World Rankings 901–950, MAHE operates across multiple campuses — Manipal (flagship), Bengaluru, Mangaluru, and Jamshedpur — with offshore campuses in Dubai (UAE) and Malaysia. The flagship Manipal campus, in the coastal university town of Udupi district, hosts 22+ constituent institutions including the renowned MIT (Manipal Institute of Technology), Kasturba Medical College, Manipal College of Pharmaceutical Sciences, and the T.A. Pai Management Institute (TAPMI).

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register on the MAHE Admissions Portal
Visit the official admissions portal at admissions.manipal.edu. Create an account with your email ID and mobile number. Select the campus (Manipal / Bengaluru / Mangaluru) and preferred course. Pay the application fee (₹700 – ₹1,500 depending on course).
Step 2 — Appear for MET / Submit National Exam Score
For B.Tech, appear for the Manipal Entrance Test (MET) — conducted online in multiple cities. For MBBS/BDS, qualify NEET UG. For MBA, submit CAT / MAT / GMAT / NMAT scores. For M.Tech, submit GATE scores. The merit list is prepared with MET score (50%) + 10+2 marks (50%) for B.Tech.
Step 3 — Fill Online Application & Upload Documents
Complete the detailed application form with personal information, academic details, and category (SC/ST/OBC/PwD/NRI). Upload 10th & 12th marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), passport-size photograph, and ID proof.
Step 4 — Rank / Merit List Declaration
MAHE releases MET rank lists and course-wise merit lists on the official admissions portal. Candidates are shortlisted based on composite scores. For MBA, shortlisted candidates are called for Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) rounds conducted at designated centres.
Step 5 — Online Counselling & Seat Allocation
MAHE conducts online counselling rounds. Candidates fill in branch / program preferences based on their rank. Seats are allocated across Manipal, Bengaluru, and Mangaluru campuses. For MBBS, centralised counselling is through MCC / KEA for Karnataka domicile students.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Reporting to Campus
Confirm your admission by paying the first-year / semester fee online. Report to the allotted campus with original documents for physical verification: marksheets, migration & transfer certificate, entrance scorecard, category certificate, domicile certificate, and photographs. Hostel allocation is done separately.

Campus Life — Manipal University Town

Manipal is a planned university town entirely built around the university — often called the "Student Paradise" of India. With 28,000+ students from 57+ countries, it offers a truly cosmopolitan, self-contained campus experience unlike any other university in India.

🎉 Signature Events & Fests
⚡
TechTatva — MAHE's massive annual National Technical Festival hosted by MIT Manipal, attracting 15,000+ students from 500+ colleges with robotics, coding contests, hackathons, and tech exhibitions
🎭
