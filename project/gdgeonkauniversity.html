About GD Goenka University

GD Goenka University, established in 2013, is a premier private university located on Sohna Road, Gurugram, Haryana. The university is recognized by the UGC and holds approvals from BCI, PCI, COA, and NCTE. Offering more than 100 programs across engineering, management, law, pharmacy, medical sciences, architecture, and design, GD Goenka University is known for its modern residential campus, industry-linked curriculum, and strong placement support. The university fosters an environment of innovation and holistic development through its state-of-the-art facilities.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply Online
Apply through the university's online admission portal at gdgoenkauniversity.com
Step 2 — Upload Documents
Submit academic certificates, ID proof, category certificate, and passport-size photographs
Step 3 — Entrance / Interview
Appear for entrance examination or personal interview if applicable to your program
Step 4 — Merit List & Counselling
Merit list is published followed by a structured counselling process for seat allotment
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Confirmation
Pay semester fees online through the university portal and confirm your seat allocation
