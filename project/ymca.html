About J.C. Bose University (YMCA)

J.C. Bose University of Science and Technology, YMCA — formerly YMCA University of Science and Technology — is a prestigious State Government University established in 1969 in Faridabad, Haryana. Accredited with NAAC "A+" Grade and recognised by UGC, AICTE, and NBA, the university is one of the most respected technical institutions in the Delhi-NCR region. Originally established as an Indo-German collaborative project with the National Council of YMCAs of India to provide practical-oriented technical education, the university now offers 150+ programs across Engineering, Technology, Management, Computer Applications, Sciences, Humanities, and Media Studies. Its urban residential campus in Faridabad gives students excellent access to the Delhi-NCR corporate and industrial corridor.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Visit the Admission Portal
Navigate to the university's official admission portal at jcboseust.ac.in and register your applicant account online.
Step 2 — Fill Application Form & Upload Documents
Fill the online application form and upload your academic documents including marksheets, certificates, and photo ID.
Step 3 — Appear for Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), GATE (M.Tech), or CAT/MAT/CMAT (MBA) as applicable. Other programs follow merit-based selection.
Step 4 — Counselling / Merit List
B.Tech admissions via HSTES (Haryana State Technical Education Society) counselling. Other programs via university merit list.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit original marksheets, degree certificates, category certificate, entrance scorecard, and ID proof for physical verification.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Seat Confirmation
Pay admission fees online or at the university fee counter to confirm your seat and complete enrolment formalities.

Placement Highlights

J.C. Bose University (YMCA) is well-known for its strong engineering and IT placement record. The university's dedicated Training & Placement Cell actively supports students with campus recruitment, industrial internships, and technical training opportunities. Its Faridabad location within the Delhi-NCR corridor gives students exceptional access to top-tier IT companies and multinational corporations. The university is particularly noted for its coding culture and affordable technical education. Industrial training exposure and an active placement ecosystem ensure that engineering and IT graduates are consistently placed in leading national and multinational companies.
