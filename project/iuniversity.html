About Integral University

Integral University, Lucknow is a premier NAAC A+ accredited private university established in 2004, recognized by UGC under Sections 2(f) and 12(B). Located in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, the university offers over 225 programs across 11 faculties including Engineering, Medical Sciences, Pharmacy, Architecture, Law, Management, Commerce, Humanities, Sciences, Agriculture, and Nursing. With a student strength of 15,000+ and over 3,500 hostellers, the university is NIRF ranked and holds multiple statutory approvals including NMC, PCI, BCI, COA, and INC. It is also recognized as a Scientific & Industrial Research Organisation (SIRO) by DSIR with NABL-accredited laboratories and a NABH-accredited hospital.

UG Admission Process
Step 1 — Online Application
Visit the official Integral University website (iul.ac.in) and fill out the online application form for your desired program.
Step 2 — IUET / Merit / National Entrance Exam
Appear for IUET (Integral University Entrance Test) or submit relevant national exam scores. Medical programs require NEET; B.Arch requires NATA; Law may use CLAT.
Step 3 — Document Submission
Upload 10th & 12th marksheets, transfer certificate, category certificate (if applicable), entrance scorecard, and ID proof to the admissions portal.
Step 4 — Merit List & Seat Confirmation
Merit list is published on the university website. Confirm your seat by paying the admission fee within the stipulated time.
Step 5 — Scholarship Assessment
Apply for applicable scholarships at the time of admission — merit-based, minority, sports, EWS, and government scholarship schemes are available.
PG / PhD Admission Process
M.Tech — GATE Score / IUET
Apply with a valid GATE score in the relevant engineering discipline, or via IUET / merit where applicable.
MBA — CAT / MAT / IUET + GD/PI
Submit CAT / MAT / CMAT scores or appear for IUET. Group Discussion and Personal Interview may be conducted for final selection.
MD / MS — NEET PG
Medical PG admissions are based on NEET PG scores and centralized counselling by the respective authority.
PhD — Entrance Exam + Interview
Apply via the official portal. Appear for the PhD entrance examination followed by an interview before the departmental selection committee. Merit list published after evaluation.
