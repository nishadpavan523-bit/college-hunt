About NIT Patna

National Institute of Technology Patna is one of India's premier technical institutions, originally established in 1886 as Bihar College of Engineering — one of the oldest engineering colleges in the country. Granted the status of NIT and declared an Institute of National Importance in 2004 by the Government of India, it is governed by the Ministry of Education. The institute offers a wide range of programmes spanning Engineering, Architecture, Science, Humanities, Management, and Computer Applications at the UG, PG, and PhD levels. The main campus is located at Ashok Rajpath, Patna, with ongoing infrastructure expansion projects to support its growing academic community.

Admission Process
Step 1 – Appear for Relevant Entrance Exam
B.Tech/B.Arch → JEE Main | M.Tech → GATE | MCA → NIMCET | MBA → CAT/MAT/CMAT | PhD → UGC-NET / GATE / Institute Test
Step 2 – Participate in Counselling
B.Tech/B.Arch → JoSAA Counselling | M.Tech → CCMT Counselling | MCA → NIMCET Counselling
Step 3 – Upload Required Documents
Submit academic certificates, category certificates, ID proof, and entrance scorecard on the portal
Step 4 – Seat Allotment & Verification
Seat is allotted based on rank and preferences; physical verification at the institute may be required
Step 5 – Fee Payment & Confirmation
Pay the prescribed fee online and confirm admission to secure your seat
