About Manipal University Jaipur
🏛️ UGC Recognised Deemed University · NAAC 'A' Grade Accredited

Manipal University Jaipur (MUJ), established in 2011, is a premier private deemed university located on a sprawling 122-acre campus in Dehmi Kalan, Jaipur. Accredited with NAAC A Grade and ranked among India's top private universities by NIRF, MUJ is part of the globally recognised Manipal Education Group. The university offers over 200+ programs across 20+ Schools including Engineering, Technology, Management, Law, Pharmacy, Architecture, Design, Sciences, Journalism, and Humanities. With a strong focus on industry-oriented education, research, and innovation, MUJ has built a robust placement ecosystem and strong industry partnerships with 500+ companies recruiting from campus.

Admission Process — MET Based
Step 1 — Register for MET (Manipal Entrance Test)
Register on the official MUJ admissions portal (jaipur.manipal.edu) for the Manipal Entrance Test (MET). MET is conducted online across multiple cities. Alternatively, candidates can apply using JEE Main / NATA / CLAT / GATE / CAT scores for respective programs.
Step 2 — Appear in MET / Qualifying Exam
MET is a computer-based test covering Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics / Biology and English. Duration: 2.5 hours. Candidates applying via JEE Main / CLAT / GATE / CAT can skip MET and apply directly with their scores.
Step 3 — Apply to MUJ Jaipur
After MET results, apply to MUJ Jaipur on the admissions portal. Submit MET / JEE / GATE score, 12th board marks, program and specialisation preference, and personal details.
Step 4 — Merit List & Counselling
MUJ publishes merit lists based on MET / JEE / qualifying exam scores. Shortlisted candidates are called for personal interaction and document verification. Multiple rounds of allotment are conducted.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, MET / JEE / qualifying exam scorecard, category certificate, transfer / migration certificate, passport photos, and medical fitness certificate.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay the semester fee online to secure the seat. Report to campus with original documents for enrollment. Collect student ID and hostel allotment letter to formally begin your academic journey at MUJ Jaipur.
