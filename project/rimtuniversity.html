About RIMT University

RIMT University is a multidisciplinary private university located in Mandi Gobindgarh, Punjab. Evolved from the RIMT Group of Institutions — active in higher education since 1998 — the university was formally established in 2015 under the Punjab State Legislature Act. It is recognized by UGC and holds approvals from AICTE, PCI (Pharmacy), BCI (Law), and NCTE (Education). Spread across a 50+ acre residential campus, RIMT offers programs across 10+ distinct schools covering Engineering & Technology, Management, Agriculture, Law, Pharmacy, Health Sciences, Sciences, Humanities, Education, and Hotel Management. With Diploma, UG, PG, and PhD programs, RIMT is a comprehensive destination for professional and academic growth in northern India.

Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), or prepare merit documents for BCA, BBA, B.Com, Agriculture, Law, Pharmacy, and other programs as applicable.
Step 2 — Apply via University Admission Portal
Register at the official RIMT University admissions portal, fill the application form, and pay the registration fee online.
Step 3 — Merit List / Counselling
Admissions are processed through the university counselling process based on entrance scores and qualifying marks for the respective program.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Submit 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, entrance scorecard, and photographs. Complete fee payment and complete enrolment formalities.
Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Choose Program & Check Eligibility
Confirm eligibility: graduation in a relevant discipline with the minimum qualifying percentage as per university norms.
Step 2 — Submit National Score or University Criteria
Submit CAT/MAT/CMAT scores for MBA or GATE scores for M.Tech. Other programs admit on merit or university entrance criteria.
Step 3 — Apply via Admission Portal & Pay Fee
Register on the RIMT University admission portal, fill the form, and pay the registration fee to confirm your application.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Enrolment
Submit degree certificates, marksheets, category certificate, and ID proof. Pay the semester/annual fee to complete enrolment.

Placement Notes

RIMT University has a dedicated Training & Placement Department that facilitates campus recruitment across its diverse schools. The university's strong ties with leading IT companies — Infosys, TCS, Wipro, HCL, Cognizant, and Accenture — provide excellent placement opportunities for Engineering, BCA, and MCA graduates. Management graduates are recruited by banking and financial services firms including ICICI Bank and Axis Bank. The university's Punjab location near industrial zones and its focus on practical training and internship programs helps bridge the gap between academics and industry requirements.
