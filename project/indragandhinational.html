About IGNTU

Indira Gandhi National Tribal University (IGNTU) was established in 2007 by the Government of India with a special focus on providing quality higher education and research opportunities to tribal communities. Located in the serene town of Amarkantak, Madhya Pradesh — known for its rich biodiversity, forests, and natural heritage — the university holds a NAAC Accreditation and is recognized by UGC, AICTE, NCTE, and BCI. With a sprawling 374-acre green campus, IGNTU offers regular, research, and professional programs across arts, science, engineering, law, management, and education. Its proximity to forest zones makes it an exceptional destination for biodiversity, tribal studies, and environmental research.

Admission Process
UG Admission: Apply via CUET-UG (NTA) at cuet.nta.nic.in. B.Tech also accepts JEE Main. IGNTU admissions portal: igntu.ac.in/admission.aspx
Step 1 — Register for CUET-UG / JEE Main / NCET
Most programs: Register for CUET-UG at cuet.nta.nic.in. B.Tech candidates can also register for JEE Main. B.Ed candidates can appear in CUET-UG or NCET.
Step 2 — Appear in Entrance Examination
Appear in CUET-UG. Eligibility: Passed 10+2 from a recognized board. B.Tech requires Physics + Mathematics. B.Pharm requires PCB or PCM. Minimum 50% for General; 45% for SC/ST/PwD categories.
Step 3 — Check IGNTU Merit List / Counselling
After CUET-UG results, IGNTU releases merit lists based on scores. Register for IGNTU counselling through the university admissions portal at igntu.ac.in/admission.aspx.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment & Document Verification
Documents required: 10+2 marksheet, caste/category certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, CUET scorecard. Document verification is conducted at the university or designated venue.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allocation
Pay admission fee as per category. Hostel facilities are available for boys and girls on a first-come basis. Mess and accommodation charges apply separately.
Step 6 — PG & PhD Admission (Separate Process)
PG: Apply via CUET-PG at cuet.nta.nic.in. IGNTU conducts counselling post-result. PhD: Admission through UGC-NET / CSIR-NET / GATE or university entrance test + interview + research proposal.

Career Pathways & Research Opportunities

IGNTU's Central University status and NAAC accreditation make its degrees widely recognized. The peaceful academic environment is highly suited for students preparing for UPSC, NET/JRF, and teaching examinations. Its location near forests and biodiversity zones provides unique exposure for tribal studies, biodiversity, and environmental research. Students are encouraged to participate in internships, seminars, and workshops to enhance their career readiness.
