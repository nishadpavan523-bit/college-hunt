About SGPGIMS

Sanjay Gandhi Postgraduate Institute of Medical Sciences (SGPGIMS), Lucknow is one of India's top super-speciality medical institutes, established by the Government of Uttar Pradesh in 1983. Spread over approximately 550 acres, the institute is nationally recognized for advanced medical education, patient care, research, and super-speciality healthcare services. It houses over 1600 hospital beds, advanced ICU and HDU facilities, modern operation theatres, organ transplant services, and cancer care facilities. SGPGIMS is consistently ranked among the top medical institutions in India by NIRF and holds a NAAC A++ accreditation.
