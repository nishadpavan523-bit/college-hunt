About University of Kashmir

The University of Kashmir, popularly known as Kashmir University (KU), is a State Public University established in 1948 and located at Hazratbal, Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir. It is accredited with the prestigious NAAC A++ Grade — the highest accreditation category — and ranked 34th among Universities in India in NIRF 2025. The university spans a scenic campus of approximately 247 acres along the banks of Dal Lake and is recognized by UGC, AICTE, AIU, and ACU. It is structured into 14 Schools, 50+ Departments, and 21 Research Centres, with satellite campuses at Zakura, Baramulla, Anantnag, and Kupwara. The Institute of Technology at Zakura Campus offers B.Tech programs through JEE Main. Admissions to UG and PG programs are conducted through CUET-UG and CUET-PG respectively.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for CUET-UG / CUET-PG (where applicable)
Appear for the Common University Entrance Test conducted by NTA — CUET-UG for undergraduate programs and CUET-PG for postgraduate programs. For B.Tech programs at the Institute of Technology, Zakura Campus, a valid JEE Main score is required instead of CUET.
Step 2 — Apply through Kashmir University Admission Portal
Visit the official Kashmir University admissions portal and register using your valid email ID and mobile number. Keep your CUET/JEE scorecard, academic documents, and category details ready before starting the application process.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of all required documents including 10th and 12th marksheets, certificates, category certificate (if applicable), domicile/domicile proof, passport-size photographs, and CUET/JEE scorecard in the prescribed format and file size.
Step 4 — Merit List / Entrance Examination
Kashmir University releases merit lists based on CUET scores, JEE Main scores, or university entrance test results depending on the program. Check the official university website regularly for merit list announcements and shortlisting updates.
Step 5 — Counselling & Document Verification
Shortlisted candidates are called for counselling in order of merit. Report to the university with original documents for physical verification: marksheets, certificates, CUET/JEE admit card and scorecard, category and domicile certificates, and migration certificate.
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed program fees (tuition, hostel, mess, and other charges) as per the Kashmir University fee schedule within the stipulated deadline. Late fee payment may result in forfeiture of the seat, so complete the payment process promptly.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation
Complete enrollment formalities, submit all required documents, and confirm your admission for the academic year. Collect your admission letter and enrollment number from the university. Report to your respective department on the date specified in the admission schedule.

Placement Notes

University of Kashmir has a dedicated Placement & Career Counselling Cell that conducts regular internship and skill-development programs throughout the academic year. Placements are comparatively stronger in MBA, MCA, IT, and Management programs. Students are recruited primarily in Banking, IT, Education, and Government sectors. A significant number of students also pursue higher education and research opportunities after graduation. The university's NAAC A++ status and NIRF ranking strengthen its placement credibility with national and regional recruiters.
