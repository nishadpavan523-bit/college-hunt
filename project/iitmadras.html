About IIT Madras

Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IITM) is one of India's foremost institutes of higher education and research, established in 1959 with German technical collaboration. Located in the heart of Chennai on a lush 617-acre forested campus that is home to spotted deer and blackbucks, IITM is India's #1 ranked institution in the NIRF rankings for multiple consecutive years. It is an Institute of National Importance recognized by UGC and the Government of India. With 16 academic departments, numerous research centres, and India's first on-campus incubator (IITM Research Park), it is a global powerhouse in engineering, science, and innovation. IIT Madras also runs a unique BS in Data Science & Applications online degree program open to all.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify Entrance Examination
Appear and qualify in JEE Advanced (B.Tech / Dual Degree), GATE (M.Tech / MS), IIT JAM (M.Sc), CAT (MBA), or CEED (M.Des) as applicable.
Step 2 — Register on Admission Portal
Register on JoSAA portal for UG programs or the IIT Madras PG admission portal (https://research.iitm.ac.in) using your entrance exam roll number.
Step 3 — Counselling / Seat Allotment
Fill branch preferences in JoSAA for B.Tech / Dual Degree. PG applicants are shortlisted based on GATE/JAM score and appear for written test / interview.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, JEE/GATE score card, category certificate, ID proof, and passport photographs for verification.
Step 5 — Seat Acceptance & Fee Payment
Accept the allotted seat online and pay the semester fee and hostel charges to confirm enrollment at IIT Madras.
Step 6 — Reporting & Registration
Report to IIT Madras campus for physical verification, medical check-up, and formal academic registration for your program.
