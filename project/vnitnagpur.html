About VNIT Nagpur

Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT) Nagpur is one of India's premier National Institutes of Technology, established in 1960 as the Visvesvaraya Regional College of Engineering (VRCE). Located in Nagpur, Maharashtra, it is recognized as an Institute of National Importance by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, and is accredited by NAAC. Spread across a lush 215-acre campus, VNIT offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in Engineering, Architecture, Sciences, and Humanities. Admissions to B.Tech programs are through JEE Main + JoSAA, and M.Tech through GATE + CCMT. VNIT is ranked consistently among the Top NITs in India in NIRF rankings and has a strong alumni network across industry and academia.

Admission Process (B.Tech)
Step 1 — Qualify JEE Main
Appear for JEE Main (Paper 1 for B.Tech / Paper 2A for B.Arch) conducted by NTA. A valid JEE Main score is mandatory for VNIT admission through the central counselling process.
Step 2 — Register through JoSAA / CSAB
Register on the JoSAA portal (josaa.nic.in) using your JEE Main roll number and other credentials. CSAB is used for subsequent special rounds if seats remain vacant after JoSAA rounds.
Step 3 — Choice Filling & Locking
Fill and lock your preferences for institute, branch, and category carefully. VNIT Nagpur seats can be selected across different branches based on your rank and category.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is done based on JEE Main rank, category, and choices filled. Multiple rounds are conducted. Accept the seat and pay the seat acceptance fee within the deadline.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, JEE Main scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, ID proof, passport photographs, and transfer certificate.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed tuition fee and complete all enrollment formalities at VNIT Nagpur within the stipulated deadline to confirm your admission.

Placement Notes

VNIT Nagpur is among the top-performing NITs in placements. The CSE branch recorded an average package of ₹17.40 LPA and a highest package of ₹64 LPA in recent placement data. Strong placement performance is seen across CSE, ECE, and EEE branches. Students receive offers from both software and core engineering companies. Excellent internship opportunities and PPO (Pre-Placement Offers) are available through industry collaborations. The overall placement rate stands at 85–95%, supported by a strong alumni network and research ecosystem.
