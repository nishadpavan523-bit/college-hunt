About A.N. Sinha Institute of Social Studies

A.N. Sinha Institute of Social Studies (ANSISS) is one of the oldest and most prestigious social science research institutions in India. Inaugurated on 31 January 1958 by President Rajendra Prasad, it was named after Bihar leader Dr. Anugrah Narayan Sinha. The institute was later granted statutory autonomous status through the A.N. Sinha Institute of Social Studies Act, 1964 by the Government of Bihar. Located near Gandhi Maidan, Patna — on the southern bank of the River Ganga — it serves as a major centre for social science research in eastern India. The institute is funded by the Department of Education, Government of Bihar and the Indian Council of Social Science Research (ICSSR). The Ph.D. course is affiliated with Aryabhatta Knowledge University (AKU), Patna.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Check Official Notification
Visit ansiss.res.in for PhD admission notifications. Applications typically open between March–April each year.
Step 2 — Download & Submit Application
Download the application form from the official website. Submit with self-attested documents, ID proof, and academic certificates before the last date.
Step 3 — PhD Admission Test (PAT)
Shortlisted candidates appear for the PAT — a 200-mark written test. Paper I: MCQ on GS, GK, Teaching & Research Aptitude (100 marks). Paper II: Subjective — subject-specific Masters-level paper (100 marks).
Step 4 — Interview
Candidates clearing the PAT cutoff are called for an interview worth 20 marks. Research background and proposal are evaluated by the selection panel.
Step 5 — Merit List & Enrolment
Final merit is calculated using PG percentage + PAT score (5 pts) + interview marks (20 marks). Document verification and fee payment completes enrolment.

PhD Admission Test (PAT) — Minimum Qualifying Marks

The PAT is 200 marks total (Paper I: 100 + Paper II: 100). Candidates must score at least the following to qualify:

Category Min. Qualifying % Min. Marks (out of 200) Status
General (UR) 50% 100 / 200 Qualifying
OBC / EBC 45% 90 / 200 Qualifying
SC 45% 90 / 200 Qualifying
ST 45% 90 / 200 Qualifying
⚠️ Note: Clearing the PAT minimum does not guarantee admission. Final selection is merit-based — combining PG percentage, PAT score (5 pts), and interview marks (20 marks). The institute reserves the right to fill or not fill seats.

Academic Benefits

ANSISS provides researchers with access to high-impact social science projects, policy-oriented fieldwork, and academic networking with scholars from Indian and international institutions. The institute's affiliation with ICSSR opens doors to fellowship funding, collaborative research grants, and national-level recognition. Scholars also benefit from the institute's specialized 3-floor library — visited by former PM Indira Gandhi during its Silver Jubilee in 1984 — and decades of archival social science research data.

🏙️ Location Advantage

Located near Gandhi Maidan in the heart of Patna, Bihar — on the southern bank of the River Ganga — the institute enjoys proximity to Bihar government offices, the Patna High Court, and key administrative institutions, making it ideal for policy-oriented social science research. The campus holds special historical significance as Mahatma Gandhi stayed here in 1947, a heritage the institute proudly preserves.
