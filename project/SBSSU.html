About SBSSU

Shaheed Bhagat Singh State University (SBSSU) is a Punjab Government University located on Moga Road, Ferozepur. The institution began in 1995 as Shaheed Bhagat Singh College of Engineering & Technology and was upgraded to a full-fledged state university under the Shaheed Bhagat Singh State University Act, 2021. Spread across approximately 98 acres, the university is UGC approved and offers AICTE-approved technical programs. SBSSU focuses on Engineering, Technology, Management, Computer Applications, Sciences, Architecture, Applied Sciences, and Research & Innovation, with a strong emphasis on industry-oriented learning and skill development.

UG Admission Process
Step 1 — Visit the University Admission Portal
Go to the official SBSSU admissions portal at sbssu.ac.in and explore the available programs and eligibility criteria.
Step 2 — Register Online
Create your account and register on the admissions portal with valid personal and academic details.
Step 3 — Fill the Application Form
Complete the application form by selecting your preferred program and providing all required academic information.
Step 4 — Upload Documents & Pay Application Fee
Upload scanned copies of 10th & 12th marksheets, category certificate (if applicable), and other required documents. Pay the application fee online.
Step 5 — Counselling / Merit Process
Participate in university counselling or merit-based selection. For B.Tech, JEE Main scores are considered. For B.Arch, NATA / JEE Paper 2 scores apply.
Step 6 — Document Verification & Seat Confirmation
Attend document verification, deposit the admission fee, and confirm your seat allotment to complete the admission process.
