About KSDSU

Kameshwar Singh Darbhanga Sanskrit University (KSDSU) is one of India's most distinguished Sanskrit universities, established in 1961 and situated in Darbhanga, Bihar — a region historically celebrated as a great seat of Sanskrit learning and the Maithili cultural tradition. Named after Maharajadhiraja Kameshwar Singh of Darbhanga, a renowned patron of Sanskrit scholarship, the university is dedicated to the advancement of classical Sanskrit studies, Indology, Vedic sciences, Darshana (Philosophy), Jyotisha (Astronomy & Astrology), Vyakarana (Grammar), Sahitya (Literature), and allied disciplines. It is NAAC Accredited and recognized by the UGC. The university functions as an affiliating university for numerous Sanskrit colleges across Bihar and serves as a vital custodian of India's ancient intellectual heritage, offering programs from Shastri (UG) to Acharya (PG) and Vidyavaridhi (PhD) levels.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply via KSDSU Portal
Register on the KSDSU official admission portal, fill in the application form with required academic details, and upload necessary documents.
Step 2 — Merit List / Entrance Result
For most Shastri and Acharya programs, admission is merit-based. B.Ed. and research programs require a university entrance test or valid UGC-NET score.
Step 3 — Counselling
Attend the university's counselling session as per schedule. Seat allotment is done on the basis of merit, chosen subject specialisation, and category.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: Madhyama / 10+2 / Shastri marksheets, Acharya certificate (for PhD), category certificate, ID proof, and passport-size photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the annual or semester fee and complete enrolment formalities at the respective department or affiliated Sanskrit college.

Placement Notes

As a specialized Sanskrit university, KSDSU's placement outcomes are strongly oriented toward the Education, Civil Services, Research, and Cultural sectors. A large proportion of graduates pursue careers as Sanskrit teachers in central and state government schools (KVS, NVS, BPSC), while many enter competitive examinations including UPSC, BPSC, and UGC-NET. The university's career guidance cell assists students with teacher eligibility tests (CTET/STET) and government job preparation. The highest package on record is approximately ₹4–7 LPA for government teaching and administrative positions, with an average of ₹2–3.5 LPA.
