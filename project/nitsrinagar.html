About NIT Srinagar

National Institute of Technology Srinagar (NIT Srinagar), formerly known as Regional Engineering College (REC) Srinagar, is an Institute of National Importance established in 1960 by the Government of India. Situated on a scenic campus of approximately 67 acres near the famous Dal Lake in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, it is recognised by the Ministry of Education, Government of India and is ranked among the top engineering institutions in India under the NIRF framework. The institute offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across Engineering, Sciences, Humanities, and Management disciplines. NIT Srinagar houses dedicated departments with state-of-the-art labs, research facilities, and a vibrant campus life, catering to students from across India through competitive national entrance examinations.

Admission Process (B.Tech)
Step 1 — Qualify JEE Main
Appear for the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE Main) conducted by NTA. Secure a valid JEE Main score; a strong rank significantly improves chances of admission into preferred branches at NIT Srinagar.
Step 2 — Register through JoSAA
Register on the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) portal using your JEE Main Roll Number and Application Number. JoSAA handles centralized seat allotment for all NITs, IITs, and GFTIs across India.
Step 3 — Choice Filling
Fill in your preferred institute-branch combinations carefully in order of preference on the JoSAA portal. Candidates can fill multiple choices; ensure NIT Srinagar branches are listed based on your JEE Main rank and category.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment
JoSAA releases multiple rounds of seat allotment. Provisional allotment is based on JEE Main rank, category, and seat availability. Accept your allotted seat online within the stipulated deadline and pay the seat acceptance fee.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Report to NIT Srinagar for physical verification of original documents: 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates, JEE Main admit card & scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, medical fitness certificate, and migration certificate.
Step 6 — Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed program fees (tuition, hostel, mess, and other charges) as per the NIT Srinagar fee schedule. Complete enrollment formalities, submit all required documents, and confirm your admission for the academic year.

Placement Notes

NIT Srinagar has demonstrated significant improvement in placements in recent years, with peak placement rates exceeding 95%. CSE, IT, and ECE branches consistently lead in both placement rates and salary packages. The institute has a dedicated Training & Placement Cell that supports internship programs and final placements across IT and Core Engineering sectors. The highest package of ₹36.5 LPA reflects growing interest from top-tier technology firms. Students from all branches receive opportunities in both the software industry and public sector undertakings (PSUs).
