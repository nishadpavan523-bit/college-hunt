About Khalsa College Amritsar

Khalsa College Amritsar is one of the oldest and most prestigious educational institutions in North India. Established in 1892 during British India, the college has played a significant role in education, sports, literature, politics, and public service. Affiliated with Guru Nanak Dev University and accredited with NAAC "A++" Grade, the college holds autonomous status and is recognized by the UGC. Spread across a sprawling ~300-acre heritage campus, its iconic Indo-Saracenic architecture is considered one of Punjab's most recognizable educational landmarks. The college is home to multiple faculties covering Science, Arts, Commerce, Agriculture, Computer Applications, Physical Education, and Journalism.

Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Qualify 10+2
Complete 10+2 from a recognized board. Science stream required for B.Sc. programs; any stream acceptable for B.A., B.Com., BCA, BBA, and BJMC.
Step 2 — Apply via Admission Portal
Register on the college admission portal at khalsacollege.edu.in, fill the application form, and upload required documents.
Step 3 — Merit-Based Counselling
Admissions are conducted through merit-based counselling. Seat allotment is based on qualifying marks in 10+2 and course-specific eligibility.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Submit 10th & 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, and passport-size photographs. Complete fee payment to confirm admission.
Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Complete Graduation
Obtain a Bachelor's degree in the relevant discipline from a recognized university with the minimum required percentage.
Step 2 — Apply on the Portal
Fill the online application form on the college portal and pay the registration fee before the deadline.
Step 3 — Graduation Merit Evaluation
PG admissions are merit-based, evaluated on graduation percentage in the concerned subject/discipline.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Enrolment
Submit graduation certificates, category certificate, and ID proof. Complete fee payment and enrolment formalities as directed by the college.

Campus Recruitment

Khalsa College's dedicated Career Guidance & Placement Cell facilitates campus recruitment and internship opportunities across its faculties. The cell provides comprehensive soft skills training, interview preparation, and resume building workshops. The college's legacy and strong alumni network spanning over 130 years across public service, industry, sports, and academia provide students with exceptional professional opportunities throughout Punjab and beyond.
