About RUHS

Rajasthan University of Health Sciences (RUHS) is a State University established in 2005 by the Government of Rajasthan to oversee and regulate medical and health science education across the state. Headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, RUHS is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC), the Medical Council of India (NMC), the Dental Council of India (DCI), the Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), the Indian Nursing Council (INC), and other apex bodies. The university affiliates over 200+ colleges across Rajasthan offering programs in Medicine, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Nursing, Allied Health Sciences, Physiotherapy, and Paramedical courses. Admission to UG medical programs is through NEET UG and PG programs through NEET PG / RUHS CET.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify the Relevant National/State Entrance Exam
Appear for NEET UG (for MBBS/BDS/BAMS/BHMS/BUMS), NEET PG (for MD/MS/Diploma), NEET MDS (for MDS), GPAT (for M.Pharm), or RUHS CET (for Nursing, Pharmacy, Allied Health, MPT, M.Sc Nursing).
Step 2 — Register on the Official RUHS / Counselling Portal
Visit ruhsraj.org or the Rajasthan Medical Counselling portal. Register with your NEET/GPAT/RUHS CET score card, personal details, category certificate, and required documents.
Step 3 — Fill College & Course Preferences
Select and lock your choice of colleges and courses (Government, Aided, or Private colleges affiliated to RUHS) during the online counselling window. Multiple counselling rounds are held.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, NEET/GPAT/CET scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, ID proof, passport photographs, and gap certificate (if applicable).
Step 5 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is based on NEET/GPAT/CET merit rank, category, college availability, and Rajasthan domicile status (for state quota seats). Multiple rounds are held until all seats are filled.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed tuition fee at the allotted college and confirm admission by reporting and completing all enrollment formalities within the stipulated deadline.

Career Notes

RUHS-affiliated colleges produce a large share of Rajasthan's healthcare workforce. Graduates from MBBS, MD/MS, and allied health programs are actively absorbed by government hospitals, ESIC, Railways, and defence health services. The pharmacy stream has strong industry placements with leading national and multinational pharma companies. Nursing and paramedical graduates find placements in both India and abroad, with growing demand from Gulf countries, UK, Canada, and Australia. The placement rate for clinical programs is above 85%.
