About IIIT Delhi

Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi (IIIT Delhi / IIITD) is a premier autonomous institute established in 2008 by the Government of NCT of Delhi. Recognized by the UGC and accredited with NAAC A Grade with NBA-accredited programs, IIITD is ranked among the Top Engineering Institutes in India by NIRF. The institute houses dedicated Schools of Computer Science, Electronics, Computational Biology, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Mathematics and Social Sciences, offering world-class education across its state-of-the-art 25-acre campus in Okhla, New Delhi.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify JEE Main
Appear in JEE Main and secure a valid score. IIIT Delhi admissions are based on JEE Main ranks through the JAC Delhi counselling process.
Step 2 — Register through JAC Delhi
Register on the Joint Admission Counselling (JAC) Delhi portal with your JEE Main roll number and scorecard details.
Step 3 — Fill College & Branch Preferences
Fill in your preferred colleges and branches carefully in order of priority. IIIT Delhi and branch preference can be set during this step.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, JEE Main scorecard, category certificate, ID proof, and passport photographs at the allotted reporting centre.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is published based on JEE Main rank, category, and branch preference. Candidates are notified through the JAC Delhi portal.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the tuition and hostel fee online and complete enrolment formalities at IIIT Delhi to confirm your seat.
