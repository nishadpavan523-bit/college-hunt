About Jawaharlal Nehru University

Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU), established on 22 April 1969 under the JNU Act 1966, is India's premier central public research university. Recognised as an Institution of Eminence (IoE) with UGC Category-I and Graded Autonomy, JNU holds the highest NAAC accreditation in India — A++ with a CGPA of 3.91. Ranked #2 among Universities and #9 Overall (NIRF 2025) and 501–550 globally (QS World 2026), JNU is organised into 14 Schools and 7+ Special Centres across Arts, Social Sciences, Sciences, Engineering and Management. With ~8,847 students — of whom over 4,200 are PhD scholars — JNU has the highest PhD-to-total-student ratio in India. It is also celebrated for its extremely low fee structure (M.A. fees as low as ₹375/semester) and its unique Deprivation Points policy ensuring equitable access for rural and first-generation learners.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear for Entrance Exam
Register for CUET-UG / CUET-PG / GATE / CAT / GAT-B as applicable to your chosen programme. Conducted by NTA / IITs.
Step 2 — Register Separately on JNU Portal
Visit jnuee.jnu.ac.in and register. Application fee: ₹200–1,500 depending on category and programme.
Step 3 — Deprivation Points Applied
JNU's unique policy adds Deprivation Points to CUET-PG scores for candidates from rural areas, economically backward regions, and first-generation learners.
Step 4 — Merit List & Seat Allotment
Merit list published on JNU portal. No interview for PG programmes (except MBA). PhD shortlisted candidates called for Viva Voce by Departmental Research Committee.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay fees online and complete enrolment. Hostel allotment conducted for residential accommodation on the 1,019-acre campus.
