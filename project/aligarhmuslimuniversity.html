Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) is a premier Central University and Institution of National Importance established in 1920. Originally founded as MAO College in 1875 by Sir Syed Ahmad Khan, AMU has grown into one of India's largest residential universities with 13 faculties, 117 departments, and 37,000+ students — including learners from 50+ countries. Accredited with NAAC A+ and ranked #10 among Indian Universities (NIRF 2025).

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register for AMUEEE (B.Tech/B.Arch – May), AMUEE (BA/B.Com/Law), NEET-UG (medical), CUET-UG/PG, GATE (M.Tech), CAT (MBA), or NEET-PG as applicable. AMU portal: amucontrollerexams.com
Step 2 — Fill AMU OEPS Application
Register on the AMU Online Entrance & Processing System (OEPS) at amucontrollerexams.com with your qualifying exam enrolment number. Pay application fee: ₹1,000–₹2,500 depending on course and category.
Step 3 — Appear for Exam / Wait for Score
Sit for AMUEEE / AMUEE conducted by AMU directly. For NEET / CUET / GATE, use NTA / IIT scores. AMU OEPS processing begins after score declaration.
Step 4 — Merit List & Counselling
AMU releases subject-wise merit lists. Candidates are called for centralised counselling on the AMU portal for seat allotment based on rank, category, and preference.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th & 12th certificates, category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS), transfer certificate, migration certificate, and two passport-size photographs at the respective faculty.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester fees online via the AMU portal. Hostel allocation through the concerned Hall of Residence. Collect student ID and complete library/hostel registration.

Fellowships & Scholarships
🏆 UGC-JRF / SRF Fellowship
₹37,000/month (JRF, years 1–2) → ₹42,000/month (SRF, year 3+) + HRA. For PhD scholars qualifying UGC-NET JRF. One of the most competitive national fellowships.
🔬 CSIR-JRF Fellowship (Science)
₹37,000/month + HRA (revised 2025 rates) for science discipline PhD scholars qualifying CSIR-NET JRF. Covers all living expenses during PhD tenure.
🌟 PM Research Fellowship (PMRF)
₹70,000–80,000/month for meritorious PhD scholars from IITs, NITs, and top central universities. One of India's highest PhD stipends.
💡 DST-INSPIRE PhD Fellowship
₹31,000/month + ₹20,000 annual contingency grant from the Department of Science & Technology for science stream PhD scholars.
🕌 Maulana Azad National Fellowship (MANF)
For students from minority communities (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi) pursuing M.Phil/PhD. Covers tuition, maintenance, HRA, and contingency.
📘 National Fellowships for SC / ST Students
NFSC (SC) and NFST (ST) fellowships: ₹31,000/month for PhD scholars from Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. Administered via the Ministry of Social Justice.
🎓 AMU Merit-cum-Means Scholarship
For financially weaker meritorious students at AMU. Covers partial to full tuition fee waiver based on academic performance and family income assessment.
📋 UP State Scholarship
80–90% fee waiver for eligible UP domicile students (SC/ST/OBC/Minority/General EWS) via the National Scholarship Portal (NSP). Applicable across UG, PG, and PhD levels.
🏛️ ICMR-JRF (Medical Research)
₹37,000/month for PhD scholars in biomedical and health science research, funded by the Indian Council of Medical Research.
Note
Scholarship amounts and eligibility criteria are subject to revision by the funding agency. Students should verify current rates at amu.ac.in and the National Scholarship Portal at scholarships.gov.in.
