About Chandigarh University

Chandigarh University (CU) is a premier private university established in 2012, located at Gharuan, Mohali, Punjab. Accredited with NAAC "A+" Grade, CU offers 250+ acre residential campus with state-of-the-art infrastructure. It is recognized by UGC, AICTE, BCI, PCI, NCTE and other statutory bodies. CU has partnerships with 450+ universities across 90+ countries, making it one of India's most globally connected private universities. Programs span Engineering, Management, Law, Pharmacy, Media, Design, Sciences, and Liberal Arts.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply through Official Portal
Apply through the official Chandigarh University admission portal. Upload all required academic and category documents.
Step 2 — Appear in Entrance Exam
Appear in CUCET / JEE Main / CAT / NEET / CLAT or relevant entrance examination as applicable for your chosen program.
Step 3 — Check Merit List & Scholarship
Check merit list and scholarship eligibility. CUCET scorers may receive up to 100% tuition fee waiver.
Step 4 — Counselling & Document Verification
Attend counselling and complete document verification process on campus or online.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Seat Confirmation
Pay admission fees to confirm your seat in the chosen program.
