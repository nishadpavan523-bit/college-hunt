About IISc Bengaluru

The Indian Institute of Science (IISc), established in 1909 in Bengaluru, is India's premier science and engineering research institution. Founded through the philanthropic vision of Jamsetji Nusserwanji Tata and supported by the Government of India, IISc has consistently been ranked as #1 University in India by NIRF since NIRF rankings began. Designated as an Institute of National Importance and an Institute of Eminence (IoE) by the Government of India, IISc is recognized globally with QS World Rankings placing it among the top 200 universities globally. The institute offers programs in Science, Engineering, Design, and Management, spanning over 40 academic departments and centres. IISc's research output, patents, and spinoff startups place it at the very top of Indian academia. With a lush 371-acre green campus in the heart of Bengaluru — India's technology capital — IISc attracts top talent from across India and the globe.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register at IISc Admissions Portal
Visit admissions.iisc.ac.in. Create an account and register for your chosen program — BS Research, M.Tech, M.Des, MBA, M.Sc (Engg.), or PhD. Note that each program has a separate application window. Application fees are non-refundable.
Step 2 — Ensure Valid Entrance Score
Depending on the program: BS Research requires JEE Advanced (through JoSAA counselling) or KVPY scholarship. M.Tech requires a valid GATE score. MBA requires CAT/GMAT. M.Des requires CEED. PhD requires GATE / CSIR-NET / JAM / JEST / DBT JRF or IISc-level written test.
Step 3 — Fill Application & Upload Documents
Complete the online application with academic records, research experience, publications (if any), Statement of Purpose (SoP) for PhD, entrance scorecard, category certificate, domicile proof, ID, and photograph. Verify all details carefully before submission.
Step 4 — Shortlisting & Written Test (PhD/Research Programs)
For PhD and M.Sc (Research) programs, IISc conducts a department-level written test followed by a research interview. M.Tech admissions are largely based on GATE score and may include a direct offer. BS Research seats are offered through JoSAA centralized counselling.
Step 5 — Research Interview (PhD)
Shortlisted PhD candidates attend a research interview panel conducted by the respective department faculty. Candidates must demonstrate subject expertise, research aptitude, and clarity on their area of interest. International-level preparation recommended.
Step 6 — Offer, Document Verification & Enrollment
Selected candidates receive an official offer letter. Submit original documents for physical verification — degrees, marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate, and identity proof. Pay program fees and complete the enrollment process for the academic term (August intake typically).
