About Central University of South Bihar

The Central University of South Bihar (CUSB) is a prestigious central university established by the Government of India under the Central Universities Act, 2009, located in Panchanpur, Gaya, Bihar. Recognized by UGC, Ministry of Education and AIU, and accredited by NAAC, CUSB is a residential university spread across a ~300-acre modern campus. The university offers multidisciplinary education across Sciences, Humanities, Social Sciences, Law, Management, Education, Media, Languages and Vocational Studies. CUSB follows a CUET-based national admissions framework and is known for its research-oriented academics, interdisciplinary learning and affordable Central Government fee structure.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register & Appear for CUET
Register for CUET-UG (UG programmes) or CUET-PG (PG programmes) on the NTA portal. For PhD: apply directly through the CUSB portal.
Step 2 — Register on CUSB Admission Portal
Visit cusb.ac.in and fill the online application form. Submit application fee as prescribed for your category.
Step 3 — Merit List & Seat Allotment
Merit list released on CUSB portal based on CUET scores. No separate interview for UG/PG (except MBA and PhD).
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10+2 / graduation marksheets, category certificate, CUET scorecard, ID proof and photographs at the reporting centre.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allotment
Pay admission fees online. Apply for hostel accommodation. CUSB is a residential campus — accommodation available for all admitted students.
