About CSMU

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University (CSMU), Navi Mumbai is a private university established in 2018 under the Maharashtra Government Act. Located in Panvel, Navi Mumbai, the university offers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma and doctoral programs across engineering, management, law, pharmacy, science, commerce, humanities and allied disciplines. The university is UGC approved and holds approvals from AICTE, PCI, BCI and COA. CSMU features a residential and urban campus with modern infrastructure, Wi-Fi connectivity, air-conditioned classrooms and dedicated research and innovation centres.

Admission Process
📅 Applications Open: Apply online at csmu.ac.in · UG admissions based on merit & entrance · Scholarships available based on academic performance
Step 1 — Register on csmu.ac.in
Visit the official admissions portal at csmu.ac.in. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number. Fill in personal and academic details as required.
Step 2 — Select Program & School
Choose your preferred school and program. UG admissions are merit-based; professional programs (B.Tech, Law, Pharmacy, MBA) may require national-level entrance scores.
Step 3 — Pay Application Fee
Pay the application fee online through the portal. Keep the payment confirmation receipt for future reference.
Step 4 — Merit List / Entrance Result
Merit list is published on csmu.ac.in. For programs requiring entrance exams, check your qualifying exam results. Shortlisted candidates are called for counselling.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Visit the university with original documents: 10+2 / Graduation marksheets, entrance score cards, category certificates, domicile proof and recent photographs.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the programme tuition fee to confirm your admission. Hostel fee is payable separately. Scholarship applications can be submitted at this stage.

Career & Financial Support

CSMU's Training & Placement Cell provides placement assistance to all students. The university maintains strong ties with industry partners for internships, live projects and final placements. Students are encouraged to gain additional certifications and skill development courses to enhance their employability. The university also runs personality enhancement programmes to prepare students for competitive recruitment processes. Placements vary by department and individual student skill sets.
