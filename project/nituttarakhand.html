About NIT Uttarakhand

National Institute of Technology Uttarakhand (NIT Uttarakhand / NIT UK) is an Institute of National Importance established in 2009 by the Government of India under the NIT Act, functioning under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Located at Srinagar (Garhwal), Uttarakhand, the institute operates its permanent campus nestled in the scenic Himalayan foothills of Garhwal. NIT Uttarakhand is one of the 31 NITs established by the Government of India and offers B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc, and Ph.D programs across Engineering, Sciences, and Humanities disciplines, with several programs holding NBA accreditation. The institute has a growing placement record — highest package of ₹17 LPA, a B.Tech average package of ₹8.7 LPA, and CSE average of ₹10.85 LPA. Admission to B.Tech is through JEE Main followed by JoSAA and CSAB Counselling.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify JEE Main
Appear and qualify JEE Main (conducted by NTA). A valid JEE Main score and rank is mandatory for admission to all B.Tech programs at NIT Uttarakhand. For M.Tech programs, qualify GATE and register through CCMT. For M.Sc programs, qualify through CCMN.
Step 2 — Register through JoSAA
Register on the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) portal using your JEE Main application number and password. JoSAA conducts the centralized counselling process for all NITs, IITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. Registration is mandatory to participate in seat allocation.
Step 3 — Choice Filling
Fill your preferred program and institute choices on the JoSAA portal in order of preference. You can fill up to 25,000 choices. Strategic choice filling is important — list NIT Uttarakhand programs such as CSE and ECE based on your rank and category.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment
JoSAA conducts multiple rounds of seat allotment based on your JEE Main rank, category, and submitted choices. Check your seat allotment result on the JoSAA portal after each round and decide whether to accept, float, or slide as per your preference. CSAB conducts additional special rounds for unfilled seats.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Report to the designated Reporting Centre for online document verification. Documents required: JEE Main admit card and scorecard, 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates, category certificate (if applicable), and PwD certificate (if applicable). Verify all documents carefully before reporting.
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed seat acceptance fee and program fees online through the JoSAA/NIT Uttarakhand portal. Tuition fee is ₹62,500 per semester for General/OBC/EWS categories. SC/ST students receive full tuition fee waiver as per Government of India norms.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation
Report to NIT Uttarakhand campus on the designated date for physical reporting and admission confirmation. Complete all enrollment formalities, submit original documents, pay hostel and mess fees, and complete hostel accommodation allotment to confirm your admission.

Placement Notes

NIT Uttarakhand has a dedicated Career Counselling and Placement Cell with growing placement performance year after year. The strongest performance is in CSE, ECE, and EEE branches. For 2024–25, the institute reported a B.Tech average package of ₹8.7 LPA and a highest package of ₹17 LPA, with CSE students achieving an average of ₹10.85 LPA. Students are recruited by IT, consulting, and core engineering companies. The minimum package stands at ₹4 LPA, and internship opportunities are available through industry collaborations.
