About MMHAPU

Maulana Mazharul Haque Arabic and Persian University (MMHAPU) is a state university established by the Government of Bihar in 1998. Named after Maulana Mazharul Haque — a renowned freedom fighter and educationist — the university was created to promote higher education in Arabic, Persian, Urdu and other languages while also offering modern professional, technical, management and vocational education. It is among the few universities in India dedicated specifically to Arabic and Persian higher education. The university has expanded significantly and now offers a wide range of academic and professional programs through affiliated institutions and study centres across Bihar. It is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

Admission Process (UG Programs)
Step 1 — Check Eligibility
Ensure you have passed 10+2 from a recognized board. Subject requirements vary by course (e.g., language background for B.A. programs).
Step 2 — Visit the University Admission Portal
Navigate to mmhapu.ac.in and look for the current admission notification. Fill the application form with required academic details.
Step 3 — Merit-Based Selection
UG admissions are primarily merit-based. A merit list is drawn based on qualifying examination scores and declared on the university portal.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Submit 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, and ID proof. Pay the applicable course fees online or at the university office.
Admission Process (PG & PhD)
Step 1 — Verify Eligibility
PG: Bachelor's in relevant discipline with minimum prescribed marks. PhD: Master's degree in the relevant field.
Step 2 — Apply via University Process
Submit application through the university's admission process. PG admissions follow merit / university process; PhD requires entrance examination.
Step 3 — Entrance Test & Interview (PhD)
Appear for the university research entrance test. UGC-NET / JRF qualified candidates may be exempted as per regulations. Shortlisted candidates proceed to interview.
Step 4 — Enrolment & Fee Payment
Submit original degree certificates, marksheets, category certificate, and ID proof. Complete fee payment and complete the enrolment formalities.

Placement Notes

MMHAPU graduates are uniquely positioned for careers in Arabic, Persian and Urdu language-related fields — including academia, translation, government services and cultural institutions. The university's specialisation in classical languages makes its graduates sought-after in international language services and diplomatic translation. BCA and BBA graduates find placement in IT and business sectors, while MBA and MCA graduates access management and technology roles. The university's location in Patna provides access to state government employment and Bihar's growing educational sector.
