About NITK Surathkal

The National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK), established in 1960 as the Karnataka Regional Engineering College (KREC) in Surathkal, is one of India's premier engineering institutions. Situated on a scenic 295-acre coastal campus along the Arabian Sea near Mangaluru, NITK is consistently ranked among the top 20 engineering institutions in India by NIRF. Designated as an Institute of National Importance, NITK Surathkal is recognized for its strong academic programs, cutting-edge research, and an exceptionally vibrant campus culture. The institute offers programs across Engineering, Technology, Science, and Management, spanning 18 academic departments. NITK is especially known for its Techfest "Engineer" — one of the largest technical festivals in South India — and for producing top talent hired by global tech companies. With its unique beachside campus and rich student culture, NITK Surathkal offers a truly distinctive college experience.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register & Appear in JEE Main / GATE / JAM
For B.Tech: Register and appear in JEE Main conducted by NTA. For M.Tech: Register and appear in GATE. For M.Sc: Register and appear in IIT JAM. For MBA: CAT / CMAT. For MCA: NIMCET. Scores from these national exams are the primary eligibility gateway for NITK admissions.
Step 2 — JoSAA / CSAB Counselling (B.Tech)
B.Tech admissions are conducted through the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) centralized counselling. Register on josaa.nic.in, fill your branch and institute preferences in order, and participate in the seat allocation rounds. CSAB conducts special rounds (NEUT / Supernumerary) for remaining seats after JoSAA.
Step 3 — CCMT Counselling (M.Tech / M.Sc / MCA)
M.Tech admissions use the CCMT (Centralised Counselling for M.Tech/M.Arch/M.Plan/M.Des) portal. Register at ccmt.nic.in using your GATE score. Fill preferences for programs at NITK and other NITs. M.Sc uses JAM counselling; MCA uses NIMCET counselling.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Seat Acceptance
After seat allotment, accept the seat online and pay the seat acceptance fee within the stipulated deadline. Upload scanned documents including 10th/12th marksheets, graduation degree, entrance scorecard, category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS if applicable), and identity proof.
Step 5 — Physical Reporting & Enrollment (PhD)
For PhD programs, shortlisted candidates attend a departmental written test and interview. Selected candidates receive an offer letter and report physically to NITK campus for document verification. Original documents including degree certificates, marksheets, and GATE/NET scorecard are verified by the department.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Registration
Pay the program tuition fee online through the NITK student portal. Complete the academic registration process for the semester. Hostel allotment is done separately through the NITK hostel office. The academic year typically begins in August (Odd Semester).
