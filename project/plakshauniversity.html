About Plaksha University

Plaksha University is a Private University established in 2021 in Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar (Mohali), Punjab. Founded by a collective of over 100 entrepreneurs, business leaders, and academicians, Plaksha was conceived to reimagine technology education in India. Recognized under the Punjab State Private Universities framework and applicable UGC provisions, the university focuses on Technology, Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Robotics, and Entrepreneurship. The university follows an interdisciplinary model combining engineering, design, humanities, and leadership, and maintains academic collaborations with institutions including Cornell University, UC Berkeley, Purdue University, and the University of Pennsylvania.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Online Application Submission
Submit your application online through the official Plaksha University admissions portal. Provide personal details, academic background, achievements, and extracurricular activities.
Step 2 — Academic Profile Evaluation
Your 10+2 academic records, Physics and Mathematics scores, and board results are reviewed. Both Indian and International board students (AIU-recognized) are eligible.
Step 3 — Holistic Assessment
Plaksha evaluates candidates holistically — considering academics, leadership potential, extracurricular achievements, entrepreneurial mindset, and personal statement rather than marks alone.
Step 4 — Personal Interview
Shortlisted applicants are invited for a personal interview with the admissions committee. The interview assesses motivation, problem-solving aptitude, and alignment with Plaksha's interdisciplinary vision.
Step 5 — Admission Offer
Successful candidates receive an official offer of admission from Plaksha University. Merit-based scholarship consideration is automatic and communicated along with the offer.
Step 6 — Fee Payment and Enrolment
Accept the offer, pay the applicable tuition and hostel fees, and complete all enrolment formalities within the stipulated deadline to confirm your seat.

Career Notes

Plaksha University emphasizes industry projects, entrepreneurship, and research exposure as core pillars of career development. Students receive mentorship from industry leaders, entrepreneurs, and global academicians. The university's interdisciplinary curriculum, combined with its global collaborations and startup incubation support, positions graduates for roles in AI, robotics, data science, biotech, and technology management. Career development programs and leadership training are embedded throughout all programs.
