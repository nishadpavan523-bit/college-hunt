About AUM

Amity University Mumbai (AUM) is a Private University established in 2014 under the Government of Maharashtra Act No. 13 of 2014. Located in Panvel, Navi Mumbai, AUM is a part of the prestigious Amity Education Group — one of India's largest private education networks. The university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited by NAAC. AUM offers programs across Engineering, Management, Law, Design, Architecture, Media, Science, Commerce, and Humanities. The university has a strong focus on industry exposure, internships, and placements through its dedicated Corporate Resource Centre (CRC), with recruiters including Amazon, Deloitte, TCS, Infosys, Accenture, HDFC Bank, and more.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply through Amity Admission Portal
Visit the official Amity University Mumbai admissions portal. Register and fill in the application form with your personal details, academic records, and preferred program. Pay the application fee online.
Step 2 — Upload Academic Documents
Submit scanned copies of 10th & 12th marksheets, entrance exam scorecards (JEE Main / CAT / MAT / NATA), category certificate (if applicable), ID proof, and passport-size photographs through the portal.
Step 3 — Entrance Test / Interview (if applicable)
Appear for the relevant entrance exam: JEE Main for B.Tech, CAT/MAT/XAT/CMAT for MBA, NATA for B.Arch, portfolio submission for B.Des, and Amity Written Test + PI for BBA and Law programs.
Step 4 — Merit Evaluation
AUM evaluates candidates based on entrance exam scores, academic performance, interview performance, and category. Shortlisted candidates receive a merit list notification via email/SMS.
Step 5 — Admission Offer Letter
Selected candidates receive an official Admission Offer Letter from AUM. Verify all details in the offer letter, including the course, fees, and reporting date. Accept the offer within the stipulated deadline.
Step 6 — Fee Payment and Enrollment
Pay the prescribed tuition and other fees online or at the university finance office. Complete enrollment formalities by reporting to the campus with all original documents for final verification and induction.

Placement Notes

Amity University Mumbai operates a dedicated Corporate Resource Centre (CRC) that manages end-to-end placement activities. There is a strong focus on internships and industry exposure starting from the first year. The university provides extensive training in aptitude, group discussions, interview skills, and communication. Placement opportunities span IT, Banking, Consulting, Media, and Engineering sectors. AUM's growing recruiter network continues to expand with companies across India and globally, supported by the Amity Group's strong alumni network.
