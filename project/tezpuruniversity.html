About Tezpur University

Tezpur University is a prestigious central university established in 1994, located in Napaam, Tezpur, Assam. Accredited with NAAC "A+", it houses Schools of Sciences, Engineering, Humanities & Social Sciences, Management Sciences, Education and Law. The university offers UG, PG, Integrated, Diploma, Certificate and PhD programs across multiple disciplines. With a sprawling 262-acre green campus, world-class research labs, and strong industry connections, Tezpur University is consistently ranked among the top central universities in India. Its central university status ensures highly subsidized fee structures accessible to all students.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register for CUET-UG / CUET-PG / JEE Main / CAT as applicable. Deadlines typically Feb–Apr.
Step 2 — Appear for Exam
Exams conducted by NTA. Tezpur University also conducts its own entrance test for select programs.
Step 3 — Merit List / Counselling
Check merit lists on the Tezpur University portal. Attend counselling for seat allotment.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester fees online and complete enrolment formalities.
