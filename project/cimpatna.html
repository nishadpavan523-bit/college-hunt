About Chandragupt Institute of Management Patna

Chandragupt Institute of Management Patna (CIMP) is a premier autonomous management institute established in 2008, located in Patna, Bihar. CIMP is AICTE Approved and NBA Accredited, and its PGDM program is recognized as equivalent to MBA by the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). The institute specializes in Business Administration, Entrepreneurship, Analytics, and Research. With a modern urban campus equipped with smart classrooms and world-class management learning facilities, CIMP offers PGDM, PGDM-IEV, FPM, Executive FPM, and various certificate and management development programs.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply via Official CIMP Portal
Submit your application through the official CIMP admission portal with all required academic and personal details.
Step 2 — Submit Entrance Exam Scores
Submit valid CAT / XAT / MAT / CMAT scores. Scores from any recognized national management entrance exam are accepted.
Step 3 — Shortlisting
Candidates are shortlisted based on academic profile, entrance exam score, and work experience (if applicable).
Step 4 — Group Discussion / Personal Interview
Selected candidates appear for GD/PI rounds conducted at the institute. Research programs require a Research Proposal presentation.
Step 5 — Merit List Publication
Final merit list is published on the official website. Candidates are notified via email/SMS.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Confirmation
Pay program fees online via the institute portal and complete enrolment and documentation formalities.

Placement Support

CIMP's placement cell provides comprehensive career support including resume building, aptitude training, and mock interviews. The institute maintains 100% internship support for all enrolled students. Top recruiting sectors include Banking, FMCG, Consulting, IT, and Analytics. The median package stands at ₹6–6.5 LPA with the highest package reaching ₹16–18 LPA.
