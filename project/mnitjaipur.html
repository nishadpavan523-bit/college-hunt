About MNIT Jaipur

Malaviya National Institute of Technology Jaipur (MNIT Jaipur), commonly known as NIT Jaipur, is a premier public technical university located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. Established in 1963 as Malaviya Regional Engineering College, it was granted the status of a National Institute of Technology and declared an Institute of National Importance by the Government of India. Recognised by the Ministry of Education and accredited by NAAC, MNIT Jaipur is consistently ranked among the top NITs in India (NIRF). It offers programs in Engineering, Architecture, Sciences, Management, and Humanities across a sprawling 317-acre campus with world-class infrastructure and strong industry connect.

Admission Process (B.Tech / B.Arch)
Step 1 — Qualify JEE Main
Appear and qualify JEE Main (Paper 1 for B.Tech / Paper 2A for B.Arch) conducted by NTA.
Step 2 — Register through JoSAA
Register on the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) portal using your JEE Main roll number and application number.
Step 3 — Choice Filling
Fill and lock your preferred branches and institutes in order of priority on the JoSAA portal.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment
Seats are allotted across multiple JoSAA rounds based on category rank, filled choices, and seat availability.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit original documents at the allotted NIT: 10th & 12th marksheets, category certificate, JEE admit card, and ID proof.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the semester fee online through JoSAA/institute portal to confirm seat and complete the admission process.
