About CGC University

CGC University, Mohali is part of the Chandigarh Group of Colleges educational ecosystem, located at Jhanjeri, Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar (Mohali), Punjab. The university received its University Status in 2025 and is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), with approvals from AICTE, PCI, and BCI for respective programs. CGC University focuses on industry-oriented education, research, innovation, entrepreneurship, and global collaborations, offering more than 100 programs through multiple schools including Engineering, Management, Computing, Law, Pharmacy, Allied Health Sciences, and Liberal Arts. The university boasts over 300+ global tie-ups across 70+ countries, 50+ smart labs, and a dedicated innovation and incubation ecosystem. Admission is through CGCUET, JEE scores, or merit-based processes depending on the program.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register Online
Visit the official CGC University admission portal at cgcuniversity.in or call the helpline 1800-500-5005. Create an account and begin the application process for your desired program.
Step 2 — Appear for CGCUET (if applicable)
Appear for the CGC University Common Entrance Test (CGCUET) if required for your program. Engineering aspirants may also submit JEE scores. Management and most programs are merit-based.
Step 3 — Submit Academic Documents
Upload and submit required academic documents including 10th & 12th marksheets, entrance score card (if applicable), category certificate, identity proof, and passport photographs.
Step 4 — Counselling / Document Verification
Attend the scheduled counselling session or document verification process either online or at the campus. Verify all submitted documents with originals at this stage.
Step 5 — Receive Admission Offer
Upon successful verification and merit/entrance-based selection, receive your admission offer letter from CGC University for your allocated program and branch.
Step 6 — Pay Fees & Confirm Admission
Pay the prescribed tuition and hostel fees (if applicable) through the online payment portal and complete all enrollment formalities within the stipulated deadline to confirm your seat.
