About Sikkim University

Sikkim University is a Central University established in 2007 by an Act of Parliament (Sikkim University Act, 2006) under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Located in Gangtok, Sikkim, it is a NAAC accredited institution spread across a sprawling campus of approximately 265 acres and is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC). The university is structured into Schools of Humanities, Social Sciences, Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Professional Studies, and Languages. It offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs catering to students across India through national entrance examinations including CUET-UG and CUET-PG. Sikkim University plays a pivotal role in higher education access for the North-East region of India.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear in CUET-UG / CUET-PG
Appear for the Common University Entrance Test (CUET-UG for undergraduate programs, CUET-PG for postgraduate programs) conducted by NTA. Secure a valid CUET score as it is mandatory for admission to all UG and PG programs at Sikkim University.
Step 2 — Register on Sikkim University Admission Portal
Visit the official Sikkim University admissions portal and register using your valid email ID and mobile number. Keep your CUET application number and scorecard details ready before beginning the registration process.
Step 3 — Fill Online Application Form
Complete the online application form carefully, selecting your preferred program and specialization. Ensure all academic details, personal information, and category details are filled accurately as per your official documents.
Step 4 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of all required documents including marksheets, certificates, category certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, passport-size photographs, and CUET scorecard in the prescribed format and size.
Step 5 — Merit List / Counselling
Sikkim University releases merit lists based on CUET scores. Candidates are called for counselling in order of merit. Check the official university website regularly for merit list announcements and counselling schedules.
Step 6 — Document Verification
Report to Sikkim University for physical verification of original documents: 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates, CUET admit card and scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate, and migration certificate.
Step 7 — Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed program fees (tuition, hostel, mess, and other charges) as per the Sikkim University fee schedule. Complete enrollment formalities, submit all required documents, and confirm your admission for the academic year.

Placement Notes

Sikkim University has a dedicated Placement and Career Counselling Cell that conducts pre-placement training, seminars, and workshops regularly. Placements are comparatively stronger in MBA, MCA, and professional programs. A significant number of students pursue higher education, research, and government sector opportunities after graduation. Industry interaction and internship support are actively facilitated. The university's location in North-East India gives graduates a distinctive advantage in regional development and government sector roles.
