About LNMU

Lalit Narayan Mithila University (LNMU) is a State Public University established in 1972, named after Dr. Lalit Narayan Mishra — eminent freedom fighter, statesman and Railway Minister of India. Originally founded at Mohanpur House, Sara Mohanpur village, the university shifted in 1975 to the historic palatial Raj Campus (230 acres) of Maharajadhiraja Kameshwar Singh of Darbhanga — one of the most heritage-rich campuses in Bihar. Recognised by UGC, DEB and AIU, and accredited NAAC B++, LNMU is ranked 1st in Bihar (uniRank) and 92nd in India. It houses 7 Faculties, 21 PG Departments, 43 Constituent + 24 Affiliated Colleges across Darbhanga, Madhubani, Samastipur and Begusarai districts, and conducts the Bihar B.Ed. CET on behalf of the Bihar Government. Motto: Sa Vidya Ya Vimuktaye (That is knowledge which liberates).

Admission Process (2025-26)
📅 Admissions 2025-26 Open: Apply at lnmu.ac.in/admissions · PG: 5 selection lists already released · PhD: deadline was 11 Feb 2025 (next cycle opening soon)
Step 1 — Appear for the Relevant Entrance Exam
UG: Apply on OFSS Bihar (ofssbihar.in) for B.A./B.Sc./B.Com. — merit-based, no exam needed. B.Tech: LNMU CAT. MBBS/BDS: NEET-UG. B.Ed.: Bihar B.Ed. CET (conducted by LNMU). PG: CUET-PG (NTA) for M.A./M.Sc./M.Com.; CAT/CMAT/MAT for MBA; GATE for M.Tech.
Step 2 — Register on the Official LNMU Portal
Visit lnmu.ac.in → Click 'Apply Now' and register with your email and mobile number. Fill the application form with personal details, academic qualifications and course preferences.
Step 3 — Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of marksheets, photo, ID proof, category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD), domicile certificate and entrance score card.
Step 4 — Pay Application Fee
Pay the application fee online via Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking or UPI as notified on the portal. Keep the payment confirmation receipt for all further stages.
Step 5 — Merit List / Selection List
LNMU releases merit lists / selection lists on the portal (up to 5 lists for PG 2025-26). Check and download your selection letter from lnmu.ac.in. PhD shortlisted candidates are called for Interview / Research Proposal Presentation.
Step 6 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Attend document verification at the university or designated centre. Pay admission fees online to confirm enrolment. MBA final selection includes Group Discussion + Personal Interview. PhD selection: PAT Written Test (70%) + Interview (30%).

Placement Notes

LNMU's Placement Cell places approximately ~150 candidates annually. The median salary for UG graduates stands at ₹3.5 LPA and for PG graduates at ₹4 LPA (2023 data). Being a state university with a strong focus on humanities, sciences and professional education, graduates predominantly secure positions in IT companies, banking, government services, educational institutions and civil services. The MBA programme (IIRF 2025: 92nd in Management) and MCA attract IT and management sector recruiters. LNMU's UNICEF collaboration provides exposure to development and social sector organisations. Students also qualify in UPSC, BPSC and other civil service examinations in significant numbers.
