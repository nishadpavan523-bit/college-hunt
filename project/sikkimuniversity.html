About Sikkim University

Sikkim University was established in 2007 under an Act of Parliament. It is a Central University located in Gangtok, Sikkim. The university offers UG, PG, Integrated, Diploma, Certificate and Ph.D. programs in Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Management, Psychology, Computer Applications and Social Sciences.
