About Patliputra University

Patliputra University (PPU), established on 18 March 2018 by the Government of Bihar, is a state public university headquartered at Kankarbagh, Patna. It exercises jurisdiction over all colleges of Patna and Nalanda districts (except those under Patna University). PPU has 25 constituent colleges, 2 Government Girls' Colleges and 3 Minority Colleges, along with 125+ affiliated colleges. The university is recognised by UGC and offers 500+ UG, PG, Diploma, Vocational and PhD programmes. PPU is known for its extremely affordable fee structure — M.A./M.Sc./M.Com. total fee is just ₹5,120 for the entire 2-year programme, and PhD total fee is ~₹5,120. The university follows Government of Bihar reservation norms, including 35% horizontal reservation for women.

Admission Process
Step 1 — UG (BA/BSc/BCom): Apply via OFSS Bihar
Visit ofssbihar.in for traditional UG programmes. No separate entrance exam — merit-based on 10+2 marks. Application fee: ₹300 (General) / ₹100 (Reserved).
Step 1 (Alt) — UG Professional (BBA/BCA/BBM): PPU UGCET
Register and appear for PPU UGCET — online MCQ test, 100 questions, 120 minutes, no negative marking. Apply on ppup.ac.in.
Step 2 — PG (MA/MSc/MCom): PPU SAMARTH Portal
Register on ppup.ac.in. Traditional PG is merit-based on graduation marks. MBA/MCA requires PPU PGCET (online MCQ test). Application fee: ₹500–1,000 General / ₹300–500 SC/ST/PwD.
Step 3 — Merit List & Allotment
Merit / rank list released on ppup.ac.in. Seat allotment to constituent college as per merit and preferences.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Attend document verification at allotted constituent college. Submit originals: 10+2 / graduation marksheets, category certificate, ID proof and photos.
Step 5 — Confirm Admission
Pay fees online via SAMARTH portal to confirm admission. Apply for hostel accommodation (limited availability) at the respective college.
