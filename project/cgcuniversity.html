About CGC University (Jhanjeri)

CGC University (Chandigarh Group of Colleges), Jhanjeri is a premier private state university established in 2012 under the Punjab Private Universities Policy, 2010, located in Jhanjeri, Mohali, Punjab. Accredited with NAAC "A+" Grade, CGC Jhanjeri is one of North India's leading technical universities approved by UGC, AICTE, BCI, PCI and NCTE. The university offers more than 100+ programmes across Engineering, Technology, Management, Law, Pharmacy, Sciences, Agriculture, Education, Design and Allied Health Sciences. With a sprawling 100+ acre campus, state-of-the-art labs, strong industry connections and record placement figures — including a highest package of ₹45 LPA — CGC Jhanjeri is a top destination for students in the Punjab-Chandigarh belt.

Admission Process — B.Tech (JEE Main / CGCET Routes)
🔵 Route A — via JEE Main
Step 1 — Appear for JEE Main
Register at jeemain.nta.nic.in. Appear in Session 1 (Jan) or Session 2 (Apr). Best of two sessions is considered.
Step 2 — Apply on CGC Portal
Visit cgc.ac.in and apply with your JEE Main percentile / score. Eligible for merit-based admission and JEE scholarship.
Step 3 — Merit List & Scholarship Assessment
Based on JEE Main percentile, CGC Jhanjeri offers fee scholarships: 90%ile+ → 100% tuition fee waiver; 75–90%ile → 50% waiver; 50–75%ile → 25% waiver.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Confirmation
Submit 10+2 marksheets, JEE Main scorecard, category certificate, ID proof and pay admission fees to confirm your seat.
🟡 Route B — via CGCET (CGC Common Entrance Test)
Step 1 — Register for CGCET
Register for CGCET (CGC Common Entrance Test) at cgc.ac.in. Conducted online. No negative marking.
Step 2 — Appear for CGCET
MCQ-based test covering subject knowledge relevant to chosen course. Results declared within few days.
Step 3 — Counselling & Seat Allotment
Merit list declared on cgc.ac.in. Multiple rounds of counselling. Choose branch preference during counselling.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee
Physical / online document verification. Pay semester fee to confirm seat. Apply for hostel.

CGC Jhanjeri's Training & Placement Cell (TPC) is one of the most active in Punjab, consistently delivering strong campus placements.
