About BAMU

Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Marathwada University (BAMU), formerly known as Marathwada University, is a State Public University established in 1958 by the Government of Maharashtra. Located in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), BAMU is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC), the Association of Indian Universities (AIU), and the Government of Maharashtra. The university is spread across a sprawling campus of approximately 725 acres and comprises over 50 university departments and 450+ affiliated colleges. BAMU offers a wide range of programs across Arts, Commerce, Science, Management, Computer Applications, Law, and Engineering (through affiliated colleges), serving the educational needs of the Marathwada region of Maharashtra.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register on BAMU Admission Portal
Visit the official BAMU admissions portal at bamu.ac.in. Register with your personal details, academic information, and preferred course. Pay the application fee online through the portal.
Step 2 — Fill Online Application Form
Complete the online application form with accurate academic details, category information (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD), domicile status, and preferred department or affiliated college. Double-check all entries before final submission.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of 10th and 12th marksheets, graduation marksheets (for PG), entrance scorecards (MAH MBA CET / MAH MCA CET / MHT-CET), category certificate, domicile certificate, ID proof, and passport-size photographs.
Step 4 — Merit List / Entrance Examination
For UG Arts, Commerce, and Science programs, admission is merit-based. For B.Tech, candidates must qualify MHT-CET or JEE Main. For MBA and MCA, MAH MBA CET and MAH MCA CET scores are required respectively. Merit lists are published on the BAMU portal.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Shortlisted candidates must report to the university or affiliated college for physical verification of original documents: marksheets, category certificate, domicile proof, gap certificate (if applicable), migration certificate, and transfer certificate.
Step 6 — Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed tuition and other fees online or at the college finance office. Complete enrollment formalities including submission of signed forms, photographs, and other required documents to confirm admission for the academic year.

Placement Notes

BAMU primarily focuses on academics and research, with placement opportunities varying significantly among affiliated colleges. MBA, MCA, and Computer Science students generally receive better placement opportunities with IT and banking companies. The university provides career guidance and placement support through university departments and affiliated college placement cells. Students from B.Tech affiliated colleges have stronger campus placement records. Government sector recruitment through MPSC, UPSC, SSC, and banking examinations remains a major career pathway for a significant proportion of BAMU graduates.
