About Christ University

Christ (Deemed to be University), established in 1969 as Christ College and granted deemed university status in 2008 by the Government of India, is one of India's premier private higher education institutions. Managed by the Carmelites of Mary Immaculate (CMI) — a religious congregation of the Catholic Church — Christ University is known for its commitment to academic excellence, holistic development, and ethical values encapsulated in its motto "Excellence and Service." The university holds a coveted NAAC A++ Grade accreditation and is classified as a Category I Deemed University by UGC. With 6 campuses — in Bengaluru (Central, Kengeri, Yeshwanthpur, BGR), Pune, and Delhi NCR — Christ University is home to over 30,000 students and 1,500+ faculty. It offers programs spanning Arts, Science, Commerce, Engineering, Law, Architecture, Management, Education, Social Work, Media Studies, and more. Christ is particularly renowned for its BBA, BCom, BA, LLB, BCA, MBA, and MCA programs, which consistently attract top students from across India.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Online Application at Christ University Portal
Visit admissions.christuniversity.in and register with your email and mobile number. Select your program, campus, and fill in the online application form. Pay the application fee (₹1,000 – ₹1,500 depending on the program). Applications open typically from November/December for the following academic year.
Step 2 — Appear for Christ University Entrance Test (CUET)
Most UG and PG programs require the Christ University Entrance Test (CUET — not to be confused with NTA CUET), conducted online or at designated centres. The test evaluates verbal reasoning, quantitative aptitude, general awareness, and subject-specific knowledge. Some programs accept national scores (JEE, CLAT, CAT, GATE, etc.) directly.
Step 3 — Group Discussion & Personal Interview (PI)
Shortlisted candidates are called for a Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI) at the respective campus. This is a critical selection stage for management, law, media, and professional programs. Be prepared to discuss current affairs, your SoP, future goals, and subject knowledge. GD-PI results are shared within a week.
Step 4 — Merit List & Offer Letter
Christ University publishes merit lists on its admission portal. Candidates are selected based on a composite score of entrance test marks, GD-PI performance, and academic record. Offer letters are issued in phases (multiple rounds of admission). Check the portal regularly for your name in the list.
Step 5 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Selected candidates must report to the campus for document verification — 10th, 12th, and bachelor's marksheets, transfer certificate, migration certificate, category certificate (if applicable), ID proof, and passport-size photos. Pay the program fees online/offline within the stipulated deadline to confirm enrollment.
Step 6 — Enrollment & Orientation
Upon confirmed admission, attend the orientation program conducted by the university. Collect ID cards, library cards, and complete biometric registration. Orientation covers academic schedules, rules and regulations, spiritual formation activities, and campus life introductions. Academic sessions typically begin in July–August.
