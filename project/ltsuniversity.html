About LTSU

Lamrin Tech Skills University (LTSU) is a Private Skill University established in 2021 under Punjab State Act No. 22 of 2021, located on the Chandigarh–Jalandhar Highway (NH-344A), near Ropar/Rupnagar, Punjab. The university is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is built on the foundation of industry-integrated, skill-based education. LTSU offers programs in Engineering, Computer Applications, Management, Pharmacy, Commerce, and Hospitality. The university has strong industry partnerships with IBM, Larsen & Toubro (L&T), Tata Technologies, and Ansys, ensuring curriculum relevance and placement readiness. Admission to B.Tech is through JEE Main / Merit and PG pharmacy programs through GPAT / Merit.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Fill Online Application Form
Visit the official LTSU website (ltsu.ac.in) and complete the online application form with your personal details, course preference, and academic information.
Step 2 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of required documents including 10th & 12th marksheets, JEE Main / GPAT scorecard (if applicable), category certificate, identity proof, and passport photographs.
Step 3 — Eligibility Verification
The university verifies your eligibility based on qualifying exam scores, academic percentage, and course-specific criteria (PCM for B.Tech, PCB/PCM for B.Pharm, etc.).
Step 4 — Merit / Entrance Score Evaluation
For B.Tech: JEE Main score or university merit criteria is evaluated. For M.Pharm: GPAT score is considered. For other programs: merit is based on qualifying examination marks.
Step 5 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed tuition fee through the university's online payment portal. Fees vary by program and specialization. Hostel fees are payable separately if opting for accommodation.
Step 6 — Admission Confirmation & Hostel Allotment
Receive admission confirmation letter. If hostel accommodation is required, apply for hostel allotment through the university's hostel management portal.

Career Notes

LTSU's industry-first approach ensures graduates are job-ready from day one. The university maintains strong ties with IBM, L&T, Tata Technologies, and Ansys, enabling industry-mentored training, paid internships, and skill certification programs. The university conducts regular campus recruitment drives and provides placement training covering aptitude, technical skills, and interview preparation. The pharma stream offers strong prospects in manufacturing, clinical research, and drug regulatory affairs. Management graduates are placed in marketing, finance, logistics, and operations roles across sectors.
