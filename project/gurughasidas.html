About Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya

Guru Ghasidas Vishwavidyalaya (GGV), formerly known as Guru Ghasidas University, was established in 1983 and is one of the largest and oldest institutions of higher learning in Chhattisgarh. It was elevated to Central University status under the Central Universities Act, 2009 (No. 25 of 2009). Accredited with a prestigious NAAC A++ Grade, the university is approved by UGC, AICTE, and NCTE. Located in Koni, Bilaspur — just 5 km from the city — GGV's sprawling 655-acre residential campus is a fully self-contained hub of academic excellence. The university offers 100+ UG, PG, diploma, certificate and PhD programmes across Engineering, Sciences, Law, Commerce, Arts, Management, and Pharmacy. It is ranked 47th in Pharmacy (NIRF 2025), 22nd in Science category, and has affiliations with AIU and ACU. The university also promotes the Dr. Ambedkar Centre of Excellence and offers distance education through CDOE.

Admission Process (2026)
📅 Admissions 2026 Open: Applications for MBA, MCA & BCA (CDOE mode) are currently open · CUET-UG 2026 ongoing · Apply at ggu.ac.in
Step 1 — Appear for Relevant Entrance Exam
B.Tech: JEE Main (nta.ac.in). MTech: GATE. UG (BSc/BA/BCom/BPharm): CUET-UG. PG (MSc/MA/MBA/MCA): CUET-PG. M.Pharm: GPAT. LLB/LLM: CUET. PhD: VRET (university's own exam).
Step 2 — Register on GGV Admission Portal
Visit ggu.ac.in → Admissions section. Register online with email and mobile. Fill in personal, academic details and preferred course. Upload necessary documents.
Step 3 — Select GGV During National Counselling
For B.Tech: Select GGV as preferred college in JoSAA/JAC counselling. For CUET-based courses: select GGV during NTA/university counselling. Verify cut-off ranks and eligibility before exercising preferences.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Shortlisted candidates visit the campus for document verification. Carry all academic certificates, category certificate (if applicable), and entrance exam scorecard.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Reporting
Pay semester/annual fee online or at the university cashier. Report to the campus on the designated date to complete enrolment and collect ID card. Apply for hostel separately through the university portal.

Placement Notes

GGV has a dedicated placement cell that conducts campus recruitment drives annually. The university achieves a 70–80% placement rate with the 2023 placements reporting a highest median salary of ₹4 LPA. The highest reported package is ₹64 LPA (from top tech recruiters). B.Tech programmes, especially CSE and IT, see the best placement outcomes. The placement cell organises guest lectures, group discussions, aptitude tests, and mock interviews to prepare students. Pharmacy graduates are placed in leading pharma companies and hospitals. Students from management, sciences and humanities pursue government jobs, research, and higher studies.
