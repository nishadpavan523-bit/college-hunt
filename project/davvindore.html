About DAVV, Indore

Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), established in 1964 in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, is named after the revered Maharani Ahilyabai Holkar, the legendary queen of Indore who transformed the city into a cultural and administrative powerhouse. DAVV is one of the largest and most prestigious universities in central India, affiliating over 800+ colleges across the Indore and Ujjain divisions of Madhya Pradesh, serving more than 5 lakh students. Accredited by NAAC with A+ Grade and ranked by NIRF, DAVV's main teaching campus at Takshashila (Khandwa Road, Indore) houses 35+ departments offering programs across Engineering, Science, Commerce, Law, Management, Arts, Social Sciences, Pharmacy, Library Science, and Journalism. DAVV is especially known for its flagship institutes — SGSITS (Engineering), IMS (Management), and the School of Computer Science & IT (SCSIT) — which produce graduates recruited by top national and multinational companies. The university plays a central role in making Indore — India's cleanest city — a growing hub for education, startup culture, and economic development in central India.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear for Relevant Entrance Exam
Engineering (B.E./B.Tech): Appear in JEE Main (national) or MP Pre-Engineering Test (MPET) if conducted. PG Engineering (M.Tech): Appear in GATE. Science/Commerce/Arts UG-PG: Appear in DAVV CET (Common Entrance Test), conducted by DAVV online every year. MBA: CAT / MAT / CMAT / DAVV CET. LLB/LLM: CLAT / Merit. PhD: DAVV Research Entrance Test (RET) or qualify UGC-NET/GATE (exempted from written test).
Step 2 — Register on DAVV / MP DTE Portal
For Engineering programs: register on the MP DTE online portal (dte.mponline.gov.in). For all other programs at DAVV constituent departments: register on the official DAVV admission portal (dauniv.ac.in). Fill all personal, academic, and entrance details and upload required documents within the announced application window.
Step 3 — Fill College / Department Preferences
For Engineering via MP DTE: fill college and branch preferences during online counselling. For DAVV direct programs: fill department preferences in the DAVV online portal. Seat allotment is based on entrance rank / merit, category (General/OBC/SC/ST/EWS), and number of available seats. Multiple counselling rounds are conducted.
Step 4 — Document Verification
After seat allotment, report to the designated college/department for physical document verification. Carry originals of 10th and 12th marksheets, entrance scorecard (JEE/DAVV CET/GATE/CAT etc.), category certificate, domicile certificate (M.P. domicile for state quota), income certificate (if applying for scholarship), and passport-size photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay tuition fees online or at the college as per the seat allotment letter. SC/ST students of M.P. domicile are eligible for full fee reimbursement under state scholarship schemes. After fee payment, complete the enrollment process at the college/department and collect your student ID card and academic schedule.
Step 6 — DAVV Enrollment Number & Exam Registration
Upon enrollment, students receive a unique DAVV enrollment/registration number. This is used for semester exam registration, admit card generation, result access, and degree issuance. Ensure timely registration for each semester's examination on the DAVV exam portal to avoid any academic penalty.
