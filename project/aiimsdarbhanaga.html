AIIMS Darbhanga is NOT yet operational. The foundation stone was laid on 13 November 2024 by PM Narendra Modi. Construction is currently underway with a target of 48 months from Cabinet approval (~2027). Clinical courses are expected to scale by 2027–2028 subject to NMC inspection and approval. No MBBS or B.Sc. Nursing admissions have been announced. Students currently aspiring for AIIMS in Bihar should apply to AIIMS Patna (aiimspatna.edu.in).

Why AIIMS Darbhanga? – Significance

AIIMS Darbhanga is a landmark step for North Bihar and the entire Mithilanchal region. Darbhanga is the cultural and educational capital of Mithilanchal, serving a population of 40+ million in North Bihar. The region historically lacked tertiary healthcare infrastructure, forcing patients to travel to Patna, Delhi or Kolkata for advanced treatment. AIIMS Darbhanga will be the FIRST AIIMS in North Bihar (AIIMS Patna currently serves South Bihar).

Admission Process – MBBS (Once Operational)
🚧 Not Open Yet: MBBS admissions will open only after NMC approval and operationalisation (expected 2027–2028). The process below is based on the standard AIIMS admission pattern.
Step 1 — Qualify NEET-UG (NTA)
Appear in NEET-UG conducted by NTA (usually in May each year). Minimum qualifying score required: General/OBC/EWS: 600–650+; SC/ST: 500–550+ (based on newer AIIMS pattern).
Step 2 — MCC All India Quota (AIQ) Counselling Registration
Register on the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) portal for AIQ counselling. All AIIMS seats (including AIIMS Darbhanga) fall under 100% All India Quota — open to candidates from all states.
Step 3 — Participate in Counselling Rounds
Round 1 → Round 2 → Mop-Up Round → Stray Vacancy Round. Fill in choices as per preference on MCC portal during the choice-filling window.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment & Document Verification
Documents required: NEET scorecard, 10th & 12th marksheets, ID proof, category certificate (if applicable), medical fitness certificate, domicile certificate. Report to AIIMS Darbhanga upon seat allotment.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the extremely subsidised AIIMS fee (expected ₹1,628–₹6,500/year). AIIMS Darbhanga is a Central Govt. Institution of National Importance — among the lowest fees in India for MBBS.
Admission Process – B.Sc. Nursing (Once Operational)
Step 1 — Appear in AIIMS B.Sc. Nursing Entrance Exam
Conducted by AIIMS Delhi on behalf of all AIIMS. Eligibility: 10+2 with PCB + English; min. 60% (General/OBC); 55% (SC/ST/PwBD); Age: 17–25 years.
Step 2 — Centralised Counselling for All AIIMS
Centralised counselling is held for all AIIMS B.Sc. Nursing seats. Participate in rounds and fill choices as per preference.
Step 3 — Reporting & Enrolment
Report with verified documents. Fee: expected ₹1,000–₹3,000/year (fully subsidised as a Central Govt. institution).
