About SIU

Symbiosis International (Deemed University), popularly known as SIU Pune, is a leading private deemed university established in 2002. Recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Ministry of Education, Government of India, SIU holds the prestigious NAAC A++ accreditation — the highest grade awarded by NAAC. The university operates across multiple campuses in Pune, Noida, Nagpur, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Nashik, with constituent institutes in Management, Engineering, Law, Computer Studies, Health Sciences, Media, Economics, Liberal Arts, and Design. Flagship institutes include SIBM Pune, SCMHRD, SIT Pune, and SLS Pune, each renowned in their respective fields.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for the Relevant Entrance Exam
Register for SET (Symbiosis Entrance Test) for BBA/BCA/B.Sc programs, SITEEE / JEE Main for B.Tech, SLAT for Law programs, or SNAP (Symbiosis National Aptitude Test) for MBA programs. Each test has a separate registration window.
Step 2 — Appear for the Entrance Examination
Appear for the respective entrance exam at the designated test centre. SET and SNAP are conducted online. SLAT and SITEEE are also conducted in online mode. Scores are valid for SIU constituent institutes only.
Step 3 — Personal Interview / WAT (where applicable)
Shortlisted candidates are called for a Personal Interview (PI) and/or Written Ability Test (WAT) — mandatory for MBA programs (SIBM, SCMHRD, SIIB). UG programs may require a PI round at the respective institute level.
Step 4 — Merit List Publication
SIU publishes merit lists based on a combination of entrance exam score, PI/WAT performance, academic record, and category. Each constituent institute publishes its own list and cut-off separately.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, entrance exam scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), identity proof, passport photographs, migration certificate, and gap certificate (if applicable).
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed tuition fee online or at the respective institute and confirm your admission within the stipulated deadline. Hostel allocation is done separately after admission confirmation.

Placement Notes

Symbiosis International University is among India's top private university systems. SIBM Pune and SCMHRD Pune are particularly renowned for MBA placements, with placement rates above 95% and average packages ranging from ₹12–₹28 LPA. SIT Pune has strong placements in Computer Science, AI, and Electronics. The university has excellent internship opportunities and PPO conversions with a strong corporate network across consulting, finance, technology, and FMCG sectors. Over 200 companies recruit from SIU campuses annually.
