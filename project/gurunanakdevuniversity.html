About GNDU

Guru Nanak Dev University, established in 1969 at Amritsar, Punjab, is a premier state public university named in honour of the founder of Sikhism. GNDU holds the prestigious NAAC A++ Grade accreditation — the highest possible — and is recognized by UGC, AICTE, BCI, PCI, NCTE and other statutory bodies. Spread across a sprawling ~500 acre campus, it offers programs in Engineering, Sciences, Humanities, Commerce, Management, Law, Pharmacy, Fine Arts and Sports Education. It functions as both a residential and affiliating university, providing UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate and Ph.D. programs across multiple disciplines.

Admission Process
Portal: Apply through the official GNDU Admission Portal at gndu.ac.in. Appear in JEE Main / CMAT / CAT or relevant entrance examination as applicable.
Step 1 — Apply on Official GNDU Admission Portal
Register on the official GNDU admission portal (gndu.ac.in). Fill in the application form with personal and academic details. Pay the application fee online.
Step 2 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Examination
For B.Tech: JEE Main. For MBA: CMAT / CAT / University Entrance. For M.Tech: GATE. For MCA: Entrance / Merit. For Law: Entrance / Counselling. For B.Sc. / B.Com. / B.A.: Merit-based.
Step 3 — Upload Academic & Category Documents
Upload 10+2 / Graduation marksheets, caste certificate (if applicable), sports certificates (for sports quota), and any other relevant documents as specified.
Step 4 — Check Merit List & Counselling Schedule
Monitor the GNDU portal for merit list publication. Check your rank and eligibility status. Download the counselling schedule and slot booking details.
Step 5 — Attend Counselling & Document Verification
Report for counselling at the designated venue on your allotted date. Carry all original documents for verification: 10+2 certificate, ID proof, category certificate, and entrance rank card.
Step 6 — Pay Admission Fees for Seat Confirmation
After document verification and seat allotment, pay the admission fee within the stipulated time to confirm your seat. Choose hostel accommodation if required (separate process).

GNDU graduates are recruited by leading IT, consulting and technology firms. Engineering and Computer Science graduates see the most active placement activity, with top recruiters visiting campus regularly.
