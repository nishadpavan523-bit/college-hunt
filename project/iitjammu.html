About IIT Jammu

Indian Institute of Technology Jammu (IIT Jammu) is an Institute of National Importance established in 2016 by the Government of India under the IIT Act, functioning under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Located at Jagti, Jammu, Jammu & Kashmir, the institute is spread across a sprawling permanent campus of approximately 400 acres. IIT Jammu is a fully residential campus offering B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc, and PhD programs across Engineering, Sciences, Humanities, and Management disciplines. The institute has rapidly emerged as one of the fastest-growing newer IITs in India with a strong placement record — highest package of ₹53 LPA, an overall average of ₹15.5 LPA, and excellent internship opportunities with leading technology companies. Admission to B.Tech is through JEE Advanced and JoSAA Counselling.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify JEE Advanced
Qualify JEE Main (conducted by NTA) and then appear for JEE Advanced (conducted by IITs). A valid JEE Advanced rank is mandatory for admission to all B.Tech programs at IIT Jammu. For M.Tech, qualify GATE; for M.Sc, qualify JAM.
Step 2 — Register through JoSAA
Register on the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) portal using your JEE Advanced roll number and password. JoSAA conducts the centralized counselling process for all IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. Registration is mandatory to participate in seat allocation.
Step 3 — Choice Filling
Fill your preferred program and institute choices on the JoSAA portal in order of preference. You can fill up to 25,000 choices. Strategic choice filling is important — list IIT Jammu programs such as CSE, Mathematics & Computing, and EE based on your rank and category.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment
JoSAA conducts multiple rounds of seat allotment based on your JEE Advanced rank, category, and submitted choices. Check your seat allotment result on the JoSAA portal after each round and decide whether to accept, float, or slide as per your preference.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Report to the designated Reporting Centre for online document verification. Documents required: JEE Advanced admit card and rank card, 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates, category certificate (if applicable), and PwD certificate (if applicable). Verify all documents carefully before reporting.
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed seat acceptance fee and program fees online through the JoSAA/IIT Jammu portal. Tuition fee is ₹1,00,000 per semester for General/OBC/EWS categories. SC/ST students receive a full tuition fee waiver. Income-based fee remission is available for eligible EWS/OBC students.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation
Report to IIT Jammu campus on the designated date for physical reporting and admission confirmation. Complete all enrollment formalities, submit original documents, pay hostel and mess fees, and complete the allotment of hostel accommodation to confirm your admission.

Placement Notes

IIT Jammu has rapidly emerged as one of the fastest-growing newer IITs with consistently improving placement outcomes. The strongest performance is in CSE, Mathematics & Computing, and Electrical Engineering. The institute has excellent internship opportunities with leading technology companies and a growing record of PPO (Pre-Placement Offer) conversions. High recruiter participation spans software, analytics, consulting, and core engineering sectors. The institute's growing research ecosystem and industry collaborations continue to attract a wider range of top-tier recruiters each year.
