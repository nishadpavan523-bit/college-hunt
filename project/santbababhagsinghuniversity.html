About SBBSU

Sant Baba Bhag Singh University (SBBSU) is a NAAC Accredited Private University established in 2015, recognized by the UGC under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956. Located at Village Khiala, P.O. Padhiana, Jalandhar, Punjab, SBBSU offers a rural residential campus with academic, hostel, sports, and research facilities. The university provides a diverse range of programs across Engineering, Agriculture, Sciences, Management, Law, Education, Health Sciences, Commerce, Humanities, Computer Applications, and Physical Education, with approvals from AICTE, PCI, NCTE, and other statutory bodies as applicable.

General Admission Process
Step 1 — Visit Admission Portal
Visit the official SBBSU admission portal at sbbsuniversity.ac.in to explore available programs and begin your application.
Step 2 — Fill Online Application Form
Complete the online application form with accurate personal, academic, and contact details.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of marksheets, certificates, identity proof, category certificate (if applicable), and passport-size photographs.
Step 4 — Pay Application Fee
Pay the application fee online through the portal. Keep the payment receipt for future reference.
Step 5 — Merit List / Selection Process
Admission is granted based on the merit list published by the university. For some programs, an entrance test or university-specific selection process applies.
Step 6 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Report for document verification at the university. Pay the semester/annual fee to confirm your admission.
