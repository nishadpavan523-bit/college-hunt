About Andhra University

Andhra University (AU Visakhapatnam) is one of the oldest and most prestigious universities in South India, established in 1926 and recognized by the UGC, AIU, and Government of Andhra Pradesh. Located in Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, the university spans a lush campus of approximately 422 acres and comprises 5 Constituent Colleges and 300+ Affiliated Colleges. Andhra University holds the prestigious NAAC A++ Grade with a CGPA of 3.74/4.00 and is ranked 23rd in the University Category and 41st Overall in NIRF 2025. The university offers a wide spectrum of programs across Engineering, Sciences, Commerce, Arts, Law, and Management. Engineering placement highlights a highest package of ₹28 LPA, a UG median of ₹9.3 LPA, and an impressive 94% Engineering Placement Rate. Admission to B.Tech is through AP EAPCET followed by counselling.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register and appear for the relevant entrance exam: AP EAPCET for B.Tech / B.Arch, AP LAWCET for Law programs, AP ICET for MBA / MCA, AP PGECET or GATE for M.Tech, and AUCET or Merit for M.Sc and Arts programs. Registration is done through the official AP State Entrance exam portals.
Step 2 — Apply through Andhra University Portal
After qualifying the entrance exam, register and apply through the official Andhra University admissions portal. Fill in your personal, academic, and entrance exam details accurately. Upload required documents including marksheets, certificates, and category certificates if applicable.
Step 3 — Participate in Counselling
Attend the state-level or university-level counselling as applicable. For B.Tech, the AP EAPCET counselling is conducted by APSCHE. Submit your college and branch preferences in order of priority. Choice filling strategy is important to secure admission in the desired program at Andhra University.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Report for document verification at the designated centre. Documents required include: entrance exam scorecard and hall ticket, 10th and 12th marksheets and pass certificates, Transfer Certificate, Conduct Certificate, category certificate (if applicable), income certificate, and Aadhaar card.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is based on your entrance exam rank, category, and submitted preferences during counselling. Multiple rounds of allotment may be conducted. Check your allotment status on the official counselling portal and confirm or exercise slide/float options as per your preference.
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed tuition and other fees online or at the designated payment centre within the stipulated deadline. Fee varies by program: B.Tech total fee ranges from ₹1.5 – ₹2.5 Lakh. SC/ST and other eligible category students can avail fee reimbursement as per Andhra Pradesh state government norms.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation
Report to Andhra University or the respective constituent college on the designated date with all original documents and fee receipts to confirm your admission. Complete enrollment formalities, obtain student ID, and complete hostel registration if opting for on-campus accommodation.

Placement Notes

Andhra University is one of the oldest and most prestigious universities in South India with strong placements in Engineering, Management, IT, and Commerce streams. The university has a dedicated Placement and Training Cell with over 150+ companies visiting campus annually. Engineering placement reports show a 94% placement rate with a UG median package of ₹9.3 LPA and a highest package of ₹28 LPA. PG Median Package stands at ₹10.5 LPA. The university has excellent opportunities in IT, Core Engineering, and Banking sectors with strong industry collaborations and internship programs.
