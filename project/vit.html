About VIT Vellore

Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT Vellore) is a Private Deemed-to-be University established in 1984, recognized by UGC, AICTE, and the Government of India. Located in Vellore, Tamil Nadu, the institute spans a sprawling campus of approximately 372 acres, offering world-class infrastructure and academic resources. VIT Vellore holds a prestigious NAAC A++ accreditation and consistently ranks among India's top engineering institutions in the NIRF rankings. With academic schools spanning Engineering, Science, Management, Law, and Design, VIT offers programs including B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc, MBA, MCA, and Ph.D. The institute has an exceptional placement record — highest package of ₹1 Crore+, an average package of ₹9.23 LPA, and over 10,000 offers for the 2026 batch from more than 700 companies. Admission to B.Tech is through VITEEE followed by online counselling.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for VITEEE
Register on the official VIT website to apply for VITEEE (VIT Engineering Entrance Examination). Fill in personal, academic, and contact details accurately. VITEEE is conducted by VIT University and is mandatory for admission to all B.Tech programs. For M.Tech, qualify GATE and register through VITMEE. For MBA, appear in CAT / XAT / MAT / CMAT.
Step 2 — Appear in VITEEE Examination
Appear for the VITEEE computer-based test conducted at centres across India and abroad. The exam covers Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics/Biology, English, and Aptitude. VITEEE is known for its moderate difficulty level and serves as the single gateway to all B.Tech programs across VIT campuses.
Step 3 — Check Rank Card
Download your VITEEE rank card from the official VIT website after results are declared. Your VITEEE rank determines your eligibility for specific branches and fee categories during counselling. Lower rank numbers indicate better performance and offer access to premium programs like CSE and its specializations.
Step 4 — Participate in Online Counselling
Register for VIT's online counselling process using your VITEEE rank and application credentials. Counselling is conducted in multiple rounds. Pay the required counselling registration fee to secure your seat in the process. VIT conducts its own independent counselling — it is not part of JoSAA or CSAB.
Step 5 — Branch Selection Based on Rank
Select your preferred program, specialization, and fee category during online counselling based on your VITEEE rank. Top-ranked students get priority in popular programs like CSE, CSE (AI & ML), and ECE. Categories 1 to 5 reflect varying annual tuition fees from ₹1.10 Lakh to ₹4–₹5 Lakh per year.
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed tuition and program fees online through the VIT admission portal after seat allotment. Tuition fees vary by category from ₹1.10 Lakh (Category 1) to ₹4–₹5 Lakh per year (Category 5). Hostel fee of ₹70,000–₹1.5 Lakh per year is payable separately. Security deposit is refundable upon completion of the program.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation
Report to VIT Vellore campus on the designated date for physical reporting and final enrollment. Submit all original documents including mark sheets, entrance results, and category certificates if applicable. Complete hostel registration, medical formalities, and ID card enrollment to confirm your admission to VIT Vellore.

Placement Notes

VIT Vellore has a dedicated Career Development Centre (CDC) and is one of India's best private engineering universities for placements. The strongest performance is in CSE, AI, Data Science, and Electronics branches. For the 2026 batch, VIT crossed 10,000 placement offers with an average package of ₹9.23 LPA and a highest package exceeding ₹1 Crore. More than 700 companies participated in campus placements, with over 3,000 super dream offers made to students. Excellent internship and Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) opportunities are available through industry collaborations across sectors.
