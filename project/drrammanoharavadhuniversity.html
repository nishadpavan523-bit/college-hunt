About RMLAU

Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Avadh University, commonly known as RMLAU or Avadh University, is a prominent State Public University established in 1975 in Ayodhya (Faizabad), Uttar Pradesh. It is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and holds NAAC Accreditation. The university functions as a Residential-cum-Affiliating University, offering programs spanning UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate and PhD levels. It is affiliated with 700+ colleges spread across several districts of Uttar Pradesh, making it one of the largest affiliating universities in the state. RMLAU provides a wide range of programs in Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Education, Technology and Management.

Admission Process
UG Admission: Apply via the RMLAU official portal (rmlau.ac.in) or CUET-UG for eligible programs. For B.Tech: UP JEECUP / JEE counseling. For B.Ed: UP B.Ed JEE.
Step 1 — Apply for CUET-UG / Relevant Entrance Exam
For most UG programs: Register for CUET-UG at cuet.samarth.ac.in (conducted by NTA). For B.Tech: appear in JEE Main / UP JEECUP. For B.Ed: appear in UP B.Ed Joint Entrance Exam.
Step 2 — Appear in Entrance Examination
Appear in the relevant entrance exam. CUET-UG tests language, domain subjects and general aptitude. B.Tech tests Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM). B.Ed tests domain subject knowledge and teaching aptitude.
Step 3 — Check RMLAU Admission Notification
Visit rmlau.ac.in for official admission schedule, seat matrix and merit list. RMLAU releases the merit list / counseling schedule based on CUET scores or university entrance results.
Step 4 — Online Registration on University Portal
Register on RMLAU's admission portal (rmlauexams.in). Fill application form, upload documents: 10+2 marksheet, category certificate, domicile proof, passport photo, ID proof.
Step 5 — Counseling & Seat Allotment
Participate in centralized counseling. Select preferred department/college. Seat allotment is based on merit rank, category and seat availability. Report to the allotted college with original documents.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the prescribed fee online or at the designated bank. Collect enrollment number and student ID. PG and PhD admissions are conducted through separate RMLAU notifications on rmlauexams.in.

Career Pathways

RMLAU graduates pursue diverse career paths across government and private sectors. The most popular routes are UPSC Civil Services, UP PCS (Provincial Civil Services), and UP Police / Judiciary. B.Tech and BCA/MCA graduates are recruited by IT companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro and HCL. Commerce and Management graduates target banking (SBI/PO, RBI, IBPS), CA, and MBA corporate roles. Law graduates pursue judiciary, litigation, and legal advisory. Education graduates often join teaching services via UPTET / CTET.
