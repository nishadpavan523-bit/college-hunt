About Sushant University

Sushant University (formerly Ansal University) is a private university established in 2012 under the Haryana Private Universities Act, 2006, located on Golf Course Road, Sector 55, Gurugram, Haryana. The university was officially renamed from Ansal University to Sushant University in 2020 under the Haryana Private Universities (Amendment) Act, 2020. Recognized by UGC, BCI, COA and PCI, the university houses 8 distinct schools offering programmes in Engineering, Architecture, Design, Management, Law, Hospitality, Health Sciences and Urban Planning. With its motto "Soaring High", SU focuses on professional excellence, industry integration and modern skill development in the heart of the NCR region.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Online Registration
Register on the official admissions portal at sushantuniversity.edu.in. Fill the application form for your desired programme.
Step 2 — Submit Academic Documents
Upload 10+2 / graduation marksheets, entrance exam scorecards, category certificate and ID proof.
Step 3 — Entrance Examination (if applicable)
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech) / NATA (B.Arch) / CLAT or SUAT (Law) / CAT/MAT (MBA) / GATE (M.Tech) as applicable to your programme.
Step 4 — Personal Interview
Shortlisted candidates are called for a personal interview for final selection and scholarship eligibility assessment.
Step 5 — Merit List, Fee Payment & Confirmation
Merit list declared on the portal. Pay fees online to confirm your admission and apply for hostel accommodation.
