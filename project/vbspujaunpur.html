About VBSPU, Jaunpur

Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University (VBSPU), Jaunpur is a prominent state public university established in 1987 under the Uttar Pradesh State Universities Act. Recognized by the UGC and accredited with NAAC A+ Grade, VBSPU is one of the largest affiliating universities in Eastern Uttar Pradesh with around 589 affiliated colleges. The university offers a comprehensive range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across engineering, science, pharmacy, commerce, management, arts, law, and education. Its residential-cum-affiliating campus model provides quality education to students across the region.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register on University Portal
Visit the official VBSPU portal (vbspu.ac.in) and register for the relevant program. Deadlines typically announced May–July each year.
Step 2 — Entrance Exam / Merit Check
UG programs are largely merit-based (12th marks). Professional courses (B.Tech, B.Pharm, MBA, MCA) require entrance examinations. PhD requires PUCRET.
Step 3 — Merit List & Counselling
Merit lists are published on the portal. Candidates must participate in counselling rounds and choose their preferred program/college.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, category certificate, domicile, ID proof, and passport-size photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester/annual fees online via the university portal and complete the enrolment process to confirm your seat.

Placement Notes

VBSPU's training and placement cell facilitates campus recruitment for engineering, pharmacy, and management students. Major IT companies including TCS, Infosys, and Wipro recruit from the university. The placement cell also offers career counseling and training programs to enhance employability. PG and PhD students find opportunities in academia, research institutions, government services, and private enterprises.

Scholarships Available: UP Government Scholarship · SC/ST/OBC · Merit Scholarships · National Scholarship Portal
