About Osmania University

Osmania University (OU) is a State Public University established in 1918, recognised by the UGC, AICTE, and AIU and accredited with a NAAC A+ Grade. Located in Hyderabad, Telangana, the university operates across a sprawling campus of approximately 1,600 acres and is organised into 12 Faculties and 53+ Departments, with a wide network of constituent colleges and 700+ affiliated colleges. Osmania University is ranked 30th in the University category and 7th among State Public Universities in NIRF 2025, and offers programs spanning B.Tech, B.Pharm, B.Sc, B.Com, BBA, BA, BCA, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, M.Sc, MA, M.Com, LLB, LLM, and Ph.D, alongside integrated programs. The university has reported a highest package of ₹45 LPA and an average engineering package of ₹10 LPA. Admission to UG engineering programs is through TG EAPCET, while PG programs use TG ICET, TG PGECET/GATE, and CPGET as applicable.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exams
Appear and qualify TG EAPCET (for B.Tech/B.Pharm), TG ICET (for MBA/MCA), TG PGECET/GATE (for M.Tech), or CPGET (for M.Sc/MA/M.Com/PhD) as applicable to your chosen program. A valid score and rank in the relevant entrance exam is mandatory for admission to Osmania University programs.
Step 2 — Apply through Osmania University Admission Portal
Register on the official Osmania University Admission Portal using your entrance exam hall ticket number and personal details. Online application and document upload is mandatory before counselling begins.
Step 3 — Participate in Counselling
Participate in the centralized counselling process conducted by the relevant admission authority. Fill your preferred branch and college choices in order of preference based on your rank and category.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Report to the designated Help Line Centre / Reporting Centre for document verification. Documents required: entrance exam hall ticket and rank card, 10th/12th/graduation marksheets and certificates, category certificate (if applicable), and income/EWS certificate (if applicable).
Step 5 — Seat Allotment
Counselling authorities conduct multiple phases of seat allotment based on your rank, category, and submitted choices. Check your allotment result after each phase and decide whether to accept, change college, or wait for further phases.
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed seat acceptance fee and program fees online through the admission portal. Tuition fees vary by program — refer to the approximate fee structure for your chosen course. SC/ST students are eligible for fee reimbursement under the Telangana E-PASS scheme.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation
Report to the respective Osmania University college/department on the designated date for physical reporting and admission confirmation. Complete enrollment formalities, submit original documents, and complete hostel accommodation allotment (if applicable) to confirm your admission.

Placement Notes

Osmania University has a dedicated Training & Placement Cell with strong placements in Engineering, MBA, and Computer Applications. For recent years, the university reported a highest package of ₹45 LPA, an average engineering package of ₹10 LPA, a UG median package of ₹11 LPA, and a PG median package of ₹8.5 LPA. Over 200+ companies visited the campus, with excellent internship opportunities and a strong alumni network across government, academia, and industry.
