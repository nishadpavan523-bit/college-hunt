About Galgotias University

Galgotias University is a leading private university established in 2011, located on Yamuna Expressway, Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Recognized by UGC and approved by AICTE, BCI, PCI and other statutory bodies, the university holds a prestigious NAAC "A+" Grade. It offers UG, PG, Diploma, Polytechnic, Certificate and Ph.D. programs across engineering, management, law, media, medical sciences, agriculture and more. Admissions are conducted through national exams including JEE, CUET, NEET, CLAT, CAT and MAT. The university has 20+ schools including School of CSE, School of Artificial Intelligence, School of Business, School of Law, School of Medical & Allied Sciences and more.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register for JEE Main / CUET / NEET / CLAT / CAT / MAT as applicable for your program.
Step 2 — Apply on University Portal
Submit online application via the official Galgotias University admission portal with required details.
Step 3 — Merit List / Counselling
B.Tech via JEE Main merit. Other programs via university merit-based counselling rounds.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester fees online and complete enrolment formalities to confirm admission.
