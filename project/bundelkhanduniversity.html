About Bundelkhand University

Bundelkhand University (BU), established in 1975, is a premier state public university located in Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh, serving the historic Bundelkhand region. Spread across 186 acres of urban residential campus, BU is the academic hub for thousands of students across multiple disciplines including Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Law, Arts, Science, Education, Physical Education and Medical Sciences. The university is NAAC Accredited and recognised by UGC, AICTE, PCI, BCI and NCTE for relevant programs. It operates through multiple Institutes and Faculties — including the Institute of Engineering & Technology, Institute of Management Studies, Institute of Pharmacy, Institute of Agriculture and Faculties of Arts, Science, Law and Education. BU also has numerous affiliated colleges across the Bundelkhand region, making it a significant regional academic centre offering UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate and PhD programmes.

Placement Notes

Bundelkhand University's placement record is strongest in its Engineering, Management and professional programmes. The highest package reported stands at ₹12–₹20 LPA, while the average package for most graduates ranges between ₹3–₹5 LPA. Top recruiting companies include TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL and Tech Mahindra for engineering graduates, while management graduates find roles in BFSI, consulting and IT. Graduates from Arts, Science and Humanities departments pursue careers in government services, academia, research, teaching and civil services. Pharmacy graduates find opportunities in the pharmaceutical industry, hospitals and research. The university's regional importance also opens opportunities in the UP state government sector and PSUs.
