About HNBGU

Hemwati Nandan Bahuguna Garhwal University (HNBGU), named after the legendary Uttarakhand leader, was established in 1973 as a state university and later converted to a Central University. Located in the scenic Garhwal region of Uttarakhand, the university sits amidst the Himalayas and offers a serene academic environment. Accredited with an NAAC 'A' Grade and recognized by UGC, AICTE, and PCI, HNBGU operates across three campuses — Srinagar (Main), Pauri, and Tehri. The university offers over 756 courses across 16 streams, 11 schools, 47 departments, and 2 centres covering Agriculture, Commerce, Earth Science, Arts, Law, Engineering and more. Its School of Engineering and Technology houses flagship B.Tech and M.Tech programmes. The university's Placement & Counselling Cell has been operational since 2006, supporting students with career guidance and campus recruitment.

Admission Process (2026)
📅 Admissions 2026 Open: CUET-UG 2026 exams ongoing until May 31, 2026 · CUET-PG 2026 results released · Counselling schedule to follow · Apply at hnbgu.ac.in
Step 1 — Appear for National Entrance Exam
B.Tech: Register and appear for JEE Main (nta.ac.in). UG courses (BSc/BA/BCom/BPharm): Register for CUET-UG. PG courses (MSc/MA/MBA/MCA): Register for CUET-PG. MTech: Appear for GATE. PhD: Appear for UET or hold UGC-NET/JRF.
Step 2 — Check HNBGU Notification
Visit hnbgu.ac.in and check official admission notification. HNBGU releases its own admission schedule after national exam results. Online and offline application forms available.
Step 3 — Fill Application Form
Fill the HNBGU online application form with personal and academic details. Select campus preference (Srinagar/Pauri/Tehri) and course. Pay the application fee online or at SBI branch.
Step 4 — Merit List & Counselling
HNBGU prepares merit list based on CUET/JEE scores. Shortlisted candidates attend counselling at the respective campus with original documents for verification.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay admission fees online to confirm seat. Enrol at the campus on the reporting date with original documents and hostel application if required.

Placement Notes

HNBGU's Placement & Counselling Cell has been active since 2006 under the School of Engineering and Technology. As per NIRF 2024 data, 67 UG students were placed with a median salary of ₹2,60,000. The median package for UG 4-year programmes was ₹4.5 LPA and for PG 5-year programmes was ₹5.5 LPA in 2024. B.Pharm placement median stood at ₹2.04 LPA. The highest packages reach up to ₹12 LPA in top companies like TCS, Wipro, and Tech Mahindra. Placement rate is approximately 60–70% for technical courses. For non-engineering programmes, most students pursue higher studies, research, or government jobs.
