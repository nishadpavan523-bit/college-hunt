About CRSU

Chaudhary Ranbir Singh University (CRSU), Jind is a state public university established on 4 July 2014 by the Government of Haryana, named after Chaudhary Ranbir Singh, a noted leader and social reformer from Haryana. Formerly known as the Kurukshetra University Post Graduate Regional Centre, CRSU is recognised by UGC and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities. Spread across a 75-acre campus near Gohana Bypass, the university offers full-time UG, PG and doctoral programmes across 13+ teaching departments, with about 155 affiliated institutions spanning 7 districts of Haryana. CRSU is currently preparing for NAAC accreditation and follows National Education Policy 2020 guidelines.

Admission Process — UG & PG
Step 1 — Choose Application Mode
CRSU applicants can submit applications either online (via crsu.ac.in or DHE Haryana portal for UG) or offline at the university, depending on the course.
Step 2 — Fill Application Form & Pay Fee
Fill the application form with academic details for 10th and 12th (or graduation for PG). Pay the application fee (approx. ₹500, varies by course).
Step 3 — Entrance Exam or Merit-Based Selection
Selection is based on the merit list of qualifying examination marks, or entrance test scores where applicable (MDUCEE for MBA, GATE for M.Tech, MAT/CAT, LSAT/CLAT for Law programmes via affiliated colleges, JEE/NATA/CUET as accepted by partner colleges).
Step 4 — Merit List Release
CRSU releases merit lists for PG courses typically in June; doctoral applications are processed in May each year. Check crsu.ac.in regularly for announcements.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Bring original 10th, 12th, and graduation (for PG) marksheets, entrance scorecard (if applicable), category certificate, domicile proof (Haryana), ID proof, and photographs for verification.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the course fee to confirm admission. M.A.: ₹12,005–36,500 total · MBA: ₹58,400 total · M.Sc.: ₹20,980 total · B.P.Ed: ~₹88,000 total. Hostel allotment follows for eligible students.

Placement Notes

CRSU has a dedicated placement cell that organises campus drives, with the university citing good placement records for MBA and technical courses (M.Tech, MCA). The university has tie-ups with various companies for internships and placements, and the admission process is designed considering industry requirements. CRSU also collaborates with national and international institutions for research opportunities. As a young, NEP-2020 aligned university with growing infrastructure (new library, residential complexes under construction), CRSU is positioning itself for stronger industry partnerships. The flagship programmes — MBA, MA and B.Tech — see the highest placement engagement.
