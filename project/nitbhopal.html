About NIT Bhopal (MANIT)

Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT), popularly known as NIT Bhopal, was established in 1960 as the Regional Engineering College (REC) Bhopal. Renamed as NIT in 2002, it is one of the 31 National Institutes of Technology in India — all designated as Institutes of National Importance under the NIT Act 2007. The institute is fully funded and governed by the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Spread across a sprawling 650-acre green campus in the state capital Bhopal, MANIT offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across 14 engineering disciplines, sciences, humanities, and management. With a consistent NIRF ranking in the top 60 institutions of India, NAAC 'A' accreditation, and a growing research output, MANIT is one of the most sought-after NITs for engineering aspirants from Madhya Pradesh and across India.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
For B.Tech: Appear in JEE Main (January & April session). For M.Tech/Ph.D: Appear in GATE. For M.Sc: Appear in IIT JAM. For MCA: Appear in NIMCET. For MBA: Appear in CAT / MAT. For B.Arch: Appear in JEE Main Paper 2A or NATA.
Step 2 — JoSAA Counselling (B.Tech/B.Arch)
B.Tech and B.Arch admissions are fully centralized through JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority). Register on josaa.nic.in after JEE Main results. Fill your college and branch choices in order of preference. MANIT follows Home State (MP) and Other State (OS) quota — MP state students have up to 50% seats reserved under home state quota.
Step 3 — M.Tech / M.Sc / MCA — CCMT Counselling
M.Tech admissions are through CCMT (Centralized Counselling for M.Tech) based on GATE score. M.Sc admissions via JAM counselling. MCA via NIMCET counselling. Register on respective portals, fill preferences, and await allotment. Candidates can report to MANIT after receiving a seat allotment letter.
Step 4 — MBA / PhD — Institute-Level Admission
MBA admissions are conducted directly by MANIT's School of Management. After CAT/MAT shortlisting, candidates attend a GD & PI round on campus. PhD admissions are conducted twice a year through a MANIT-level written test and research interview. Apply at manit.ac.in/admissions.
Step 5 — Document Verification & Reporting
After seat allotment, report to MANIT's admission office with original documents: 10th and 12th marksheets, entrance score card, category certificate (if applicable), transfer certificate, migration certificate, and identity proof. Pay first-semester fees at the time of reporting.
Step 6 — Enrollment & Hostel Allotment
Complete online and offline enrollment formalities. Apply for hostel through the hostel office — hostels are available for all enrolled students. Orientation program and department-level induction sessions are conducted in the first week of the academic semester (typically late July / August).
