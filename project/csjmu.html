About CSJMU

Chhatrapati Shahu Ji Maharaj University (CSJMU), formerly known as Kanpur University, was established in 1966 by the Uttar Pradesh government. Spread across 264 acres in Kanpur, it is one of the largest affiliating universities in UP, with over 700+ affiliated colleges across Kanpur, Unnao, Farrukhabad, Kannauj, Auraiya, and Kanpur Dehat districts. Accredited with NAAC B++ Grade and recognised by the UGC, AICTE, Bar Council of India (BCI), the university offers programs in Science, Arts, Commerce, Law, Education, Engineering, and Management. Admissions are primarily through CUET, UPCET, and merit-based direct admissions for various programs.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify the Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for CUET UG/PG (Central University Entrance Test), JEE Main (B.Tech), NEET UG (B.Pharm/Medical), CLAT (Law), UPBED JEE (B.Ed), CAT/MAT (MBA), GATE/UGC-NET (PhD) as applicable for your course.
Step 2 — Register on CSJMU Admissions Portal
Visit kanpuruniversity.org or the official UP admissions portal. Register using entrance scorecard, personal details, category certificate, and required documents within the advertised window.
Step 3 — Fill Course & Department Preferences
Choose preferred departments (University campus departments or affiliated colleges) during online counselling. Campus departments are most competitive. Multiple counselling rounds are generally held.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th & 12th / graduation marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate (UP domicile for state quota), migration/transfer certificate, and passport photographs.
Step 5 — Merit List & Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is merit-based using entrance rank/marks, category, and preferences. UP domicile candidates compete for state quota seats. All-India quota seats are available for select programs.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay the prescribed tuition fee at the allotted department or affiliated college and report in person within the given deadline for enrollment, document submission, and ID card issuance.

Career Notes

CSJMU is a large affiliating university with placement quality varying significantly between campus departments and affiliated colleges. The MBA, BCA, and science programs at campus departments have better placement support. A significant proportion of graduates pursue government jobs (UPSC/UPPSC/SSC/Bank PO), making competitive exam preparation a core part of career strategy. Engineering and MBA graduates from top affiliated colleges tend to receive the highest packages.
