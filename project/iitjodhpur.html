About IIT Jodhpur

Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur (IITJ) is an Institute of National Importance established in 2008 by the Government of India under the Ministry of Education. Governed by the Institutes of Technology Act, IIT Jodhpur operates as a fully autonomous institution with a sprawling ~852-acre campus. The institute is ranked among the Top IITs and Engineering Institutes in India (NIRF). IIT Jodhpur offers programs through its Schools of Engineering, Science, Artificial Intelligence, Management, Design, Bioscience, and Interdisciplinary Research — with a strong focus on cutting-edge research, innovation, and industry collaboration. Admission to B.Tech programs is through JEE Advanced + JoSAA counselling.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify JEE Advanced / GATE / JAM / CAT / CEED
Appear in the relevant national entrance exam based on your desired program — JEE Advanced for B.Tech, GATE for M.Tech, IIT JAM for M.Sc, CAT for MBA, and CEED for M.Des.
Step 2 — Register through the Respective Admission Portal
Register on JoSAA (for B.Tech/Dual Degree) or the IIT Jodhpur admission portal (iitj.ac.in) for PG programs with your exam score and personal details.
Step 3 — Participate in JoSAA / Counselling
For B.Tech, participate in JoSAA online counselling rounds. Fill choices, lock preferences, and await seat allotment based on JEE Advanced rank and category.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, JEE/GATE/JAM/CAT scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), ID proof, and passport photographs.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is done through JoSAA rounds for UG and by the institute for PG/PhD based on merit, GATE score, interview performance, and category norms.
Step 6 — Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation
Pay the admission fee online through the JoSAA or institute portal and complete all enrollment formalities to confirm your seat at IIT Jodhpur.

Placement Notes

IIT Jodhpur has emerged as one of the fastest-growing IITs in terms of placements, with exceptional results in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Electronics, and Core Engineering. Multiple international offers and PPO opportunities are available every year, with a placement rate of 90%+.
