About Shaheed Bhagat Singh State University

Shaheed Bhagat Singh State University (SBSSU) is a Punjab Government university located on Moga Road, Ferozepur. The institution originated in 1995 as Shaheed Bhagat Singh College of Engineering & Technology and was later elevated to a full State University under the Shaheed Bhagat Singh State University Act, 2021. SBSSU is recognized by the UGC and holds AICTE approval for its technical programs. The university focuses on engineering, technology, management, sciences, architecture, and research with a commitment to industry-oriented learning and innovation across its ~98-acre campus.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Visit University Admission Portal
Go to the official SBSSU admission portal at sbssu.ac.in and register with a valid email and mobile number.
Step 2 — Fill Application Form
Complete the online application form with personal, academic, and category details. Upload required documents.
Step 3 — Pay Application Fee
Pay the application fee online via net banking, UPI, or debit/credit card through the university portal.
Step 4 — Counselling / Merit Process
B.Tech via JEE Main score counselling. B.Arch via NATA/JEE Paper 2. Other programs via merit-based selection by the university.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, passport photographs.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Seat Confirmation
Deposit admission fee online and complete enrolment formalities to confirm seat allotment.

Placement Notes

SBSSU has an active Training & Placement Cell that conducts regular campus recruitment drives. Companies from IT, ITES, core engineering, and management domains visit the campus. The university focuses on skill development, industry-oriented learning, and internship tie-ups. For PG and PhD students, opportunities exist in research institutions, academia, government jobs, and private enterprises. Being a relatively newer state university, placement numbers are growing year over year.
