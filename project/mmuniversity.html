About MM University

Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University), Mullana is a prestigious institution established in 1993, located in Mullana, Ambala, Haryana. Accredited with NAAC "A++" Grade, MM(DU) is one of the most reputed multidisciplinary deemed universities in the region. The university is recognised by UGC, NMC, DCI, PCI, INC, BCI and other statutory councils. With over 130+ programs across UG, PG, and PhD levels, MM University offers professional and academic excellence spanning Medicine, Engineering, Management, Law, Pharmacy, Nursing, Agriculture, and more. The university boasts a residential campus with accommodation for more than 7,000 students and an overall placement rate of approximately 93%.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Visit the Official Admission Portal
Navigate to the official website at mmumullana.org and access the online admission portal to begin your application.
Step 2 — Fill Online Application Form
Complete the online application form with your personal, academic, and programme preference details.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of all necessary documents including marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, and photographs.
Step 4 — Appear for Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), NEET UG (MBBS/BDS), NEET-PG (MD/MS), GATE (M.Tech), CAT/MAT (MBA), or GPAT (M.Pharm) as applicable to your programme.
Step 5 — Attend Counselling / Document Verification
Attend the counselling session or document verification as scheduled. MBBS/BDS admissions follow national/state counselling through MCC.
Step 6 — Pay Admission Fees & Confirm Seat
Pay the admission and semester/annual fees online to confirm your seat and complete enrolment formalities.

Placement Notes

MM(DU)'s dedicated Training & Placement Cell facilitates campus recruitment across its diverse faculties. The university's overall placement rate stands at approximately 93% with the highest reported package around ₹46 LPA. The strong healthcare ecosystem with its own teaching hospital provides direct employment pathways for MBBS, BDS, Nursing, Pharmacy, and Physiotherapy graduates in leading hospitals and healthcare chains. B.Tech and BCA graduates are recruited by top IT companies including TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, HCL, and Tech Mahindra. MBA graduates find opportunities across management consulting, banking, and agribusiness sectors. The university's proximity to the Delhi-NCR and Chandigarh industrial corridors gives students excellent access to top corporate recruiters.
