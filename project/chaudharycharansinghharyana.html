About Chaudhary Charan Singh Haryana Agricultural University

Chaudhary Charan Singh Haryana Agricultural University (CCSHAU) is one of India's premier agricultural universities, established in 1970 and located in Hisar, Haryana. A public-funded State University, CCSHAU holds prestigious NAAC "A+" accreditation and is recognized by ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research). Spread across a massive campus of approximately 7,219 acres at Hisar (8,645 acres including outstation campuses), the university is a hub for agricultural education and research. It houses 5 major colleges covering Agriculture, Agricultural Engineering, Basic Sciences & Humanities, Home/Community Science, and Fisheries Science, along with specialized Agribusiness institutes. CCSHAU is renowned for its research contributions to Indian agriculture, particularly in wheat and rice crop improvement.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register Online
Visit admissions.hau.ac.in and complete online registration. Haryana resident candidates must provide a valid Parivar Pehchan Patra (PPP) / Family ID.
Step 2 — Pay Application Fee
Submit application fee online: General category ₹1,500 | SC/BC/EWS/PWD candidates ₹375.
Step 3 — Appear for CCSHAU CET / Submit National Scores
Appear for CCSHAU Common Entrance Test (CET) or submit CAT/GATE/ICAR AIEEA scores as applicable for the course.
Step 4 — Centralized Counselling & Seat Allotment
Participate in centralized university seat allotment counseling based on merit and entrance scores.
Step 5 — Document Verification & Enrolment
Submit originals: 10th, 12th/graduation marksheets, category certificate, PPP ID, entrance scorecard, and photographs to confirm admission.

Placement Notes

CCSHAU's placement and career services connect graduates with leading agri-businesses, government departments, research organizations, and financial institutions. Agriculture graduates find opportunities in government services (ADA, ADO), agri-input companies, crop protection firms, and NGOs. Engineering graduates are placed in food processing, irrigation, and farm equipment industries. MBA Agribusiness graduates are recruited by leading corporations like ITC, Mahindra Agri, and national banks. Fellowships ranging ₹31,000–37,000+/month are available for PhD scholars under ICAR/UGC/CSIR/DBT.
