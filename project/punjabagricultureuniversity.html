About Punjab Agricultural University

Punjab Agricultural University (PAU), established in 1962, is one of India's foremost agricultural universities located in Ludhiana, Punjab. PAU played a pivotal role in India's Green Revolution, developing high-yield wheat and rice varieties that transformed the nation's food security. Recognized by UGC and ICAR, PAU offers comprehensive programs in agriculture, engineering, sciences, and allied fields. The university is renowned for its research contributions, advanced farming machinery innovation, and the famous Kisan Melas that directly benefit the farming community.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register for CET / MET / JEE Main / ICAR AIEEA as applicable. Application fee: ₹4,200 (CET/MET) or ₹6,300 (CET+BSET).
Step 2 — Appear for Exam / Submit Merit
UG admissions may be merit-based (10+2 marks) or via university entrance test. B.Tech via JEE Main scores. ICAR quota reserved seats for agriculture courses.
Step 3 — Merit List & Counselling
PAU releases merit lists based on entrance exam scores and academic performance. Counselling conducted at university level.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, domicile/residence proof, and passport-size photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester/annual fees via the PAU fee collection portal and complete hostel & enrolment formalities.

Placement Notes

PAU's placement cell connects students with leading agro-industries, seed companies, food processing firms, and government agriculture departments. The average package stands at ₹4.8 LPA, with higher packages reaching ₹7–10 LPA for specialized roles in food technology and agricultural engineering. PG and PhD students frequently secure research positions, government jobs, and academic roles across premier institutions.
