About CDLU Sirsa

Chaudhary Devi Lal University (CDLU), Sirsa was established in 2003 by the Haryana State Legislature Act No. 9 of 2003. Named after the former Deputy Prime Minister of India, Ch. Devi Lal, the university is a state government university offering a wide range of programs across arts, commerce, science, law, management, education, and engineering. CDLU is recognized by UGC, AICTE, BCI, and NCTE and holds NAAC accreditation. The university also operates a Directorate of Distance Education, making quality education accessible to learners across the region.
