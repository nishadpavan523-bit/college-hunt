About SGT University

SGT University, Gurugram is a prestigious private university established in 2013, located in Budhera, Gurugram (Gurgaon), Haryana. Accredited with NAAC "A+" Grade, SGT University is one of the most reputed multidisciplinary universities in the NCR region. Spread across a sprawling 70+ acre modern residential campus, the university houses 18+ distinct faculties covering Medicine & Health Sciences, Engineering & Technology, Dental Sciences, Pharmacy, Nursing, Law, Management, and Agricultural Sciences. With over 200+ programs across UG, PG, Diploma, and PhD levels, SGT is a one-stop destination for students seeking professional and academic excellence.

Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Visit the Admission Portal
Navigate to the centralised online dashboard at admission.sgtuniversity.ac.in and create your applicant account.
Step 2 — Choose Course & Pay Registration Fee
Select your desired course track and submit the registration fee of ₹1,500 online through the portal.
Step 3 — Entrance Exam or National Score Submission
Appear for the university's institutional entrance exam OR submit national-level scores: GATE (for M.Tech) or CAT/MAT (for MBA).
Step 4 — Personal Interview (PI)
Attend the scheduled Personal Interview round conducted over video streaming platforms for final selection.
Step 5 — Document Verification & Enrolment
Submit original marksheets, degree certificates, category certificate, and ID proof. Complete fee payment and enrolment formalities.
Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), NEET UG (MBBS/BDS/BAMS), NCET-SGTU (Nursing), or SGT Entrance Test (BCA/BBA/Law) as applicable.
Step 2 — Apply via Admission Portal
Register at admission.sgtuniversity.ac.in, fill the application form, and pay the ₹1,500 registration fee.
Step 3 — Merit List / Counselling
MBBS/BDS/BAMS via national/state counselling. Other programs via SGT merit list based on entrance scores and qualifying marks.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Submit 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate, ID proof, entrance scorecard, and photographs. Pay semester/annual fees online.

Placement Notes

SGT University's dedicated Training & Placement Cell facilitates campus recruitment across its diverse faculties. The university's strong healthcare ecosystem — with its own teaching hospital and dental college — provides direct employment pathways for MBBS, BDS, Nursing, Pharmacy, and Physiotherapy graduates in leading hospitals, clinics, and healthcare chains. B.Tech and BCA graduates are placed in top IT companies, while MBA graduates find opportunities in management consulting, banking, and agribusiness. The university's NCR location near Gurugram provides students excellent access to corporate recruiters from the Delhi-NCR corridor.
