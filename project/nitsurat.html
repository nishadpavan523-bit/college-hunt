About NIT Surat (SVNIT)

Sardar Vallabhbhai National Institute of Technology (SVNIT), commonly known as NIT Surat, is one of India's premier National Institutes of Technology, established in 1961 as Regional Engineering College Surat and upgraded to NIT status in 2002. Located in Surat, Gujarat, it is accredited with NAAC A+ Grade and consistently ranks within the Top 60 engineering institutes in India (NIRF). SVNIT offers programs across Engineering, Science, and Management. Admission to UG programs is through JEE Main + JoSAA/CSAB counselling, while PG admissions are through GATE / CCMT / IIT JAM. The institute is strategically located in Surat — one of India's fastest-growing industrial and diamond trading cities — providing excellent industry exposure.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify the Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (for B.Tech), GATE (for M.Tech / PhD), IIT JAM (for M.Sc), or CAT / CMAT (for MBA) depending on your chosen program at SVNIT.
Step 2 — Register on JoSAA / CSAB / CCMT Portal
For B.Tech: register on josaa.nic.in or csab.nic.in (CSAB Special Round). For M.Tech: register on ccmt.nic.in. For MBA/M.Sc: directly apply on the SVNIT admissions portal at svnit.ac.in.
Step 3 — Fill Preferences
Lock your college and course preferences on the respective portals. SVNIT is among the first-choice NITs for students from Western India. Multiple rounds (1–6) of seat allotment are conducted.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit: 10th & 12th marksheets, JEE Main scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile, ID proof, migration certificate, PwD certificate (if applicable), and passport photographs.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is based strictly on JEE Main merit rank and category. Home State (Gujarat) quota and All India quota seats are available. CSAB rounds fill remaining vacancies after JoSAA.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Reporting
Pay semester fee online and report to SVNIT campus for physical verification and enrollment. Confirm your seat within the specified deadline to avoid cancellation.

Placement Notes

NIT Surat (SVNIT) shows strong placement performance among NITs, particularly for CSE, ECE, and Electrical branches. The institute's location in Surat — India's 8th largest city and a diamond/textile hub — provides access to a diverse industrial ecosystem not available at most other NITs. Average packages for CSE are notably higher than the institute average. IT and software services companies dominate recruiting, while core engineering companies from Gujarat's industrial belt provide diverse options. MBA students from SVNIT's management department are recruited by BFSI and consulting firms.
