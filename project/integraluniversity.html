About Integral University

Integral University, Lucknow is a premier private university established in 2004, located in Uttar Pradesh. The university is recognized by UGC under Sections 2(f) and 12(B) and is accredited with NAAC A+ Grade. It offers 225+ programs across 11 faculties covering engineering, management, medical sciences, pharmacy, law, architecture, sciences, agriculture, and humanities. With over 15,000 students and a fully residential campus, the university provides a comprehensive academic environment backed by strong approvals from NMC, PCI, BCI, COA, INC, and DSIR recognition as a SIRO. The university also holds NIRF ranking, NABL accredited laboratories, and a NABH accredited hospital.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register for IUET (Integral University Entrance Test) or the relevant national exam (NEET for medical, JEE for B.Tech, CLAT for law). Deadlines typically Feb–May each year.
Step 2 — Appear for Exam
IUET conducted by the university. National entrance tests (NEET, JEE, GATE, CAT, CLAT, NATA) conducted by respective authorities.
Step 3 — Merit List / Counselling
Shortlisting based on entrance score or merit. University conducts its own counselling rounds for IUET-based admissions.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th & 12th marksheets, transfer certificate, category certificate (if applicable), ID proof, and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester/annual fees via the university portal and complete enrolment formalities to confirm admission.

Placement Notes

Integral University's Career Guidance & Development Cell (CGDC) actively facilitates campus placements for graduating students. Major IT and consulting companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, and Deloitte participate in campus drives. Placement outcomes are considered average to moderate depending on the branch. The university conducts regular training programs, internship facilitation, and soft-skill development sessions to improve employability.

💡 Student Tip: Developing additional technical and communication skills alongside academics is strongly recommended for better career opportunities.
