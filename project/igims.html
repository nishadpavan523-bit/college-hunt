About IGIMS Patna

Indira Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences (IGIMS), Patna is one of Bihar's premier government medical institutions, established in 1983. Spread across approximately 130 acres in Patna, it is recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and approved by UGC for medical, nursing, and paramedical education. IGIMS functions as a comprehensive Medical College, Super Speciality Hospital, Nursing College, and Research Centre, offering programs from MBBS and B.Sc. Nursing at the UG level to MD/MS, DM/M.Ch at PG and super-speciality levels, along with PhD research programs. The institute provides state-of-the-art clinical training with a strong focus on healthcare outcomes, research, and community medicine.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register for NEET-UG (MBBS), NEET-PG (MD/MS), or NEET-SS (DM/M.Ch) as applicable. For Nursing and Paramedical programs, check IGIMS admission portal for entrance/merit-based schedules.
Step 2 — Appear for Exam
NEET-UG and NEET-PG are conducted by NTA. Aim for a competitive score as IGIMS seats are limited and in high demand across Bihar and national quota pools.
Step 3 — Participate in Counselling
MBBS: MCC / State Counselling through NEET-UG. MD/MS: MCC / State NEET-PG Counselling. DM/M.Ch: NEET-SS Counselling. Nursing & Paramedical: IGIMS Institute-level counselling.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th & 12th marksheets, NEET scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), valid ID proof, passport-size photographs, and internship completion certificate (for PG).
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay admission and semester fees via the IGIMS admission portal. Complete hostel allotment formalities if required and report to the institute on the designated date.

Career & Placement Highlights

IGIMS Patna ensures strong career prospects for its graduates through comprehensive clinical training and residency programs. 100% of MBBS students are placed for internship as per NMC norms, with stipend as per Bihar Government guidelines. PG residents receive stipends as prescribed. Graduates go on to serve in Government Hospitals, Private Hospitals, Medical Colleges, Research Institutes, and Diagnostic Laboratories across India. The institute provides clinical training support, residency pathways, and active encouragement for higher studies and competitive examinations.
