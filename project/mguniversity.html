About Mahatma Gandhi University

Mahatma Gandhi University (MGU) is a State University established in 1983 by the Government of Kerala through the Mahatma Gandhi University Act. Located on the banks of the Meenachil River in Priyadarsini Hills, Kottayam, Kerala, the university spreads across a lush ~100-acre campus. MGU is one of Kerala's premier universities, accredited with NAAC A+ grade and ranked in the NIRF Top 75 Universities. It affiliates over 160 colleges across Ernakulam, Kottayam, Idukki, and Pathanamthitta districts. MGU offers a wide range of programs in Sciences, Humanities, Social Sciences, Commerce, Management, Law, Engineering, and Technology through its Schools of Learning (SoL) and affiliated colleges. The university is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). It is also known for its strong emphasis on interdisciplinary research, innovation, and community development.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify Entrance / Check Eligibility
Depending on the program, qualify the relevant entrance exam — KEAM for B.Tech and B.Pharm, CAT/KMAT/MAT for MBA, KLEE for LLB, and MGU/university-level PG Entrance for M.Sc / MA / M.Com programs. For most UG Arts, Science, and Commerce programs, admission is on the basis of Class 12 merit rank through the centralized allotment.
Step 2 — Register on MGU CAP / LBS Portal
Register on the MGU Centralised Allotment Process (CAP) portal or LBS Centre portal (for B.Tech/B.Arch). For UG programs, submit Class 12 marks, category certificates, and other required details. For PG programs, register through the respective MGU PG Admission portal after entrance results are announced.
Step 3 — Choice Filling
Fill your preferred colleges and courses in order of preference on the portal. MGU has 160+ affiliated colleges offering diverse programs across Kottayam, Ernakulam, Idukki, and Pathanamthitta districts. List choices strategically based on your rank, category, and preferred location and institution type (Government / Aided / Self-Financing).
Step 4 — Seat Allotment
The allotment is conducted in multiple rounds based on merit rank, category reservation, and choice preferences. Check your allotment result on the portal and accept or opt for re-allotment in subsequent rounds. Provisional allotment does not guarantee final admission until fee payment and document submission.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Report to the allotted college for document verification. Documents typically required: Class 10 and 12 marksheets and certificates, transfer certificate, conduct certificate, nativity/domicile proof, community/caste certificate (if applicable), income certificate (for fee concession), and entrance exam scorecard.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed tuition and other fees online or at the college. Fee structures vary significantly across Government, Government-Aided, and Self-Financing colleges. SC/ST and OBC students may be eligible for fee concessions and scholarships under state and central government schemes. Pay hostel and mess fees separately to confirm accommodation.
Step 7 — Enrollment & Commencement
Complete institutional enrollment formalities, submit original documents, receive ID card, and attend orientation as per the academic calendar announced by MGU. Register for courses as per the Choice Based Credit Semester System (CBCSS) followed by the university.

Placement Notes

MGU's wide affiliate network spans over 160 colleges across four districts, meaning placement performance varies significantly by institution and program. Engineering and Management programs from top-ranked affiliates see the strongest placement outcomes. The IT and software sector dominates campus recruitment; Kerala's IT corridor in Thiruvananthapuram and Kochi provides proximity advantage. Science graduates increasingly pursue competitive exams (GATE, NET, IAS) and postgraduate research, with high success rates. The highest packages of ₹12 LPA are reported from top engineering and MBA-affiliated colleges.
