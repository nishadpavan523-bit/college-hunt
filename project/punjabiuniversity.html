About Punjabi University Patiala

Punjabi University, Patiala is one of the premier institutions of higher education in the Punjab region, established in 1962 with a special mandate to promote the Punjabi language and culture. Recognized by UGC, AICTE, BCI, NCTE and other statutory bodies, and accredited by NAAC, the university spreads across approximately 600 acres in Patiala, Punjab. It offers a comprehensive range of UG, PG, Integrated, Diploma, Certificate and Ph.D. programs in Engineering, Sciences, Arts, Commerce, Computer Applications, Management, Pharmacy and Law. With multiple regional campuses and neighbourhood campuses across Punjab, Punjabi University plays a vital role in making quality higher education accessible across the state.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply on Official Admission Portal
Visit the official Punjabi University admission portal (pupadmissions.ac.in) and complete online registration. Fill in the application form and upload academic and category documents.
Step 2 — Appear for Entrance / Submit Merit
B.Tech via JEE Main. MBA via MET/CAT/CMAT. M.Tech via GATE. B.Ed. and Law programs via university-level entrance. UG/PG general programs via merit-based admission.
Step 3 — Check Merit List & Counselling Schedule
Merit lists and counselling schedules are published on the official portal. Participate in counselling based on entrance scores or merit rank for seat allotment.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Attend verification with original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, degree certificates (for PG), category certificate, and valid ID proof.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Seat Confirmation
Pay admission and semester fees online via the university payment portal to confirm your seat and complete enrolment formalities.

Placement Notes

Punjabi University Patiala's placement cell facilitates campus recruitment drives primarily for Engineering, Management, and professional program students. Companies from IT, ITES, pharma, education technology, and core engineering sectors visit the campus regularly. The highest package recorded is ₹15–20 LPA while the average package stands at ₹4–6 LPA. Placement opportunities are strongest in Engineering (especially CSE and ECE), MBA, MCA, and Pharmacy programs. PG and PhD graduates also find opportunities in academia, research institutions, and government services.
