About BFUHS

Baba Farid University of Health Sciences (BFUHS), established in 1998 at Faridkot, Punjab, is a dedicated State Health Sciences University serving as the apex regulatory and affiliating body for medical, dental, nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy and allied health sciences education across Punjab. The university holds NAAC Accreditation and is recognized by UGC, National Medical Commission (NMC), Dental Council of India (DCI), Indian Nursing Council (INC), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and allied regulatory bodies. BFUHS affiliates government and private medical, dental, nursing and paramedical colleges across Punjab, offering UG, PG, Super-Specialty, Diploma and Ph.D. programs in Medical and Health Sciences.

Admission Process
UG Admission: Apply via NEET-UG for MBBS/BDS. Allied health programs through Merit / Entrance. Register on BFUHS official admission portal: bfuhs.ac.in
Step 1 — Apply through BFUHS Official Admission Portal
Register on the BFUHS official portal (bfuhs.ac.in). Fill in the application form with personal, academic and category details. Upload required documents including mark sheets, certificates and photographs.
Step 2 — Appear in NEET-UG / NEET-PG / NEET-MDS or Relevant Examination
MBBS and BDS candidates must qualify NEET-UG. PG (MD/MS) candidates must qualify NEET-PG. MDS candidates require NEET-MDS score. Allied, Nursing and Pharmacy programs use Merit / Entrance exam. M.Pharm candidates should have GPAT score.
Step 3 — Register for Punjab State Counselling
Based on NEET rank and category, register for Punjab State counselling for medical and dental seats. Allied health and nursing programs follow BFUHS or institution-level counselling. Exercise college and course preferences online.
Step 4 — Upload Academic & Category Documents
Upload 10+2 mark sheet, category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS), domicile certificate, NEET scorecard and passport-size photographs. Ensure all documents are self-attested and legible.
Step 5 — Attend Counselling and Document Verification
Attend counselling session at the designated venue. Original documents are verified at the time of seat allotment. Any discrepancy in documents may lead to cancellation of seat.
Step 6 — Pay Admission Fees for Seat Confirmation
Upon seat allotment, pay the admission fee within the stipulated time to confirm the seat. First-year hostel charges and security deposit are also payable at this stage. Failure to pay within the deadline results in seat cancellation.
