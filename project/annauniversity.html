About Anna University

Anna University is a premier State Technical University in Tamil Nadu, established on September 4, 1978, and named after Dr. C. N. Annadurai, the former Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu. Headquartered in Guindy, Chennai, Anna University consists of multiple constituent colleges — the most prominent being College of Engineering Guindy (CEG), established in 1794, making it one of the oldest engineering colleges in Asia. The university is NAAC accredited A++ and consistently ranks among Top 15 Engineering Institutions in India (NIRF). It is recognized by UGC as a University with Potential for Excellence (UPE). With over 500 affiliated colleges across Tamil Nadu catering to 7.2 lakh+ students, it is one of India's largest affiliating technical universities. Admission to UG programs is through TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions) counselling, while PG programs require TANCET / GATE / CAT / NATA.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify the Relevant Entrance Exam
For B.E./B.Tech: appear for Class 12 (State or CBSE/ISC) — no separate entrance exam, admission is purely merit-based through TNEA. For B.Arch: appear in NATA. For PG: GATE or TANCET PG. For MBA/MCA: TANCET or CAT.
Step 2 — TNEA / TANCET Registration
UG candidates register on the TNEA portal (tneaonline.org) with Class 12 marks. Marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics are normalized to a score out of 200. PG candidates register on the Anna University admissions portal with their GATE/TANCET scorecard.
Step 3 — Fill College & Branch Preferences
During TNEA counselling, candidates fill preferences for colleges and branches across Tamil Nadu including CEG, MIT, ACT campuses of Anna University and its 500+ affiliated colleges. Seat allotment is based purely on normalized marks and category.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit: 10th & 12th marksheets, community certificate (BC/MBC/SC/ST if applicable), transfer certificate, nativity certificate, TNEA/TANCET/GATE scorecard, passport photographs, and aadhar card.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment (Multiple Rounds)
TNEA conducts multiple rounds of allotment. Seats are allotted category-wise (OC, BC, MBC, SC, ST). Candidates must accept/reject allotted seats online within the stipulated deadline.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Reporting
Pay the semester fee online or at the allotted college. Report to Anna University / respective campus for physical document verification and enrollment within the deadline to secure your admission.

Placement Notes

Anna University (CEG campus) is the highest-ranked government engineering college in Tamil Nadu for placements. In 2023, the highest package was ₹40 LPA and the average stood at ₹8.54 LPA. A total of 1,842 students were placed with 59 students receiving packages above ₹10 LPA, primarily from the Department of Information Science and Technology. The placement rate of 70–80% is strong for a government institution with a high total student strength. The university's proximity to Chennai's IT corridor (Tidel Park, OMR) and strong alumni base provide excellent recruitment support.
