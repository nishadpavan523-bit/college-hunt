About Vikram University, Ujjain

Vikram University, established in 1957, is one of Madhya Pradesh's oldest and most prestigious state universities, located in the ancient holy city of Ujjain — one of India's seven sacred cities (Sapta Puri) and home to the world-famous Mahakal Temple. Named after the legendary emperor Vikramaditya — whose court in Ujjain was renowned for learning and the Navaratna scholars — the university embodies a tradition of intellectual excellence deeply rooted in India's ancient heritage. Vikram University affiliates over 200+ colleges across Ujjain and Ratlam divisions of Madhya Pradesh, serving more than 1.5 lakh students. Accredited by NAAC with B++ Grade, the university spreads across a sprawling ~200-acre campus and houses 25+ departments offering programs in Science, Commerce, Arts, Law, Engineering, Management, Computer Science, Pharmacy, and Journalism. The university is particularly renowned for its Jyotish (Astrology) and Sanskrit departments, which are among the finest in India, reflecting Ujjain's historical significance as the prime meridian of ancient Indian astronomy (Ujjayini). The Vikram Kirti Mandir, the Sindhi campus, and the main Freeganj Road campus together create a vibrant academic ecosystem in the spiritual heart of Malwa region.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear for Relevant Entrance Exam
Engineering (B.E./B.Tech via affiliated colleges): JEE Main / MP DTE. PG Engineering (M.Tech): GATE. Science/Commerce/Arts UG-PG: Vikram University CET or merit based on 12th/UG percentage. MBA: CAT / MAT / CMAT / VU CET. LLB/LLM: CLAT / Merit. PhD: Vikram University RET or qualify UGC-NET/GATE (exempted from written test). B.Ed: MP B.Ed CET.
Step 2 — Register on Vikram University / MP DTE Portal
For Engineering programs: register on the MP DTE online portal (dte.mponline.gov.in). For all other programs at Vikram University constituent departments: register on the official Vikram University admission portal (vikramuniv.ac.in). Fill all personal, academic, and entrance details and upload required documents within the announced application window.
Step 3 — Fill Department / College Preferences
Choose department preferences in the VU online admission portal. Seat allotment is based on entrance rank / merit, category (General/OBC/SC/ST/EWS), and available seats. Multiple rounds of counselling are conducted both online and at the university's admission office.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Report to the university/college for physical document verification after seat allotment. Carry originals of 10th and 12th marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate, M.P. domicile certificate (for state quota), income certificate (for scholarship), and passport-size photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay tuition fees online or at the university as per allotment letter. SC/ST students of M.P. domicile are eligible for full fee reimbursement under state scholarship schemes. After fee payment, complete the enrollment process and receive your student ID card and academic timetable.
Step 6 — University Enrollment Number & Exam Registration
Upon enrollment, students receive a Vikram University enrollment/registration number for semester exam registration, admit card generation, result access, and degree issuance. Register for each semester's examination on the VU exam portal promptly to avoid academic penalties.
