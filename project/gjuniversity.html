About GJUST

Guru Jambheshwar University of Science & Technology, established in 1995, is a premier technical and multidisciplinary university in Haryana. Named after Guru Jambheshwar Ji Maharaj, a 15th-century saint and environmentalist, GJUST is recognized by the UGC under Sections 2(f) & 12(B) and holds NAAC A+ Grade (CGPA 3.38). The university operates across a sprawling 372-acre campus and 38 affiliated colleges. GJUST focuses on engineering, technology, pharmacy, environmental studies, management, and sciences. The university offers 40+ academic programs including UG, PG, Integrated, Diploma, and PhD. GJUST holds NIRF rankings in Pharmacy, Management, and University categories, reflecting its commitment to academic excellence and research.

Admission Process
📋 Multiple Pathways: UG admissions through JEE Main, HSTES Counselling (Engineering), Merit-based selection (Commerce/Science). PG admissions via GATE (M.Tech), CAT/MAT (MBA), Entrance exams or Merit. Details on gjusthisar.ac.in
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
For B.Tech: Apply via JEE Main (iitjeemain.nta.ac.in) or register with HSTES for Haryana domicile seats. For other UG courses: Follow merit-based registration on university portal.
Step 2 — Appear in Entrance Examination
Appear in JEE Main, HSTES entrance or merit test based on course. Eligibility criteria vary by program (typically 10+2 with required subjects and minimum marks).
Step 3 — Counselling & Seat Allotment
HSTES conducts centralised counselling for technical programs. Register in counselling portal, choose course and specialization preferences. Seats allotted based on merit and category.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Carry original 12th marksheet, caste/category certificate, domicile proof, admission letter, and entrance exam rank card. Verification done at the respective college/university.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allocation
Pay admission fee as per your category and stream. Hostel accommodation can be availed separately (~₹12,000–20,000 yearly + mess charges ~₹2,500–4,000/month).
Step 6 — PG/PhD Admission (Separate)
PG: GATE (M.Tech), CAT/MAT (MBA), or university entrance exam. PhD: University Entrance Test + Interview. Apply directly on gjusthisar.ac.in. Selection based on academic merit and performance.

Career Pathways

GJUST graduates have strong employment prospects in software development, IT services, pharmaceutical companies, banking, management consulting, and government services. Engineering graduates are highly sought by tech companies. Pharmacy graduates join pharmaceutical industries. MBA graduates pursue roles in consulting, finance and management. Research-oriented PG/PhD graduates often pursue academic careers, research positions in CSIR/ICMR, and entrepreneurship. The university maintains strong industry connections through guest lectures, internship programs, and campus recruitment drives.
