About CSU

Central Sanskrit University traces its roots to the Rashtriya Sanskrit Sansthan, founded in 1970, making it one of India's most prominent institutions for Sanskrit and classical studies. It was accorded the status of a full Central University in 2020 by the Ministry of Education. The university holds NAAC Accreditation and is recognised by both UGC and the Ministry of Education. CSU is dedicated to promoting Sanskrit language, Vedic studies, Indian philosophy, and classical education across India with multiple campus locations. It offers programmes from certificate and diploma levels through to undergraduate, postgraduate, M.Phil., and PhD levels, preserving and advancing classical Indian knowledge traditions.

Admission Process
UG Admission: Apply via CUET (Common University Entrance Test) conducted by NTA for most UG programmes, or through university merit/entrance norms. Official portal: sanskrit.nic.in/admission.php
Step 1 — Apply for CUET / University Entrance
Register for CUET at cuet.samarth.ac.in for UG programmes. For PG, register for CUET-PG. For PhD and Diploma/Certificate courses, apply directly through the CSU admission portal at sanskrit.nic.in/admission.php.
Step 2 — Appear in Entrance Examination
Appear in CUET / CUET-PG as applicable. For UG Shastri/B.A.: 10+2 eligibility required. For B.Ed.: graduation with Sanskrit background preferred. For Integrated Programs: 10+2 with required marks.
Step 3 — Score Card & Merit List
After result declaration, CSU releases merit lists based on CUET scores or university entrance performance. Shortlisted candidates are called for document verification and counselling.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Carry original 10+2 / graduation marksheet, caste certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, CUET score card, and passport-size photographs. Documents are verified at the respective campus.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allocation
Pay the admission fee as per programme and category. Apply for hostel accommodation separately — separate hostels available for boys and girls. Mess and dining facilities available on campus.
Step 6 — PhD Admission (Separate Process)
PhD: CSU conducts its own entrance test followed by a personal interview. UGC-NET JRF qualified candidates may be exempt from entrance test. Apply via the official CSU portal well before deadlines.

Career Pathways

CSU graduates are well-regarded in teaching, research, and government services related to classical and Sanskrit studies. The most popular career paths include school and college teaching (UGC-NET qualified), government roles through UPSC Sanskrit Services, state education departments, and positions in Sanskrit academies, ASI (Archaeological Survey of India), and cultural institutions. PG and PhD graduates primarily pursue research, academic positions, and roles in manuscript preservation and Indological studies.
