About SASTRA Deemed University

SASTRA Deemed University (Shanmugha Arts, Science, Technology & Research Academy) is a prestigious Deemed-to-be University established in 1984, located at Tirumalaisamudram, Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu. Spread across approximately 232 acres, the university holds the NAAC A++ Grade accreditation — the highest grade awarded by NAAC — and is recognized by UGC, AICTE, and IET-UK. SASTRA offers a wide range of programs spanning Engineering, Science, Commerce, Management, Law, and Education through its multiple Schools and Research Centres. Its NIRF 2025 ranking places it at Overall #51, University #29, and Engineering #40. The university has an excellent placement record with a highest package of ₹27 LPA and a B.Tech median package of ₹7.6 LPA. B.Tech admission is primarily through JEE Main score / Class 12 Merit.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register Online
Visit the official SASTRA Admission Portal at sastra.edu and register by creating an account. Fill in personal and contact details accurately. A valid email ID and mobile number are required for OTP verification and all subsequent communication regarding admission status and updates.
Step 2 — Submit Academic Details
Enter your Class 10 and Class 12 (or equivalent) academic details, including board name, roll number, year of passing, subjects studied, and marks obtained. For B.Tech admissions, provide your JEE Main Application Number and Percentile/Score. Upload scanned copies of required documents as specified in the portal.
Step 3 — Merit List / JEE Main Score Evaluation
SASTRA prepares its merit list based on JEE Main scores or Class 12 marks as per the program applied. For B.Tech, candidates with higher JEE Main percentile are given priority. For other programs (B.Sc, BBA, BCA, B.Com), merit is computed based on Class 12 percentage. Shortlisted candidates are notified via email and SMS.
Step 4 — Counselling & Seat Allotment
Eligible candidates are called for counselling (online or offline) where they select their preferred program and branch from available seats. Seats are allotted based on merit rank, category, and seat availability. SASTRA conducts multiple rounds of counselling to fill all available seats across its engineering and non-engineering programs.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Report to SASTRA University campus or the designated reporting center for document verification. Original documents required include: Class 10 and 12 mark sheets and certificates, transfer certificate, conduct certificate, community/caste certificate (if applicable), JEE Main scorecard (for B.Tech), and passport-size photographs. Verify all originals carefully.
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed tuition fee and other charges online through the SASTRA admission portal or at the accounts office. B.Tech tuition fee is approximately ₹70,000–₹85,000 per semester. Additional fees include hostel rent, mess charges, and one-time registration/enrollment fees. Fee receipts should be kept safely for future reference.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation
After successful fee payment and document verification, admission is confirmed. Students receive their enrollment number and are directed to complete hostel accommodation formalities (if applicable). The university sends a formal admission confirmation letter. Students must report on the designated date for orientation and commencement of classes.

Placement Notes

SASTRA is one of the top private engineering universities in South India with consistent placement performance year after year. The strongest performance is in CSE, IT, AI, ECE and core engineering branches. According to recent NIRF and placement reports, 1,621 B.Tech students were placed with a median package of ₹7.6 LPA and a highest package of ₹27 LPA. SASTRA has an excellent industry collaboration network and a strong alumni base in India and abroad that actively supports placements and referrals. Internship opportunities are rated excellent, with major recruiters visiting campus every year.
