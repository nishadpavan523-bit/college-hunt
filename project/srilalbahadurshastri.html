About SLBSNSU

Shri Lal Bahadur Shastri National Sanskrit University, formerly known as Shri Lal Bahadur Shastri Rashtriya Sanskrit Vidyapeetha, is a prestigious Central University established in 1962 and located in New Delhi, India. The university is recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Ministry of Education, Government of India. It holds NAAC Accreditation and stands as one of the foremost institutions in India dedicated to the study, preservation, and promotion of Sanskrit language, literature, and allied Vedic sciences. The university offers programmes from certificate level to doctoral research, covering classical disciplines such as Vedanta, Vyakarana, Nyaya, Jyotisha, and Indian Philosophy.

Admission Process
UG Admission: Apply via CUET (Common University Entrance Test) conducted by NTA for most UG programmes. Official portal: slbsrsv.ac.in
Step 1 — Apply for CUET / University Entrance
UG: Register for CUET (Common University Entrance Test) at cuet.nta.nic.in. PG: Register for CUET-PG. Certificate/Diploma: Apply directly through university portal slbsrsv.ac.in as per university merit norms.
Step 2 — Appear in Entrance Examination
Appear in CUET / CUET-PG as applicable. Subjects include Sanskrit language, relevant humanities subjects, and general aptitude. Ensure eligibility: 10+2 for UG; Bachelor's degree for PG.
Step 3 — Merit List & Counselling
After result declaration, SLBSNSU releases its merit list / cut-off on the official portal. Register for counselling and submit course preferences as per availability.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment & Document Verification
Allotted programme is displayed in the counselling portal. Carry original 10+2 / graduation marksheets, caste certificate (if applicable), CUET scorecard, and other relevant documents for verification.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allocation
Pay the admission fee as per category and programme. Apply for hostel accommodation through the hostel allotment process. Separate hostels available for boys and girls on campus.
Step 6 — PhD Admission (Separate Process)
PhD: SLBSNSU conducts its own PhD entrance test + interview. UGC-NET/JRF qualified candidates may receive preference and/or exemption from written test. Apply on official portal with research proposal.
