About BRABU

BRABU has a rich history dating back to 1952 when Bihar University was established at Patna. It was bifurcated in 1960 and shifted to Muzaffarpur, then renamed Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar Bihar University in 1992 in honour of the architect of the Indian Constitution. Today it serves 5 districts of North Bihar through 77+ colleges (42 constituent + 40+ affiliated) offering 97+ UG, PG, PG Diploma, Certificate and PhD programmes. Recognised by UGC and a member of AIU, the university holds a NAAC Grade B accreditation and was ranked 42nd in the NIRF 2023 University category.

Admission Process (2026-27)
📅 Applications Open: From 10 May 2026 · Last date: 31 July 2026 · Apply at brabu.ac.in · Application fee: ₹600 (General)
Step 1 — Register on brabu.ac.in
Visit the official admissions portal. Register with email and mobile number. Fill the online application form with personal and academic details.
Step 2 — Fill Course Preference
Select preferred course, college and subject combination. BA/BSc/BCom admissions are merit-based on 10+2 marks — no separate entrance exam required.
Step 3 — Pay Application Fee
Pay ₹600 (General/OBC/EWS) or ₹300 (SC/ST/PwD) online. Keep the payment receipt for future reference.
Step 4 — Merit List Release
Merit list released online on brabu.ac.in based on 10+2 marks (for UG) or graduation marks (for PG). Check the list regularly for updates.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Visit the allotted college with original documents for verification. Carry 10+2/Graduation marksheets, caste/category certificates, domicile proof and photographs.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the college tuition fee to confirm your seat. BA/BSc/BCom fees are very affordable at ₹5,000–10,000 per year. B.Ed. and professional course fees differ as per Bihar Govt. norms.

Career Opportunities

BRABU graduates pursue diverse career paths across government services, banking, education and private sector. The university has an active Industry Collaboration Cell for career guidance and campus placement. Top recruiters include Amazon, Flipkart, Mahindra, HDFC and SBI. Many students opt for Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC), UPSC, Teaching & Research, banking examinations and government services. The Distance Education Directorate also allows working professionals to pursue PG programmes simultaneously.
