About IMU

Indian Maritime University (IMU) is a Central University established in 2008 by an Act of Parliament under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways, Government of India. It is the country's only dedicated maritime university, created to consolidate maritime education across India and align it with global standards set by the International Maritime Organization (IMO). IMU is recognized by the UGC and operates five campuses at Chennai, Mumbai Port, Kochi, Kolkata, and Visakhapatnam — all strategically located in India's major port cities. The university offers programmes in Marine Engineering, Nautical Science, Naval Architecture, Port & Shipping Management, Logistics and Maritime Law. Graduates enjoy exceptional career prospects in the global merchant navy, shipping corporations, port authorities and maritime research institutions, often with salaries among the highest in India.

Admission Process
📅 Admissions Open: Register and apply at imu.edu.in/admissions.html · IMU-CET notifications released typically in February–March · Exam conducted in May–June · Counselling in July–August
Step 1 — Register for IMU-CET
Visit imu.edu.in and register for IMU-CET — IMU's own Common Entrance Test conducted for UG, PG and most programmes. For M.Tech: appear for GATE. For MBA: IMU-CET or valid CAT score accepted. Verify medical fitness eligibility before applying for sea-going programmes.
Step 2 — Appear in IMU-CET / GATE / CAT
IMU-CET covers Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and English for UG programmes. For BBA and management courses, the test includes logical reasoning and English proficiency. GATE score required for M.Tech; CAT/IMU-CET for MBA. Exam conducted across multiple cities in India.
Step 3 — Rank Declaration & Result
IMU-CET merit list and rank card released on the official portal. Candidates shortlisted based on IMU-CET rank. MBA candidates additionally called for GD + PI rounds at respective campuses.
Step 4 — Counselling & Campus / Programme Choice
Online counselling conducted on the IMU portal. Candidates select preferred campus (Chennai, Kochi, Kolkata, Mumbai Port, Visakhapatnam) and programme. Seat allotment based on merit rank and preference order. Multiple counselling rounds conducted to fill all seats.
Step 5 — Medical Fitness Examination
Mandatory for all nautical and marine engineering candidates. Medical examination conducted at DGS-approved medical centres. Eyesight (including colour vision), hearing, and general health checked as per ENG1 / ML-6 certificate standards. Candidates failing medical are not eligible for sea-going programmes.
Step 6 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Report to the allotted campus with original documents: IMU-CET scorecard, 10+2 marksheets, medical fitness certificate, caste certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, valid passport (mandatory for sea programmes). Pay admission and tuition fees via IMU portal. Apply for hostel allotment at the campus.

Important Notes for Aspirants
⚕️
Medical & Eyesight Fitness — Mandatory for B.Tech Marine Engineering, B.Sc Nautical Science and Diploma programmes as per ENG1 / ML-6 certificate standards under STCW Convention
📄
Passport & CDC Documentation — Valid Indian passport required before sea cadetship / training voyages. Continuous Discharge Certificate (CDC) to be obtained from DGS before embarking on sea training
🚢
International Sea Voyages — Merchant navy careers involve extended sea voyages (4–9 months), international port calls and life on board ships. Candidates must be mentally and physically prepared for this lifestyle
🛠️
Practical Training is Mandatory — All maritime B.Tech and B.Sc programmes include mandatory sea cadetship / practical training semesters on board vessels arranged by IMU and shipping companies
🔔
Monitor IMU-CET Notifications — Regularly check imu.edu.in and official notifications for IMU-CET exam dates, admit card release and counselling schedule. Registration windows are limited
