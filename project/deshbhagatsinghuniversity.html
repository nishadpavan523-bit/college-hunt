About Desh Bhagat University

Desh Bhagat University (DBU) was established under the Punjab Government's Desh Bhagat University Act and derives its name from freedom fighter Sardar Lal Singh Ji. Located in Mandi Gobindgarh, Fatehgarh Sahib, Punjab, DBU has developed into a multidisciplinary institution with NAAC A+ accreditation recognized by UGC, AICTE, PCI, BCI, INC, COA, AIU and other statutory bodies. The university offers education across nearly 50 streams and hundreds of academic programs spanning Engineering, Management, Health Sciences, Law, Agriculture, Pharmacy, Nursing, Media, Humanities and several other disciplines.

General Admission Process
Step 1 — Visit Official Admission Portal
Go to the official DBU admissions portal and register with your basic details to begin the application process.
Step 2 — Fill Online Application Form
Complete the application form with personal, academic, and program details. Select your preferred course and specialization.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload 10th & 12th marksheets, category certificates, identity proof, passport photo, and any relevant entrance exam scorecards.
Step 4 — Pay Application Fee
Submit the application fee online. Keep the payment receipt for future reference during counselling/admission stages.
Step 5 — Merit / Counselling Process
Participate in the merit-based seat allotment or counselling process as applicable for your chosen program (NEET counselling for BAMS).
Step 6 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Attend document verification at the campus. Pay the semester/annual fee and confirm your admission to secure your seat.
