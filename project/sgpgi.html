About SGPGIMS

Established in 1983 by the Government of Uttar Pradesh, SGPGIMS Lucknow is one of India's top super-speciality medical institutes, recognized nationally for advanced medical education, patient care, research and super-speciality healthcare services. Spread over a vast 550-acre campus on Raebareli Road, Lucknow, the institute is accredited with NAAC A++ Grade and consistently ranks among the top medical institutions in India in NIRF rankings. Recognized by UGC, SGPGIMS offers a comprehensive range of programmes from undergraduate nursing and allied health sciences to postgraduate MD/MS, super-speciality DM/M.Ch, and doctoral PhD programmes.

Admission Process
📅 Applications: Apply at sgpgims.in · Admissions based on NEET-PG / NEET-SS / Entrance Exams · Counselling & document verification mandatory
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
For MD/MS: Qualify NEET-PG. For DM/M.Ch: Qualify NEET-SS. For B.Sc / BPT / Allied Health: Appear in SGPGIMS entrance examination. For PhD: Appear in written examination conducted by SGPGIMS.
Step 2 — Register on sgpgims.in
Visit the official admissions portal. Register with valid email and mobile number. Fill the online application form with personal, academic and entrance exam details.
Step 3 — Merit List / Counselling Round
Merit list released based on entrance exam score. Candidates called for counselling rounds. Choose preferred programme/speciality based on rank and seat availability.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Appear at SGPGIMS campus with all original documents. Carry MBBS/PG degree, internship completion certificate, NEET scorecard, category certificates and photographs.
Step 5 — Interview (PhD only)
PhD candidates who clear the written examination must appear for a personal interview before a departmental selection committee.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the programme fee to confirm seat. MD/MS fees are approx. ₹1–1.5 lakh total. DM/M.Ch approx. ₹1.5–2 lakh. PhD approx. ₹50,000–75,000. Hostel charges approx. ₹3,000/year.

SGPGIMS graduates enjoy excellent career opportunities in medical sciences. The institute provides super-speciality residency programs, research fellowships, and strong pathways into academic and hospital placements. Graduates find opportunities across government and private healthcare organisations, including AIIMS, PGI Chandigarh, and leading private super-speciality hospitals.
