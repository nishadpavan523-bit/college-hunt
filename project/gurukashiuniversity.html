About Guru Kashi University

Guru Kashi University (GKU) is a leading private university established in 2011 under the Guru Kashi University Act, 2011 (Punjab Act No. 37 of 2011) by the Punjab State Legislature. Located in Talwandi Sabo, Bathinda, Punjab, GKU offers multidisciplinary education through various faculties including Engineering, Agriculture, Pharmacy, Law, Management, Sciences, Healthcare, Education and Arts. The university is accredited with NAAC A++ Grade and recognized by UGC and various professional councils including BCI, PCI, NCTE, and ICAR. With over 5,000 students and 140+ programs, GKU is committed to academic excellence and holistic development.

UG / PG Admission Process
Step 1 — Visit Admission Portal
Go to the official GKU admissions portal at gku.ac.in to start your application process.
Step 2 — Fill Application Form
Complete the online application form with accurate personal, academic, and contact details.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload marksheets, certificates, identity proof, and any other required documents as specified.
Step 4 — Merit List Preparation & Seat Allotment
University prepares merit list based on qualifying exam scores. Check seat allotment on the portal.
Step 5 — Fee Payment
Pay the required semester/annual fee online to confirm your allotted seat.
Step 6 — Document Verification & Final Admission
Attend document verification at the campus. Final admission is confirmed after successful verification.
