About University of Kerala

The University of Kerala, established in 1937 as the University of Travancore, is one of the oldest and most prestigious universities in South India. Located in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, it is a State Affiliating University recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited with NAAC A+ grade. The university has over 190 affiliated colleges and offers programs across Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Management, Education, Engineering, and Fine Arts through its campus departments and affiliated institutions. The university's sprawling Kariavattom Campus houses numerous departments, research centres, and modern academic facilities. It is consistently ranked among the top universities in India and has produced distinguished alumni in academia, government, law, and the arts. Admission to undergraduate programs is primarily through the Centralised Allotment Process (CAP) managed by the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Kerala.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register on Kerala CEE / University Portal
For UG programs in Science and Technology streams, register on the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Kerala portal and appear for KEAM. For Arts, Commerce, and Humanities programs, register directly through the University of Kerala's online CAP (Centralised Allotment Process) portal.
Step 2 — Appear for Relevant Entrance / Qualify Merit
For science-based UG programs, appear for KEAM. For Arts and Commerce programs, admission is based on 10+2 merit through the CAP portal. For Law programs, qualify the Kerala Law Entrance Examination. For PG programs, appear for the University PG Entrance / submit marks-based application as per program requirements.
Step 3 — Fill Options / Choice Filling
Log in to the CAP portal and fill your preferred college and course choices in order of preference. For affiliated college admissions, select from among 190+ affiliated colleges across Kerala under the University of Kerala. Strategically rank your choices based on merit, location, and program preference.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment
The university / CEE conducts multiple rounds of seat allotment based on merit, category, and submitted choices. Check allotment results on the respective portal and confirm your allotment within the prescribed deadline by paying the prescribed fee. Additional rounds may be conducted for remaining vacant seats.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Report to your allotted college / university department for document verification. Documents required: 10+2 marksheet and certificate, SSLC certificate, community/category certificate (if applicable), income certificate (for fee concession), transfer certificate, and conduct certificate.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay the prescribed tuition and other fees at your allotted institution. Fees are highly affordable ranging from ₹5,000 to ₹25,000 per year depending on the program. SC/ST students and students from economically weaker sections are eligible for government scholarships and fee concessions.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation
Complete enrollment formalities at your allotted college / university department. Submit all required original documents, complete ID card process, apply for hostel accommodation if needed, and register for the university roll number to confirm your admission.

Placement Notes

The University of Kerala is a premier research and teaching university with a strong emphasis on academic excellence, public service, and research. Unlike technical institutes, its placement outcomes are best measured through civil services, academic appointments, and government sector success. The university's campus departments are particularly strong in Sciences, Social Sciences, Law, and Humanities. The placement cell facilitates industry interactions for programs like MBA, M.Sc Computer Science, and B.Com. The university's proximity to ISRO, VSSC, NIC, and Technopark Thiruvananthapuram offers excellent research internship and placement opportunities for science graduates.
