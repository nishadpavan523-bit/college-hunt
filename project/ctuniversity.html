About CT University

CT University (CTU) is a multidisciplinary private university established in 2016, located on NH-95, Ferozepur Road, Ludhiana, Punjab. Founded by the CT Group of Institutions, the university is recognized by UGC and approved by AICTE, PCI, BCI, and NCTE. CT University offers programs across engineering, management, law, healthcare, pharmacy, hospitality, design, agriculture, sciences, and humanities — aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP). With 1800+ recruiters, 20,000+ placements, and 800+ industry collaborations, CTU is known for its industry-oriented education, skill-based learning, and strong entrepreneurship ecosystem.

Admission Process (UG & PG)
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), CAT/MAT/CMAT (MBA), or university-level entrance test as applicable to your program.
Step 2 — Apply via Admission Portal
Register at the official CT University admission portal at ctuniversity.in, fill the application form, and pay the registration fee.
Step 3 — Merit List / University Selection
Admission via merit list based on entrance scores and qualifying marks. Law and most UG programs are merit-based through the university.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit 10th, 12th marksheets, graduation certificates (for PG), category certificate, ID proof, and entrance scorecard.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester/annual fees online and complete enrolment formalities to confirm your seat. Scholarships assessed at this stage.
