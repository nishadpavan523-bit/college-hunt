About University of Rajasthan

The University of Rajasthan (UniRaj), established in 1947, is one of the oldest and most prestigious state universities in India, located on a sprawling 175-acre campus in Jaipur. Recognized by the UGC and accredited with the prestigious NAAC A+ Grade, it serves as the affiliating university for hundreds of colleges across Rajasthan. The university offers a diverse range of programs spanning Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Management, Education, Engineering, and more through its numerous faculties and departments. It houses world-class research facilities, a rich library heritage, and strong cultural traditions that have shaped generations of scholars and leaders.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear in CUET / Qualifying Exam
For UG and PG programs, candidates must appear in CUET (Common University Entrance Test) or qualify relevant entrance exams. Some programs are merit-based on 12th / graduation marks.
Step 2 — Register on University Portal
Visit the official University of Rajasthan admission portal and register with valid personal and academic details. Keep scanned documents ready.
Step 3 — Fill Application Form & Pay Fee
Complete the online application form for your desired program, pay the application fee online, and submit before the deadline.
Step 4 — Merit List / Counselling
University publishes merit lists based on CUET scores / qualifying exam scores / 12th marks. Shortlisted candidates are called for document verification and counselling.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, graduation certificate (for PG), CUET scorecard, category certificate, migration certificate, and passport photos.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the program fee and hostel fee online. Collect your enrollment number and student ID to confirm admission to the University of Rajasthan.

University of Rajasthan is one of India's oldest and most respected state universities with a legacy spanning over 75 years. Its strength lies in Arts, Social Sciences, Law, Commerce, and Science education. Graduates hold prominent positions in government services, academia, journalism, banking, and law. The university's proximity to Jaipur's thriving business environment supports strong industry linkages for professional programs like MBA and MCA.
