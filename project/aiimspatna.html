About AIIMS Patna

AIIMS Patna is one of the original 6 new AIIMS established under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana (PMSSY) Phase I — announced by PM Atal Bihari Vajpayee on 15 August 2003. It was established by ordinance on 16 July 2012 and made permanent by the AIIMS (Amendment) Act 2012. The first MBBS and B.Sc. Nursing batches enrolled in 2012; OPD/IPD services began in mid-2013. The motto — "Koi Bhi Bimari Se Pidit Na Ho" (May no one suffer from illness) — reflects its mission as the premier tertiary care institution for Bihar and East India. Recognised by NMC, INC, and formerly MCI, AIIMS Patna is approved by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, GoI and offers MBBS, B.Sc. Nursing, M.Sc. Nursing, MD, MS, MDS, DM, M.Ch., and PhD programmes. NIRF 2026 data shows UG graduates earn an average package of ₹14.26 LPA.

MBBS Admission Process
📅 Annual Admissions: MBBS admissions happen every year via NEET-UG + MCC All India Counselling. Apply at mcc.nic.in after NEET-UG results.
Step 1 — Appear in NEET-UG (NTA)
Appear in NEET-UG conducted by NTA (usually in May each year). Target score for AIIMS Patna (General, AIQ): 600–650+ | NEET 2025 closing rank (General, AIQ): ~4,952 (last round).
Step 2 — MCC All India Quota (AIQ) Counselling Registration
Register on the MCC portal (mcc.nic.in) for All India Quota counselling. AIIMS Patna has 100% AIQ seats — open to candidates from all states across India.
Step 3 — Choice Filling & Locking
Fill and lock your institute preferences during the designated window. Round 1 → Round 2 → Mop-Up Round → Stray Vacancy Round.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment & Document Verification
Documents required: NEET scorecard, 10th & 12th marksheets, ID proof, category certificate (if applicable), medical fitness certificate. Report for verification at the allotted centre.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Reporting at AIIMS Patna
Total MBBS fee for Indian nationals: ₹5,856 (entire 5.5-year course). Report to AIIMS Patna, Phulwari Sharif. Hostel is compulsory — allotment done at the time of reporting.
PG / Super-Specialty Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear in INI CET (for MD/MS/MDS) or INI SS (for DM/M.Ch.)
INI CET conducted by AIIMS Delhi twice yearly (January & July sessions). AIIMS Patna PG exams: Theory May 1–8 | Practicals May 10–17 (2025 schedule). INI SS for super-specialty DM/M.Ch. conducted separately.
Step 2 — Counselling via INI CET / INI SS Portal
Centralised counselling through the INI CET/INI SS portal. Select AIIMS Patna as preference for your desired department and specialisation.
Step 3 — Security Deposit (DM/M.Ch. only)
For DM/M.Ch. super-specialty admissions: ₹3 Lakh security deposit via RTGS/NEFT at the time of seat acceptance.
Step 4 — Joining & Residency
PG students serve as Senior Residents with monthly stipends: MD/MS ₹90,000–1,00,000/month; DM/M.Ch. ~₹1,00,000+/month. All PG students are residentially housed at AIIMS Patna.
