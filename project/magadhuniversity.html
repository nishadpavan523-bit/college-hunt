About Magadh University

Magadh University (MU), established on 1 March 1962 under the Magadh University Act 1961, is a prominent State Public University situated in Bodh Gaya, District Gaya, Bihar — near the UNESCO World Heritage Mahabodhi Temple complex. Founded by Satyendra Narayan Sinha, the university carries the motto "Amritam Tu Vidya" (Knowledge is Eternal).

MU is the First Digital University of Bihar — with entirely digitised curriculum, attendance, degree requests and administration. It has a vast jurisdiction spanning the districts of Gaya, Nawada, Jehanabad, Arwal and Aurangabad, governing 19 constituent colleges and 175 affiliated colleges.
