About Assam University

Assam University, established in 1994, is a Central University located in Silchar, Assam. The university serves the academic needs of the southern Assam region and beyond, with a main campus at Silchar and an additional campus at Diphu in Karbi Anglong. It offers a wide range of programs spanning Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Education, Engineering, Management, and Social Sciences — from undergraduate to doctoral level. Recognized by UGC and approved by AICTE/NCTE where applicable, it remains one of the premier institutions in Northeast India.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply via CUET Portal
Apply through CUET-UG / CUET-PG or respective entrance exam portals based on program.
Step 2 — Register on University Portal
Register on the Assam University admission portal after appearing in the entrance exam.
Step 3 — Upload Documents
Upload academic certificates, category documents, and other required credentials.
Step 4 — Merit List / Results
Check merit lists or entrance test results on the university website.
Step 5 — Counselling & Verification
Attend counselling session and complete document verification.
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay admission fees online to confirm your seat.
