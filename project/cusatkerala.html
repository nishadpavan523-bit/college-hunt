About CUSAT

Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) is a premier State University established in 1971, recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited with a coveted NAAC A+ Grade. Situated on a lush ~150-acre campus in Kalamassery, Kochi — the commercial capital of Kerala — CUSAT is one of India's most reputed science and technology universities with a strong legacy in engineering, law, management, and applied sciences. CUSAT offers B.Tech, LL.B, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, M.Sc, and Ph.D programs across its 30+ departments and schools. The university is consistently ranked among the top universities in Kerala and South India (NIRF) and has an excellent placement record — highest package of ₹45 LPA and a B.Tech average of ₹10 LPA. Admission to B.Tech is through the CUSAT CAT (Common Admission Test) conducted by the university.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify CUSAT CAT
Register and appear for the CUSAT Common Admission Test (CUSAT CAT), the university's own entrance exam conducted annually. For B.Tech, the test covers Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics at 10+2 level. For management programmes, CAT / CUSAT CAT scores are accepted. GATE score is additionally accepted for M.Tech admissions.
Step 2 — Application & Registration
Apply online through the official CUSAT admissions portal (admissions.cusat.ac.in) after the CUSAT CAT results are announced. Upload required documents including CUSAT CAT scorecard, 10th and 12th marksheets, identity proof, passport-size photograph, and category certificate (if applicable).
Step 3 — Merit List & Rank Publication
CUSAT publishes a merit list / rank list based on CUSAT CAT scores. Separate rank lists are published for General, SC, ST, OBC, and other reserved categories. Eligible candidates are called for allotment based on their rank and the number of available seats in each programme.
Step 4 — Choice Filling & Allotment
Eligible candidates fill their preferred department and programme choices on the CUSAT centralised allotment portal. Seats are allotted based on rank, category, and choices submitted. Multiple rounds of allotment are conducted to fill vacant seats. Candidates must accept or reject allotted seats within the stipulated deadline.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Report to the designated CUSAT centre for document verification. Documents required: CUSAT CAT admit card and scorecard, 10th and 12th marksheets and pass certificates, transfer certificate, conduct certificate, category certificate (if applicable), and PwD certificate (if applicable).
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed tuition and other fees online through the CUSAT admissions portal. B.Tech total programme fees are approximately ₹1.5–₹2 Lakh for General category, significantly lower than private institutions. Fee concessions are available for SC/ST/OBC and economically weaker section students as per Kerala Government norms.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation & Reporting
Report to CUSAT campus on the specified date for admission confirmation, physical enrollment, and hostel allotment. Submit original documents, complete biometric registration, receive your student ID card, and pay hostel and mess fees if opting for on-campus accommodation.

Placement Notes

CUSAT is one of the best value-for-money universities in South India with an excellent placement record relative to its very affordable fee structure. The strongest placement performance is in CSE, IT, Electronics, and Naval Architecture. CUSAT's unique advantage is its strategic location in Kochi — Kerala's IT and commercial capital — enabling strong industry partnerships with IT parks like Technopark and SmartCity. The UG Median Package stands at ₹6 LPA, with top CSE graduates consistently securing ₹10–₹20 LPA offers. Over 150 companies participated in recent placement seasons across IT, core engineering, finance, shipping, and consulting sectors.
