About Amity University Haryana

Amity University Haryana, situated at Amity Education Valley, Manesar, Gurugram, is a prestigious private university established in 2010. It holds a coveted NAAC A+ accreditation and is approved by UGC and AICTE. Distinctively, it is Asia's only university with both WASC (USA) and QAA (UK) accreditation, alongside ACBSP (USA) and IET (UK) recognitions. The university is ranked #33 in Law (NIRF 2025) and in the 101–125 band in Management (NIRF 2025). It offers diverse programmes across Engineering, Management, Law, Sciences, Health Sciences, and more, with 200+ hi-tech labs and 20+ interdisciplinary research centres. Students also benefit from an exclusive International Study Program with opportunities to study in the UK, USA, and Canada.

Admission Process
Apply at: www.amity.edu/gurugram · Admissions 2026–27 Open · Application fee applicable.
Step 1 — Visit Official Website & Fill Online Application
Visit www.amity.edu/gurugram. Fill out the online application form and pay the application fee for your desired programme.
Step 2 — Appear for Entrance Exam
Appear for the relevant entrance exam: JEE Main / NATA (B.Tech/B.Arch), CUET / CAT / MAT / XAT / GMAT (BBA/MBA), GATE (M.Tech), GPAT (M.Pharm), CLAT PG (LLM), CUET-PG/Merit (M.Sc/MA), or Amity PET (PhD). Amity JEE is also accepted for B.Tech.
Step 3 — Shortlisting for GD & PI
Shortlisted candidates are called for a Group Discussion (GD) and Personal Interview (PI). JEE/CAT/GATE/CUET/NET qualified candidates may receive direct admission without a separate written test.
Step 4 — Offer Letter & Document Verification
Admitted candidates receive an offer letter. Submit original documents: 12th/Graduation marksheets, caste certificate (if applicable), entrance score card, ID proof, and passport-size photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Seat Confirmation
Pay the admission fee as per the selected programme and category. Seat is confirmed upon fee payment and successful document submission. Hostel allocation (optional) is done separately.
Step 6 — PhD Admission (Separate Process)
For PhD: Appear for Amity PET (PhD Entrance Test) → attend Research Proposal Presentation + Personal Interview. UGC-NET JRF / GATE / GPAT holders may receive direct interview calls with higher weightage.

Career Pathways

Amity University Haryana graduates are placed across diverse sectors. B.Tech graduates find roles in leading IT, engineering, and defence firms. MBA graduates are absorbed in top management and consulting firms. Law graduates pursue careers in top law firms, judiciary, and corporate legal departments. The university's International Study Program provides additional global exposure, enhancing international career prospects. Top recruiters include Amazon, Physics Wallah, Bajaj Capitals, and many more.
