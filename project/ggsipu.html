About GGSIPU

Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University (GGSIPU / IPU) is a State University established in 1998 by the Government of NCT of Delhi under the Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University Act, 1998. Located in Dwarka, New Delhi, the university is accredited with NAAC A+ Grade and recognised by the UGC, AICTE, Bar Council of India (BCI), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI), and other statutory bodies. GGSIPU affiliates over 150+ colleges across Delhi-NCR offering programs in Engineering & Technology, Management, Law, Medical Sciences, Pharmacy, Education, Journalism, Architecture, and more. Admission is primarily through the university's own IPU CET and national-level exams such as JEE Main, NEET UG, CLAT, and others.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify the Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), IPU CET (BBA/BCA/MBA/MCA/LLB/M.Tech etc.), NEET UG (MBBS/BDS), CLAT (LLB/LLM), GATE (M.Tech), GPAT (M.Pharm), CAT/MAT (MBA), or NATA (B.Arch).
Step 2 — Register on the IPU Admissions Portal
Visit ipu.admissions.nic.in or ipu.ac.in. Register using your scorecard, personal details, category certificate, and required documents during the admissions window announced by GGSIPU.
Step 3 — Fill College & Course Preferences
Choose preferred colleges and courses (University Schools or affiliated colleges) during the online counselling. University Schools on campus are most competitive; multiple counselling rounds are held.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th & 12th marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile/residence proof (Delhi domicile for state quota), ID proof, and passport photographs.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is merit-based using entrance exam rank, category, and choice preferences. Delhi domicile candidates compete for state quota seats. All-India seats are open to non-Delhi candidates.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Reporting
Pay the prescribed tuition fee at the allotted University School / affiliated college and confirm admission by reporting in person within the deadline for enrollment and document submission.

Career Notes

GGSIPU — particularly its University School of Information Technology (USIT) — is among the most sought-after engineering destinations in Delhi. CSE and IT programs from top IPU colleges like MSIT, BPIT, MAIT, KIET, and Maharaja Agrasen Institute command strong campus placements with IT giants. The MBA and law programs have consistently strong placements in consulting, banking, and corporate law. The placement rate across disciplines exceeds 80%, with University Schools recording higher rates and packages.
