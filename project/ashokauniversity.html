University Overview

Ashoka University was founded with the vision of providing world-class liberal education in India. The university follows an interdisciplinary curriculum model inspired by top global universities. Students can combine majors and minors across multiple disciplines, and the institution focuses heavily on critical thinking, writing, research and leadership development. Ashoka University is considered one of India's leading Liberal Arts and Sciences universities, known for interdisciplinary education, global faculty and research-oriented learning.
