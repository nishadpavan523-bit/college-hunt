South Asian University (SAU) is a unique international university established by the eight SAARC nations — Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka. Located in New Delhi, it offers a fully residential, globally collaborative environment spanning Science, Engineering, Law, Management, Humanities, and Social Sciences.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register for JEE Main (B.Tech), CLAT (Law programs), or SAU Entrance Test for all other programs. Application windows typically open Jan–Mar each year.
Step 2 — Appear for Exam
B.Tech candidates sit for JEE Main conducted by NTA. Law candidates appear for CLAT. All other UG/PG/PhD candidates sit for the SAU Entrance Examination held at designated centres.
Step 3 — Merit List / Counselling
B.Tech via JoSAA counselling. Law programs via CLAT merit. All other programs through SAU's own centralised merit-based counselling on the SAU portal.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th & 12th marksheets, nationality/passport documents, category certificate (if applicable), and photographs at the SAU campus.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allotment
Pay semester fees via SAU's online portal. As a fully residential campus, hostel accommodation is compulsory and allocated after enrolment.

Scholarships & Financial Aid
🏆 SAARC Nation Scholarship
Reserved seats and fee concessions for students from all 8 SAARC member nations — Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, and Sri Lanka. Each country is allocated a quota of seats.
🎓 Merit-cum-Means Scholarship
Financial assistance awarded to students with excellent academic performance who demonstrate financial need. Covers partial to full tuition fee waiver based on assessment.
🔬 PhD Research Fellowship
Full-time PhD scholars are eligible for monthly research fellowships equivalent to UGC/CSIR JRF standards, funded by the SAU corpus and SAARC development fund contributions.
🌐 SAARC Development Fund (SDF) Grant
Additional grants provided through the SAARC Development Fund for students from economically weaker SAARC nations. Covers accommodation and living expenses during the academic year.
Note
Scholarship details and eligibility criteria may vary each academic year. Students are advised to check the official SAU website at www.sau.int or contact the admissions office for the latest scholarship notifications.
