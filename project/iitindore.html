About IIT Indore

The Indian Institute of Technology Indore (IIT Indore), established in 2009 as part of the second wave of IIT expansion by the Government of India, is one of the fastest-growing and most rapidly rising technical institutes in the country. Located at Simrol, near Indore in Madhya Pradesh — the commercial capital of central India — IIT Indore operates on a lush 510-acre permanent campus with state-of-the-art infrastructure. Designated as an Institute of National Importance, IIT Indore has quickly climbed the NIRF rankings and now ranks among the Top 20 Engineering Institutions in India (NIRF 2024). The institute offers programs across Engineering, Science, Humanities, and Management, with a strong emphasis on research and interdisciplinary study. IIT Indore is known for its vibrant academic culture, excellent faculty, modern labs, and consistently improving placement record. With 16 academic departments and a strong focus on innovation and entrepreneurship, IIT Indore is firmly establishing its place among the elite technical institutions of India.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register at IIT Indore Admissions Portal
Visit admissions.iiti.ac.in. Create an account and register for your chosen program — B.Tech (JoSAA), M.Tech, MBA, M.Sc, M.Des, or PhD. Each program has separate application windows and deadlines. Check the official academic calendar before applying.
Step 2 — Ensure a Valid Entrance Score
B.Tech admissions require a valid JEE Advanced rank via JoSAA counselling. M.Tech requires a valid GATE score. M.Sc requires IIT JAM. MBA requires CAT score. M.Des requires CEED. PhD programs require GATE / CSIR-NET / JAM / JEST or IIT Indore's departmental written test.
Step 3 — Fill Application & Upload Documents
Complete the online application with academic records, valid entrance scorecard, photograph, ID proof, caste certificate (if applicable), and a Statement of Purpose for PhD/PG research programs. Double-check all details before final submission. Application fees are non-refundable.
Step 4 — JoSAA Counselling (B.Tech)
B.Tech admissions are conducted centrally via the JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) portal. After JEE Advanced result declaration, candidates participate in multiple rounds of seat allocation. Choices must be filled carefully; IIT Indore opening and closing ranks are extremely competitive.
Step 5 — Shortlisting & Written Test / Interview (PG / PhD)
For M.Tech, shortlisting is based on GATE score. For PhD and M.Sc programs, a departmental written test followed by a research interview is conducted at IIT Indore campus. Candidates are evaluated on subject knowledge, analytical skills, and research aptitude.
Step 6 — Offer Letter, Fee Payment & Enrollment
Selected candidates receive official offer letters. Submit original documents for physical verification — all degree certificates, marksheets, entrance scorecards, category certificates, and government-issued ID. Pay the semester fee within the stipulated deadline and complete formalities for the July/August intake.
