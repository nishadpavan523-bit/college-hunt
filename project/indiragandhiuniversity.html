About Indira Gandhi University

Indira Gandhi University (IGU), established in 2013, is a prominent State University located in Meerpur, Rewari, Haryana, and is UGC recognized. The university spans a significant academic jurisdiction with over 140 affiliated colleges across neighboring districts of Haryana. IGU houses more than 10 faculties covering Engineering & Technology, Commerce & Management, Law, Humanities, Pharmaceutical Sciences, and Physical Sciences. Offering UG, PG, PhD, and Integrated Professional Programs, IGU serves as the primary higher education hub for the Rewari–Mahendragarh region, providing accessible quality education to students from across southern Haryana.

Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Access the IGU Registration Portal
Visit the official dynamic registration portal at online.igu.ac.in and create your applicant account to begin the admission process.
Step 2 — Set Regional Status & Submit Credentials
Set up your regional status criteria (Haryana domicile / other state) and submit your academic credentials and personal details as required.
Step 3 — Fill Academic Details & Select Course
Fill in academic specifics including qualifying marks, subject streams, and select your preferred course track (MBA, M.Tech, M.Sc., M.A., etc.).
Step 4 — Upload Documents
Upload cleanly scanned copies of your signature, standard identity proofs, academic marksheets, and category certificates as specified in the form.
Step 5 — Complete Fee Payment
Complete the registration/application fee payment via the online web gateway. Retain the payment receipt and application reference number for future correspondence.
Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), HSTES / Institutional Merit Test (B.Pharmacy), or IGU Common Entrance Test (LLB / BHMCT / Integrated M.Com) as applicable to your chosen program.
Step 2 — Apply via IGU Online Portal
Register at online.igu.ac.in, complete the application form with accurate academic details, upload required documents, and pay the application fee.
Step 3 — Merit List & Seat Allotment
B.Tech admissions through JEE Main scores and Haryana state counselling. All other UG programs through IGU merit list based on entrance scores and qualifying examination marks.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Submit 10th & 12th marksheets, category certificate, domicile proof, entrance scorecard, and passport-size photographs. Complete fee payment to confirm your admission.

Placement Notes

Indira Gandhi University facilitates campus placements for students across its various departments through its Training & Placement Cell. B.Tech (CSE) graduates find opportunities in IT and software firms across the Delhi-NCR corridor, given the university's proximity to the capital region. B.Pharmacy graduates are placed in pharma companies, hospitals, and drug regulatory bodies. LLB graduates pursue careers in law firms, courts, and government legal departments. MBA graduates find roles in banking, finance, and management domains. The university's large affiliated college network across Rewari, Mahendragarh, and neighboring districts amplifies access to regional employment opportunities. Students also benefit from government recruitment — Haryana state civil services and central government posts are a major career pathway for IGU graduates.
