About BBDU

Babu Banarasi Das University (BBDU) is a UGC Approved Private University established in 2010 in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Located in the heart of Lucknow — the cultural capital of UP — BBDU offers a wide range of UG, PG, Diploma and PhD programmes spanning Engineering, Management, Law, Pharmacy, Biotechnology and Architecture. The university features modern residential infrastructure with separate hostels, a digital library, sports complex and modern laboratories. BBDU emphasises industry exposure through internships, projects, technical workshops and coding competitions, and provides strong support for competitive exam preparation. Merit-based and government scholarships are available for eligible students.

Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), CLAT/CUET (BA LLB), NATA (B.Arch), or CUET (BBA/BCA/B.Com/B.Pharmacy/B.Sc.) as applicable for your chosen programme.
Step 2 — Apply on the BBDU Admissions Portal
Register at bbdu.ac.in/admissions. Fill the application form with personal and academic details. Pay the registration fee and upload required documents including entrance scorecard.
Step 3 — Merit List / Counselling
BBDU releases merit lists based on entrance scores and 10+2 marks. Check the portal regularly for merit list announcements. For B.Tech, JEE Main rank is the primary criterion.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Carry original 10th and 12th marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate, domicile proof, and passport-size photographs for in-person verification at the university.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the semester/annual fee online to confirm your seat. Fees: B.Tech ₹4–10 Lakh total · BBA/BCA ₹2–4 Lakh total · B.Pharmacy ₹4–7 Lakh total. Collect student ID and hostel allocation.
Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Register and Apply on Portal
Visit bbdu.ac.in/admissions. Select your PG course. MBA: submit CAT/CUET-PG score. M.Tech: submit GATE score. MCA/M.Sc./MA/LLM: CUET-PG score or merit-based. M.Pharmacy: GPAT score.
Step 2 — Personal Interview (where applicable)
MBA candidates attend a Personal Interview after shortlisting on the basis of CAT/CUET-PG score. M.Tech: GATE score based. Other PG: merit-based selection after document verification.
Step 3 — Document Verification & Enrolment
Submit graduation degree, marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate, and ID proof. Pay fees online. Confirm enrolment and collect hostel allocation if opting for residential accommodation.

Placement Notes

BBDU's Training & Placement Cell facilitates campus recruitment across its diverse programmes. B.Tech and BCA graduates in CSE and AI find opportunities in Software Engineering, Data Analytics and AI-driven roles. MBA graduates pursue careers in Management, Banking, FMCG and Corporate sectors. Biotechnology and Pharmacy graduates enter the pharma industry and research organisations. The university strongly encourages internships and projects during the programme, and hosts technical workshops and coding competitions to boost practical industry readiness. Law graduates enter corporate legal careers, courts and law firms.
