About PDEU

Pandit Deendayal Energy University (PDEU), formerly known as Pandit Deendayal Petroleum University (PDPU), was established on April 4, 2007 by GERMI (Gujarat Energy Research and Management Institute) in Gandhinagar, Gujarat. It is a Private University recognized by UGC, AICTE and accredited with a prestigious NAAC A++ grade. PDEU is also recognized by the Scientific & Industrial Research Organization (SIRO) under the Ministry of Science & Technology, Government of India.

The university houses four schools: School of Technology (SOT), School of Petroleum Technology (SPT), School of Petroleum Management (SPM), and the School of Liberal Studies (SLS). PDEU is NIRF ranked 98 in Engineering and 89 in Management (NIRF 2025). The university offers programs focused on energy, petroleum, technology, and management, with strong industry linkages to companies like Shell, Adani, Reliance, ONGC, and IOCL.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify the Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main or GUJCET (B.Tech), GATE (M.Tech/PhD), CAT/XAT/NMAT (MBA), or University Written Test (BBA/BA/BCom/BCA/MCA/MA programs) as applicable to your program.
Step 2 — Register on PDEU Admissions Portal
Visit pdeu.ac.in and fill the online application form. Upload scanned documents including marksheets, scorecard, category certificate, and photograph within the application deadline.
Step 3 — Gujarat ACPC Counselling (B.Tech)
B.Tech seats are allotted via Gujarat ACPC (Admission Committee for Professional Courses). 33% of seats are reserved for Gujarat domicile students and 67% are open for All-India candidates based on JEE Main rank.
Step 4 — Document Verification & GD/PI (For MBA)
Submit original documents for verification: 10+2 and graduation marksheets, entrance exam scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), and identity proof. MBA aspirants additionally appear for Group Discussion and Personal Interview at PDEU campus.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment & Fee Payment
Seat allotment is based on entrance exam rank / merit. Pay the prescribed semester fees online to confirm admission within the given deadline.
Step 6 — Reporting to Campus
Report to PDEU Gandhinagar campus with original documents and fee receipt for physical verification and enrollment before the stipulated date.

Placement Notes

PDEU is Gujarat's top private engineering university for energy and petroleum-sector placements. The 2025 placement season saw 175+ companies participate with a highest package of ₹60 LPA for B.Tech. The MBA program at the School of Petroleum Management (SPM) achieved 100% placement. PDEU's unique positioning in the energy, petroleum, and oil & gas sectors gives its graduates an edge in niche high-paying roles not available at most engineering colleges. CSE and AI/ML programs are seeing increasing demand from IT companies, diversifying placement outcomes further.
