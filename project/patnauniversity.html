About Patna University

Patna University, established in 1917, is one of the oldest universities in India and the oldest in the state of Bihar. Located on the iconic Ashok Rajpath along the Ganges in Patna, it is accredited with NAAC B+ Grade and recognised by the UGC and Bar Council of India. The university operates with a unitary-cum-affiliating structure, comprising constituent colleges including Patna College, Science College, Patna Women's College, Commerce College, Law College, and others. It offers programs in Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Management, and Education. Admission is through CUET, PAT (Patna Admission Test), and merit-based processes depending on the program.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify the Entrance Exam
Appear for CUET UG/PG (Central University Entrance Test) or PAT (Patna Admission Test) for UG/PG programs. For Law: CLAT. For B.Ed: Bihar B.Ed Combined Entrance Test (BPJEE). For PhD: UGC-NET / GATE / PU Research Entrance Test.
Step 2 — Register on the Patna University Portal
Visit patnauniversity.ac.in. Register using your CUET/PAT score or qualifying marks, personal details, category certificate, and required documents within the advertised admissions window.
Step 3 — Choose College & Course Preferences
Indicate preferred constituent college (Patna College, Science College, Patna Women's College, Commerce College, Law College, B.N. College, etc.) and course. Seats are very limited in constituent colleges.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th & 12th / graduation marksheets, CUET/PAT scorecard, category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS), domicile certificate (Bihar domicile preferred), migration certificate, and passport photos.
Step 5 — Merit List & Seat Allotment
Allotment is merit-based on CUET score / PAT rank / 10+2 marks. Separate merit lists are released for each college and department. Bihar domicile candidates compete for state quota seats.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay the applicable fee at the allotted constituent college or department and report in person within the deadline to confirm enrollment, submit original documents, and receive your enrollment ID.

Career Notes

Patna University is one of India's oldest institutions with a strong academic and civil services tradition. Many notable alumni have served as IAS/IPS officers, judges, and ministers. The campus placement infrastructure is relatively modest compared to newer universities, and a large percentage of graduates pursue UPSC, BPSC, SSC, banking, and teaching jobs. Science and computer science graduates from Science College do well in private sector and higher studies. The university's heritage and reputation still carries significant weight in Bihar's administrative and legal circles.
