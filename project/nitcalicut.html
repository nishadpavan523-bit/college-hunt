About NIT Calicut

National Institute of Technology Calicut (NIT Calicut), also known as NITC, is an Institute of National Importance established in 1961 by the Government of India, functioning under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Located in Kozhikode (Calicut), Kerala, NIT Calicut is one of the oldest and most prestigious NITs in India. The institute offers B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA, MBA, M.Sc, and Ph.D programs across Engineering, Architecture, Sciences, and Management disciplines. NIT Calicut is NAAC A+ accredited and consistently ranks among the top NITs in India (NIRF 2025). The institute has an outstanding placement record — highest package of ₹1.02 Crore, a B.Tech average of ₹20 LPA, and a UG median of ₹12 LPA. Admission to B.Tech is through JEE Main followed by JoSAA / CSAB Counselling.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify JEE Main
Appear and qualify JEE Main (conducted by NTA). A valid JEE Main score and percentile is mandatory for admission to all B.Tech programs at NIT Calicut. Candidates must secure a minimum qualifying percentile as prescribed by JoSAA norms. For M.Tech programs, qualify GATE and register through CCMN.
Step 2 — Register through JoSAA / CSAB
Register on the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) portal using your JEE Main application number and password. JoSAA conducts the centralized counselling process for all NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. CSAB conducts special rounds for remaining vacant seats. Registration is mandatory to participate in seat allocation.
Step 3 — Choice Filling
Fill your preferred program and institute choices on the JoSAA portal in order of preference. Strategic choice filling is important — list NIT Calicut programs such as CSE, ECE, and Electrical Engineering based on your rank and category. You may fill multiple choices to maximize your chances of securing a seat.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment
JoSAA conducts multiple rounds of seat allotment based on your JEE Main rank, category, and submitted choices. Check your seat allotment result on the JoSAA portal after each round and decide whether to accept, float, or slide as per your preference.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Report to the designated Reporting Centre for online document verification. Documents required: JEE Main admit card and scorecard, 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates, category certificate (if applicable), and PwD certificate (if applicable). Verify all documents carefully before reporting.
Step 6 — Fee Payment
Pay the prescribed seat acceptance fee and program fees online through the JoSAA/NIT Calicut portal. Total B.Tech program fee is approximately ₹5–6 Lakh for General/OBC/EWS categories. SC/ST/PwD students receive full tuition fee waiver as per Government of India norms.
Step 7 — Admission Confirmation
Report to NIT Calicut campus on the designated date for physical reporting and admission confirmation. Complete all enrollment formalities, submit original documents, pay hostel and mess fees, and complete hostel accommodation allotment to confirm your admission.

Placement Notes

NIT Calicut is one of the best-performing NITs in India with consistently excellent placement outcomes. The strongest performance is in CSE, ECE, and Electrical Engineering. The institute boasts strong internship opportunities and high PPO conversion rates. The UG Median Package stands at ₹12 LPA and the PG Median Package at ₹10 LPA, reflecting strong industry demand across disciplines. Over 200 companies participated in recent placement seasons, covering software, finance, core engineering, semiconductor, and consulting sectors.
