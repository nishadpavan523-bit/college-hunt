About the University

Pondicherry University is a premier Central University located in Kalapet, Puducherry, established in 1985. Spread across approximately 800 acres of peaceful coastal campus, the university is known for academic diversity and a student-friendly environment. It offers programs across 15 Schools and more than 40 departments, covering disciplines from Engineering and Sciences to Law, Management, and Education. The university is accredited with NAAC A+ Grade and recognized by UGC, AICTE, NCTE, and BCI. Students preparing for UPSC, NET/JRF, and government examinations benefit significantly from the academic environment. The serene coastal setting supports focused studies and research.
