About Veer Kunwar Singh University

Veer Kunwar Singh University (VKSU) is a public state university established in 1992 under the Bihar University Act, located in Arrah (Ara), Bihar. Named after the legendary freedom fighter Veer Kunwar Singh, the university serves as the apex institution for higher education across the Bhojpur, Buxar, Rohtas, and Kaimur districts. VKSU is accredited with NAAC "B+" Grade and is recognized by UGC, AICTE, and AIU. It offers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, professional, and Ph.D. programs in Arts, Science, Commerce, Management, Education, Law, and Computer Applications through its multiple constituent and affiliated colleges.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Check Eligibility
Ensure you meet the eligibility for the desired program. UG requires 10+2; PG requires graduation; Ph.D. requires Master's with 55%.
Step 2 — Apply Online / Offline
Fill the application form on the university portal or at the respective college. Application windows typically open May–July each year.
Step 3 — Merit List / Entrance Test
UG programs (Arts, Science, Commerce) are merit-based. Professional programs like B.Ed. and LL.B. require entrance tests.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th & 12th marksheets, migration certificate, category certificate (if applicable), ID proof, and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester/annual fees at the university/college cash counter or via online portal and complete enrolment formalities.

Placement Notes

Veer Kunwar Singh University's placement cell provides aptitude training and interview preparation to students. Major recruiting sectors include IT, Banking, Education, and Sales. The highest package recorded stands at ₹6–10 LPA while the average package ranges between ₹2.5–4 LPA. Internship opportunities are available in selected departments. PG and Ph.D. graduates also find opportunities in research, academia, and government sectors.
