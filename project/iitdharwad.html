About IIT Dharwad

Indian Institute of Technology Dharwad (IIT Dharwad / IITDH) is a premier technical institution established by the Government of India in 2016 under the NIT Amendment Act. It is one of the six new IITs set up in the third generation of IIT expansion. The institute is located in Dharwad, Karnataka — a city known for its rich academic tradition — and currently operates from a temporary campus while its sprawling 470-acre permanent campus at Chikkamalligawad is being developed. IIT Dharwad offers B.Tech, M.Tech, and PhD programs across 8 core engineering and science departments. Though young, the institute has already established research collaborations with leading global universities and industry partners, and is growing its research output year-on-year. Admission to B.Tech is through JEE Advanced via JoSAA counselling, placing it among the most competitive undergraduate engineering institutions in India. IIT Dharwad's faculty includes PhDs from top global universities including IITs, IISc, MIT, Stanford, and other premier institutions.

B.Tech Admission Process (JEE Advanced → JoSAA)
Step 1 — Appear for JEE Main (January & April Session)
Register and appear for JEE Main conducted by NTA. Secure a strong percentile to be eligible for JEE Advanced. Top ~2.5 lakh candidates from JEE Main are eligible to appear for JEE Advanced. IIT Dharwad accepts ONLY JEE Advanced scores — JEE Main alone is not sufficient.
Step 2 — Qualify JEE Advanced
Appear for JEE Advanced (conducted by one of the IITs on a rotational basis). IIT Dharwad B.Tech admissions happen through JEE Advanced merit. Being a newer IIT, its closing ranks are higher than older IITs — making it accessible to a wider range of JEE Advanced candidates while maintaining the IIT brand value.
Step 3 — Register on JoSAA Portal
Register on the JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) portal at josaa.nic.in. Fill in your choices of institute-branch combinations. IIT Dharwad appears as a separate institute in JoSAA. Fill your preferences carefully, listing all desired branches at IIT Dharwad.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment (Rounds 1–6)
JoSAA conducts up to 6 rounds of seat allotment based on JEE Advanced rank and filled choices. Candidates can choose to freeze, float, or slide their seat after each round. IIT Dharwad CSE and EE branches are the most competitive among its offerings.
Step 5 — Online Reporting & Document Verification
After seat acceptance and fee payment on JoSAA, report online with scanned documents: JEE Advanced scorecard, 10th & 12th certificates, category certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS/PwD), ID proof, and photographs. Complete online reporting within the JoSAA deadline.
Step 6 — Physical Reporting & Enrollment at IIT Dharwad
Report physically to IIT Dharwad during the institute's reporting dates with original documents. Verify all submitted details, complete medical examination, pay semester fees, and receive student ID. Attend orientation week before the commencement of the first semester.

Placement Notes

IIT Dharwad's Training & Placement Cell (T&P Cell) actively invites top companies for campus recruitment. Being a newer IIT, placement statistics are steadily improving each year as the institute's reputation grows. CSE, ECE, and EE branches see the highest demand from recruiters. A notable proportion of students opt for higher education abroad (MS/PhD) at top global universities, reflecting the academic strength of the student body. The institute is rapidly building its alumni network and industry partnerships, especially in the Bengaluru tech corridor which is just ~400 km away.
