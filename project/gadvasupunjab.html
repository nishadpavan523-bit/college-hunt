About GADVASU

Guru Angad Dev Veterinary and Animal Sciences University (GADVASU) is a specialized state university established in 2005, located in Ludhiana, Punjab. Recognized by UGC, ICAR and the Veterinary Council of India, it specializes in Veterinary Sciences, Dairy Technology, Fisheries, Animal Biotechnology and Allied Sciences. The main campus is at Ludhiana with constituent colleges at Rampura Phul, Kaljharani, Amritsar and other locations across Punjab.
