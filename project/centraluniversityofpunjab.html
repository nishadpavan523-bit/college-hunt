About Central University of Punjab

Central University of Punjab (CUP) is a premier central university established in 2009 by an Act of Parliament. Located in Bathinda, Punjab, it offers undergraduate, postgraduate and doctoral programs across sciences, humanities, management, law, education, engineering and pharmacy. CUP holds NAAC "A+" accreditation, is ranked #83 by NIRF 2025 in the University category, and is known for its subsidised fee structure and 500-acre green campus.

🎓 B.Tech CSE — Admission Routes
Route 1 · CUET-UG Primary Route — NTA Conducted
Exam: Common University Entrance Test – Undergraduate (CUET-UG), conducted by NTA.
Subjects to appear: Domain subjects — Physics + Mathematics + Chemistry / Biotechnology / Computer Science, plus General Test.
Counselling: After CUET results, register on the Samarth portal (cupcuet.samarth.edu.in). CUP releases category-wise merit lists and conducts up to 6 rounds of counselling.
Score type: CUET marks (out of ~800 depending on papers attempted). No percentile system; raw marks used.
Route 2 · JEE Main Alternative Route — NTA / JOSAA / CSAB
Exam: JEE Main Paper-1 (B.E./B.Tech), conducted by NTA in January & April sessions.
Eligibility: Min. 75% aggregate in 12th PCM (65% for SC/ST/PwD), OR top 20 percentile in respective State Board.
Counselling: Seats are allotted through CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) counselling — the same NIC portal used for NITs/GFTIs. CUP participates as a participating institute. Register at csab.nic.in after JEE Main results.
Score type: JEE Main Percentile Score (out of 100). Category-wise cutoff percentiles are released round-by-round on the CSAB portal.
Seat pool: JEE Main seats are part of the same 60-seat pool as CUET-UG; seat split between routes varies by session.

Admission Process — B.Tech CSE (Dual Route)
🔵 Route A — via CUET-UG
Step 1 — Register for CUET-UG
Register at cuet.nta.nic.in. Select domain subjects: Physics + Maths + Chemistry/CS/Biotechnology + General Test. Deadline typically Jan–Feb.
Step 2 — Appear for CUET-UG
NTA conducts CUET-UG. CUP accepts CUET-UG domain scores for B.Tech CSE (not JEE Main for this route).
Step 3 — Register on Samarth Portal
After results, register at cupcuet.samarth.edu.in. CUP releases category-wise merit lists across up to 6 rounds.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Upload 10th & 12th marksheets, CUET scorecard, category certificate, ID proof. Pay semester fees online to confirm seat.
🟣 Route B — via JEE Main + CSAB
Step 1 — Register for JEE Main
Register at jeemain.nta.nic.in. Appear in Session 1 (Jan) and/or Session 2 (Apr). Best of two sessions' percentile is considered.
Step 2 — JEE Main Result & Score Card
Download JEE Main scorecard from NTA website. Note your Percentile Score and All India Rank (AIR) — both are used by CSAB.
Step 3 — CSAB Counselling Registration
Register at csab.nic.in after JoSAA rounds. CUP CSE seats are available under CSAB Special Round counselling. Fill preferences and lock choices.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment & Reporting
Accept allotted seat on CSAB portal. Pay seat acceptance fee online. Report to CUP Bathinda with original documents for physical verification and enrolment.

Placement Notes

As per the NIRF 2025 report, 120 students were placed from 2-year PG programs in 2024 with a median salary of ₹3.40 LPA (₹4 LPA in 2023). For LLM graduates, the median placement package in 2023 stood at ₹6 LPA. The pharmacy department's median in 2023 was ₹3.47 LPA. CUP's placement cell conducts workshops, industry visits, and campus drives for final-year students. B.Tech CSE and MBA data is not separately published; detailed branch-wise placement reports are available from the university placement cell.

📋 JEE Main Route — Key Points

Eligibility: Min. 75% aggregate in 12th (PCM) — or top-20 percentile in State Board. SC/ST/PwD: 65%.
Counselling Body: CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) — portal: csab.nic.in. CUP participates as a GFTI/Participating Institute.
When to apply: After JoSAA rounds end (typically August). CSAB Special Rounds follow JoSAA for unfilled seats.
Score validity: Best percentile from Session 1 or Session 2 of the same year is accepted.
Seat count: Limited seats via JEE Main route (exact split varies per session — confirm at cup.edu.in).

⚠️ Note: Percentile cutoffs above are indicative estimates based on CSAB trends. Exact official cutoffs are published on csab.nic.in after each round. Always verify there before making decisions.
