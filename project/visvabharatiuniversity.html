About Visva-Bharati University

Visva-Bharati University, founded by Nobel Laureate Rabindranath Tagore in 1921, is one of India's oldest and most distinctive central universities located in Santiniketan, West Bengal. Accredited with NAAC "A" Grade, the university is organized into 10 academic institutes called Bhavanas with over 50 teaching departments offering 200+ courses across UG, PG, and PhD levels. Spanning a sprawling 1,129-acre campus, it is renowned globally for its unique open-air teaching tradition, rich cultural heritage, and excellence in arts, music, languages, sciences, and agriculture.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear for CUET (UG / PG)
Register for and appear in CUET-UG (for undergraduate) or CUET-PG (for postgraduate) conducted by NTA. Fine Arts and Music programs additionally require an aptitude/practical test by the respective Bhavana.
Step 2 — Register on VBU Admissions Portal
Fill the application form on the official Visva-Bharati Admissions Portal with your CUET score, academic records, and personal details.
Step 3 — Wait for Merit / Rank List
Subject-specific merit/rank lists are published on the official website. Check your rank and the cut-offs for your chosen program.
Step 4 — Counselling & Document Verification
Participate in the university counselling process. Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, category certificate, CUET scorecard, and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Seat Confirmation
Pay admission fees online through the university portal to confirm your seat. Hostel allotment is applied separately.

Research Fellowships (PhD & Post-Doctoral)
🏅
UGC-NET JRF/SRF — Awarded to candidates clearing UGC-NET/JRF exams. Covers stipend for doctoral research.
🔬
DST INSPIRE Fellowship — For top-ranking students pursuing doctoral research in basic and applied sciences.
📋
National Fellowship for ST/SC Candidates — Replaces the former Rajiv Gandhi National Fellowship for higher education.
👩‍🔬
Post-Doctoral Fellowship for Women — UGC financial support for women pursuing post-doctoral studies.
⭐
Dr. D.S. Kothari Post-Doctoral Fellowship — Highly sought-after fellowship for science and technology researchers.
📚
Non-NET Fellowship — Monthly stipend for PhD/M.Phil scholars not receiving UGC-JRF.
Postgraduate Scholarships
👧
PG Indira Gandhi Scholarship for Single Girl Child — Monthly stipend for female students who are the only child in their family.
🥇
PG Merit Scholarship for University Rank Holders — Awarded to students securing 1st or 2nd rank across UGC-affiliated universities.
Undergraduate & General Scholarships
🎓
Merit & Merit-cum-Means Scholarship — Awarded by Visva-Bharati to top 5–10 students per department based on academics and family income.
🌟
Swami Vivekananda Merit Cum Means Scholarship — Prominent West Bengal state government scheme for meritorious students.
📝
Post-Matric Scholarships — Available for SC, ST, OBC, EWS, and PwD category students.
