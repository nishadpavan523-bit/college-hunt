About Amity University Punjab

Amity University Punjab is part of the Amity Education Group, one of India's largest private education networks. The Mohali campus is located near Chandigarh International Airport and offers multidisciplinary programs with modern infrastructure, research facilities, international collaborations, and industry-oriented education. Established in 2021, the university is UGC Approved and NAAC Accredited, following the Learning Outcome-Based Curriculum Framework aligned with NEP 2020. With over 12 campuses across India and 2,100+ patents filed across Amity institutions, it is a globally connected academic powerhouse.

Admission Process (UG Programs)
Step 1 — Appear in Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Main (B.Tech), or prepare merit-based documents for other UG programs like BBA, BCA, BA, B.Sc, Law, and Design.
Step 2 — Apply via Admission Portal
Register at the official Amity admission portal, fill the application form with the required academic details, and upload relevant documents.
Step 3 — Merit List / Interview
Shortlisted candidates appear for an interview round (for law and select programs). Merit list is released based on academic performance and entrance scores.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Submit 10th and 12th marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate, ID proof, and passport-sized photographs. Pay the semester/annual fees online to confirm enrolment.
Admission Process (PG Programs)
Step 1 — Submit National Score or Appear for University Test
Submit CAT/MAT/XAT/GMAT scores for MBA. Appear for GATE (M.Tech) or submit graduation merit for MCA and M.Sc programs.
Step 2 — Apply via Official Portal
Fill the online application form and pay the registration fee through the Amity admission portal.
Step 3 — Personal Interview (PI)
Attend the PI round for final selection, conducted via video call or on-campus as applicable.
Step 4 — Enrolment & Fee Payment
Complete document verification — degree certificates, marksheets, category certificate, and ID proof. Pay fees and complete enrolment formalities.

Placement Notes

Amity University Punjab has a dedicated Corporate Resource Centre (CRC) that facilitates campus recruitment, internships, industry training, and corporate mentoring. The university maintains multiple industry collaborations and provides regular placement training for students across all disciplines. Mohali's proximity to Chandigarh's growing IT and industrial corridor — alongside the Amity network's pan-India reach — gives students access to top recruiters from IT, finance, law, design, hospitality, and management sectors.
