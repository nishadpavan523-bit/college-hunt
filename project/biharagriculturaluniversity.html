About BAU

Bihar Agricultural University traces its roots to the Bihar Agricultural College, Sabour — founded on 17 August 1908, making it one of India's oldest agricultural colleges. It was established as a full university by an Act of the Bihar State Legislature on 5 August 2010. BAU holds a prestigious NAAC Grade A accreditation and is ICAR Accredited, ranking 36th in NIRF 2025 under the Agriculture & Allied Sectors category. The university operates through 6 constituent colleges, 12 research stations across 3 agro-ecological zones of Bihar, and has 500+ researchers and faculty with a 1:2.4 faculty-to-student ratio. It is a fully residential university — all students must reside in campus hostels.

Admission Process
UG Admission: Apply via BCECE Board (bceceboard.bihar.gov.in) for 85% seats. ICAR AIEEA-UG (icar.nta.ac.in) for 15% national seats. BAU portal: bausabour.ac.in
Step 1 — Apply for BCECE / ICAR AIEEA-UG Entrance Exam
85% seats: Register and appear for BCECE (Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination) conducted by BCECE Board. 15% seats: Register for ICAR AIEEA-UG conducted by NTA at icar.nta.ac.in.
Step 2 — Appear in Entrance Examination
Appear in the BCECE or ICAR AIEEA-UG exam. Eligibility: 10+2 with PCB/Agriculture/Mathematics, min. 50% (General). NEET-style objective questions covering Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Agriculture.
Step 3 — Rank Card & Counselling
After result declaration, BCECE Board releases rank cards. Register for centralised counselling. Choose course preference: B.Sc. Agriculture or B.Sc. Horticulture and preferred college campus.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment & Document Verification
Allotted college is displayed in counselling portal. Carry original 10+2 marksheet, caste certificate, domicile proof, BCECE rank card. Documents are verified at the respective college.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allocation
Pay the admission fee as per category. Hostel room rent is FREE. Only mess charges (~₹2,985/semester) are applicable. Hostel allocation is mandatory — BAU is a fully residential university.
Step 6 — PG / PhD Admission (Separate Process)
PG: BAU conducts its own entrance-cum-academic performance test. Bihar domicile mandatory. Apply on bausabour.ac.in. PhD: BAU PAT (PhD Admission Test) + Interview; ICAR-JRF holders get preference.

Career Pathways

BAU graduates are well-regarded across agri-business, government services and research. The most popular career path is BPSC (Bihar Public Service Commission) Agricultural Officer — a highly sought route for BAU graduates. Other avenues include UPSC combined agriculture services, banking (Agriculture Officer exam at SBI/PNB/NABARD), ICAR research positions, State/Central Govt. extension services, agri-input companies (IFFCO, Tata Rallis) and food technology firms. PG and PhD graduates primarily go into research, teaching, and advanced agri-business roles.
