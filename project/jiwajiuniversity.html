About Jiwaji University, Gwalior

Jiwaji University, established in 1964 in the historic city of Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, is named after the legendary Maharaja Jiwaji Rao Scindia of the Scindia dynasty, rulers of the majestic Gwalior Fort. The university is one of the largest and most prominent state universities in northern Madhya Pradesh, affiliating over 450+ colleges across the Gwalior–Chambal region of M.P., serving more than 3 lakh students. Accredited by NAAC with A Grade, the university is spread across a magnificent ~550-acre campus — one of the largest university campuses in central India — and houses 30+ departments offering programs in Science, Commerce, Arts, Law, Engineering, Management, Computer Science, Pharmacy, Education, and Social Sciences. Jiwaji University is particularly renowned for its School of Studies in Chemistry (which has produced internationally recognized research), its Institute of Management Studies (IMS), and its strong Science and Commerce departments. The university serves as the primary higher education hub for the economically significant Gwalior–Chambal belt — a region with growing industry in manufacturing, defence, IT, and pharmaceuticals — and plays a crucial role in the educational development of northern Madhya Pradesh and Bundelkhand region.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear for Relevant Entrance Exam
Engineering (B.E./B.Tech via affiliated colleges): JEE Main / MP DTE. PG Engineering (M.Tech): GATE. Science/Commerce/Arts UG-PG: Jiwaji University CET (JU CET) or merit-based on 12th/UG marks. MBA: CAT / MAT / CMAT / JU CET. LLB/LLM: CLAT / Merit. PhD: Jiwaji University RET or qualify UGC-NET/GATE (exempted from written test). B.Ed: MP B.Ed CET.
Step 2 — Register on Jiwaji University / MP DTE Portal
For Engineering programs: register on the MP DTE online portal (dte.mponline.gov.in). For all other programs at Jiwaji University constituent departments: register on the official JU admission portal (jiwaji.edu). Fill all personal, academic, and entrance details and upload required documents within the announced application window.
Step 3 — Fill Department / College Preferences
Choose department preferences in the JU online admission portal. Seat allotment is based on entrance rank / merit, category (General/OBC/SC/ST/EWS), and available seats. Multiple counselling rounds are held both online and at the university's admission section.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Report to the university/college for physical document verification after seat allotment. Carry originals of 10th and 12th marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate, M.P. domicile certificate, income certificate (for scholarship), and passport-size photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay tuition fees online or at the university as per the allotment letter. SC/ST students of M.P. domicile are eligible for full fee reimbursement under state scholarship schemes. Complete enrollment and receive your student ID card and academic schedule.
Step 6 — JU Enrollment Number & Exam Registration
Upon enrollment, students receive a Jiwaji University enrollment number for semester exam registration, admit card generation, result access, and degree issuance. Register for each semester's examination on the JU exam portal promptly to avoid academic penalties.

Campus Life at Jiwaji University
🎉 Events & Activities
🎭
Srijan – Annual Cultural & Technical Festival — Jiwaji University's flagship annual fest featuring cultural performances, technical competitions, classical and folk music events (drawing on the Gwalior Gharana tradition), drama, and inter-college competitions across the Gwalior–Chambal region
🎵
Tansen Samaroh (Music Festival) — Held annually in Gwalior in memory of the legendary musician Tansen (Akbar's court musician from Gwalior), the university celebrates this heritage through music performances, student recitals, and cultural programs connecting students to the city's extraordinary musical legacy
🏆
Youth Festival (Yuva Utsav) — Inter-university cultural festival under AIU guidelines with competitions in classical music, dance, drama, fine arts, and literary events. Jiwaji teams consistently perform strongly in music categories given the Gwalior Gharana heritage
🔬
JU Research Symposium & Science Exhibition — Annual research showcase where PhD scholars and faculty present research papers, science models, and innovation projects. The Chemistry department's exhibitions are particularly acclaimed
💡
Startup & Entrepreneurship Summit — Annual event organized by JU's innovation and incubation cell with participation from Gwalior-based entrepreneurs, MSME leaders, and startup founders, aimed at connecting students with the region's growing industrial ecosystem
