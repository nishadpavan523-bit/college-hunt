About LUVAS

Lala Lajpat Rai University of Veterinary and Animal Sciences (LUVAS) was established on 1 December 2010 by the Government of Haryana under Haryana Act No. 7 of 2010. The university was carved out of the College of Veterinary Sciences of CCS Haryana Agricultural University, Hisar. It is one of India's specialized veterinary universities and serves as a major centre for veterinary education, livestock research, animal healthcare, dairy technology, and extension services. Recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and the Veterinary Council of India (VCI), LUVAS aims to provide quality education in Veterinary Sciences, Animal Husbandry, Dairy Sciences, Fisheries Sciences, and allied fields while supporting the livestock sector of Haryana and India. The Chancellor of the university is the Governor of Haryana.

Admission Process — BVSc & AH
Step 1 — Appear in NEET-UG
Qualify the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET UG). This is the mandatory gateway for BVSc & AH admission at LUVAS and all other veterinary colleges in India.
Step 2 — Participate in Haryana State Counselling
Register on the Haryana State Veterinary Counselling portal with your NEET UG scorecard, personal details, category certificate, and required documents. Fill college and course preferences.
Step 3 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is done based on NEET UG merit rank, category, Haryana domicile status, and seat availability. Multiple counselling rounds are held until seats are filled.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, NEET UG scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, ID proof, passport photographs, and gap certificate (if applicable).
Step 5 — Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed tuition fee and confirm admission by reporting to LUVAS and completing all enrollment formalities within the stipulated deadline.
Admission Process — MVSc & PhD
Step 1 — Qualify ICAR Entrance Examination
Qualify ICAR AIEEA PG (for MVSc) or ICAR AICE-JRF/SRF PhD examination. These are national-level exams conducted by the Indian Council of Agricultural Research.
Step 2 — Submit Application Form
Fill the LUVAS application form online or offline with ICAR score, academic records, category certificate, and other required documents.
Step 3 — Counselling and Merit Preparation
Attend LUVAS counselling sessions. Merit list is prepared based on ICAR score, academic performance, and category. Seat allotment follows.
Step 4 — Verification of Documents
Submit all original documents including degree certificates, marksheets, ICAR scorecard, category documents, and ID proof for verification.
Step 5 — Fee Submission and Enrolment
Complete fee payment and formal enrolment. PhD scholars may also apply for ICAR / UGC fellowships at this stage.

Career Notes

LUVAS graduates are highly sought after in the government veterinary services of Haryana and other states. BVSc & AH graduates can register as veterinary practitioners and are eligible for recruitment by state animal husbandry departments, NDDB, ICAR, and defence animal services. The dairy technology stream has strong placement in dairy cooperatives and the private dairy sector. Research-oriented graduates pursue MVSc and PhD with ICAR fellowships, leading to academic and research careers. The university's strong ties with the Haryana livestock sector ensure steady employment for its graduates.
