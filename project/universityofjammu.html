About University of Jammu

The University of Jammu, commonly known as Jammu University (JU), is a State Public University established in 1969 and located in Jammu, Jammu & Kashmir. It holds the prestigious NAAC A+ Grade accreditation and is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). Ranked 51st among Universities and 87th Overall in NIRF 2025, it is also ranked 21st among State Public Universities in India. Spread across approximately 118 acres, the university comprises 40+ departments, constituent colleges, a Directorate of Distance Education, and multiple research centres. It offers a comprehensive range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across Engineering, Sciences, Commerce, Arts, Humanities, Law, and Management disciplines.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Appear in CUET-UG / CUET-PG (Where Applicable)
Appear for the Common University Entrance Test (CUET-UG for undergraduate programs, CUET-PG for postgraduate programs) conducted by NTA. For B.Tech, qualify JEE Main. For MBA, scores from CAT / MAT are also accepted as per institute norms.
Step 2 — Register on Jammu University Admission Portal
Visit the official Jammu University admissions portal and register using your valid email ID and mobile number. Keep your CUET / JEE Main application number and scorecard details ready before beginning the registration process.
Step 3 — Fill Online Application Form
Complete the online application form carefully, selecting your preferred program and specialization. Ensure all academic details, personal information, and category details are filled accurately as per your official documents.
Step 4 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of all required documents including 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates, entrance exam scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, and passport-size photographs in the prescribed format and size.
Step 5 — Merit List / Counselling
Jammu University releases merit lists based on CUET / JEE Main scores and category-wise eligibility. Candidates are called for counselling in order of merit. Check the official university website regularly for merit list announcements and counselling schedules.
Step 6 — Document Verification
Report to Jammu University for physical verification of original documents: 10th and 12th marksheets and certificates, entrance exam admit card and scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate, and migration certificate.
Step 7 — Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed program fees (tuition, hostel, mess, and other charges) as per the Jammu University fee schedule. Complete enrollment formalities, submit all required documents, and confirm your admission for the academic year.

Placement Notes

Jammu University has a dedicated Training & Placement Cell that conducts regular job fairs, campus recruitment drives, seminars, workshops, and industry interactions. According to NIRF placement data, the PG median package stands at ₹6.78 LPA and the UG 5-year median package is ₹6.15 LPA. MBA, MCA, Law, and Professional Programs lead in placement strength. Students receive opportunities across Banking, IT, Education, and Corporate sectors, with internship support facilitated throughout the academic year.
