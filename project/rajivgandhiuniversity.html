About RGU

Rajiv Gandhi University, formerly known as Arunachal University, was established in 1984 and is a prestigious Central University nestled in the scenic hills of Arunachal Pradesh. Accredited with NAAC Grade A, RGU spans an expansive 302-acre green campus at Rono Hills, Doimukh. The university is affiliated with UGC, AICTE, NCTE and BCI, offering a wide range of UG, PG, Diploma, Integrated and PhD programs. RGU is particularly known for its research in tribal studies, biodiversity and environmental science — fields uniquely enriched by its North-East location. The university offers affordable Central University education with scholarships available for eligible students.

Admission Process
UG & PG Admission: Apply via CUET Portal (cuet.nta.nic.in) for most programs. B.Tech also accepts JEE Main. Official RGU portal: rgu.ac.in/admission
Step 1 — Register for CUET-UG / CUET-PG
Register on the NTA CUET portal (cuet.nta.nic.in). For B.Tech, you may also register for JEE Main. B.Ed applicants register for CUET-UG or NCET. Fill in subject preferences as required by the programme.
Step 2 — Appear in the Entrance Examination
Appear in the CUET-UG / CUET-PG / JEE Main exam as applicable. CUET tests cover domain subjects, general aptitude and language. Check the syllabus for the specific subjects corresponding to your chosen program.
Step 3 — Check RGU Admission Notifications
Regularly monitor rgu.ac.in and the RGU Admissions Portal for merit lists, cutoffs and counselling schedules. Students should also monitor CUET counselling portals for centralised allotments.
Step 4 — Counselling & Document Verification
Attend counselling as per the schedule announced by RGU. Carry original mark sheets, category certificate (if applicable), domicile proof and CUET scorecard. Documents are verified at the university.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allocation
Pay the admission fee as per category and programme. Apply for hostel accommodation separately. Boys' and girls' hostels are available on campus. Hostel allotment is subject to availability.
Step 6 — PhD Admission (Separate Process)
PhD: Admission through NET/JRF/GATE score or university entrance examination. Shortlisted candidates are called for an interview and research proposal presentation. NET-JRF qualified candidates get preference for fellowships.

Career Pathways

RGU graduates are well-suited for UPSC, NET/JRF and state government services. The calm academic environment is especially beneficial for students preparing for competitive examinations. B.Tech graduates pursue careers in IT, software and government engineering roles. Law graduates appear for judicial services and bar examinations. RGU is particularly strong in tribal studies, biodiversity and environmental science research, giving PG and PhD graduates a unique edge in North-East India. Participation in internships, workshops and seminars is encouraged to improve career opportunities.
