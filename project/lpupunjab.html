About Lovely Professional University

Lovely Professional University (LPU) is one of India's largest private universities, established in 2005 in Phagwara, Punjab. Accredited with NAAC A++, it offers programs in Engineering, Management, Agriculture, Pharmacy, Design, Law, Hotel Management, Sciences, Arts, and PhD research. Spread across a 600+ acre residential campus, LPU is known for its world-class infrastructure including a shopping mall, food courts, sports stadiums, and innovation centres. With 2200+ recruiters and a highest international package of ₹2.5 Crore, LPU consistently ranks among the top private universities in India.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply Online
Apply at lpu.in. Register for LPUNEST or submit JEE Main / CUET scores. Engineering requires PCM in 10+2 with min 45–50% marks.
Step 2 — LPUNEST / Entrance Score
LPUNEST (LPU National Entrance & Scholarship Test) is conducted throughout the year. JEE Main and CUET scores are also accepted for B.Tech and other UG programs.
Step 3 — Merit List & Offer Letter
Merit lists are published online. Selected students receive an offer letter. Scholarships are awarded based on LPUNEST / JEE / CUET performance.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit originals: 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate (if applicable), ID proof, and photographs at the time of reporting.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester fees online via the LPU admission portal. Complete hostel and ID card registration to begin the academic session.
