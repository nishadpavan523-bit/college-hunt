About MBSPSU

Maharaja Bhupinder Singh Punjab Sports University (MBSPSU) is Punjab's first dedicated Sports University, established in 2019 under Punjab Act No. 11 of 2019. Located at Mohindra Kothi, Near Fountain Chowk, Patiala — Punjab 147001, the university is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, and is a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU). It was established to promote excellence in Sports Education, Sports Sciences, Coaching, Sports Management, Yoga, and Athlete Development. The university also functions as a specialized institution for training, research, and higher education in sports-related disciplines, and is empowered to regulate sports and physical education institutions across Punjab.

General Admission Process
Step 1 — Visit the University Admission Portal
Go to the official MBSPSU admissions portal at mbspsu.ac.in and review available programs and eligibility criteria.
Step 2 — Fill Online Application Form
Complete the online application form with accurate personal, academic, and sports achievement details.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of marksheets, certificates, sports achievement records, category certificate (if applicable), and a valid photo ID.
Step 4 — Entrance / Physical Fitness Test (if applicable)
Appear for the entrance test or physical fitness assessment as required for BPES and other sports-specific programs.
Step 5 — Merit List Publication
The university publishes the merit list based on academic performance and sports achievements. Check your status on the portal.
Step 6 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Report for physical document verification. Pay the required semester / annual fees to confirm your admission.
