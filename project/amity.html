About Amity University

Amity University is one of India's leading private university groups, established in 2005 with its flagship campus in Noida, Uttar Pradesh. The university is NAAC A+ accredited and UGC approved, offering UG, PG, Diploma and PhD programmes across engineering, management, law, medical sciences, journalism, architecture, design, sciences and humanities. With over 12 campuses across India and international campuses in Dubai, London, Singapore, New York, Abu Dhabi, Mauritius and Tashkent, Amity has a truly global presence. The university houses multiple schools including the School of Engineering & Technology, School of Management, School of Law and School of Medical & Allied Sciences. Amity's strong industry connections, research centres and innovation hubs have established it as a premier destination for higher education in India.

Admission Process
📅 Apply Online: Visit amity.edu for current admission schedules and deadlines
Step 1 — Apply Online
Visit amity.edu and register on the official admissions portal. Fill in personal details, academic qualifications and select preferred campus and programme.
Step 2 — Submit Academic Documents
Upload mark sheets, certificates and other required academic records as per the programme applied for.
Step 3 — Entrance Exam / Score Submission
B.Tech: JEE Main score considered. MBA: CAT/MAT/XAT/GMAT scores required. Other programmes: Merit-based or internal entrance test. PhD: Entrance examination conducted by Amity.
Step 4 — Interview / Counselling
Shortlisted candidates attend personal interview or counselling. Management and law programmes may require Group Discussion (GD).
Step 5 — Merit List & Offer Letter
Final selection based on qualifying exam scores, academic merit and interview performance. Offer letter issued to selected candidates.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay fees online to confirm your seat. Complete document verification and report to campus as per schedule to complete enrolment.
