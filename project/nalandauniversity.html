About Nalanda University

Nalanda University is the revival of one of the greatest educational institutions in human history, originally flourishing from ~427 CE to ~1200 CE — almost 800 years of continuous operation. At its ancient peak, it hosted 10,000 students and 2,000 teachers from China, Korea, Japan, Sri Lanka, Tibet, Greece, Persia and Turkey. The modern revival was proposed at the 2007 East Asia Summit, supported by 18 nations, and enacted by the Indian Parliament in 2010. Operations began in 2014, with the new permanent eco-friendly campus at Rajgir inaugurated on 19 June 2024 by PM Narendra Modi. Recognised by UGC and an Institution of National Importance, the university offers exclusively PG and PhD programmes across 5 Schools and 2 Centres, with students from 24 countries (18% international student ratio).

Admission Process (2026)
📅 Admission 2026 Open: Last date 15 June 2026 for PG & PhD programmes · Apply at nalandauniv.edu.in
Step 1 — Register on the Official Admissions Portal
Visit nalandauniv.edu.in → Admissions section. Register online with email and mobile number. Fill the application form with personal details, academic qualifications and course preference.
Step 2 — Upload SOP & Academic Documents
Submit a Statement of Purpose (SOP) explaining why Nalanda, your research interests and career goals. Upload all academic records. PhD applicants must also submit a Research Proposal (2,000–3,000 words).
Step 3 — Submit Entrance Scores
For MBA: submit valid CAT/XAT/MAT/GMAT/CUET-PG score (min. 70 percentile required). For M.A./M.Sc.: submit CUET-PG (NTA) score. PhD applicants: UGC-NET/JRF/CSIR-NET qualified candidates are given shortlisting preference.
Step 4 — Pay Application Fee
Pay the application fee online as notified on nalandauniv.edu.in. Keep payment confirmation for further stages.
Step 5 — Personal Interview
Shortlisted candidates are called for a Personal Interview conducted in-person at the Rajgir campus or online. PhD interviews are conducted by the School faculty committee.
Step 6 — Final Merit List & Seat Confirmation
Final selection based on CUET-PG/CAT score + SOP + Interview. (PhD: Academic Record 30% + Research Proposal 40% + Interview 30%). Pay fees online to confirm enrolment. Fully residential — hostel accommodation is mandatory.

Placement Notes

Nalanda University's placement record is most notable for its MBA in Sustainable Development & Management programme. In 2022, 100% of MBA graduates were placed with a highest package of ₹16 LPA and an average of ₹8–10 LPA. Roles are concentrated in sustainability, CSR, EHS, green finance, energy consulting, agribusiness, oil & gas, IT, and textiles. PG and PhD graduates in Buddhist Studies, History, Languages, and Ecology find opportunities primarily in academia, research institutions, international cultural organisations, NGOs, and diplomacy. The university's global reputation and international student base attract recruiters seeking candidates with cross-cultural competence and interdisciplinary expertise.
