About IIT (BHU) Varanasi

Indian Institute of Technology (BHU) Varanasi is one of India's premier technical institutions, originally founded in 1919 as Banaras Engineering College and granted IIT status in 2012. Located within the sprawling Banaras Hindu University campus in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, it spans approximately 1300 acres. IIT (BHU) offers a comprehensive range of programs including B.Tech, Integrated Dual Degree, M.Tech, M.Pharm, M.Sc., and Ph.D. across departments of Engineering, Technology, Science, Humanities, and Interdisciplinary Studies. It ranks among the top IITs in India for engineering and research excellence.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
Register for JEE Advanced (B.Tech), GATE (M.Tech), IIT JAM (M.Sc.), or GPAT (M.Pharm) as applicable. Deadlines typically Nov–Feb each year.
Step 2 — Appear for Exam
JEE Advanced conducted by IITs on rotation. GATE / IIT JAM conducted by NTA/IITs. Scores are valid for counselling in the same year.
Step 3 — Counselling
B.Tech via JoSAA counselling. M.Tech via COAP. M.Sc. via CCMN. Register on IIT BHU admission portal for PhD.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Upload and submit originals: 10th, 12th marksheets, category certificate (if applicable), ID proof, and photographs.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay semester fees online via university portal and complete enrolment formalities to confirm your seat.

Placement Notes

IIT (BHU) Varanasi has an exceptional placement record with over 250+ companies visiting campus annually. The highest international package has crossed ₹1.68 Crore. The CSE branch consistently achieves average packages of ₹35–45 LPA with near 100% placement. Core engineering branches also attract top recruiters from manufacturing, infrastructure, and research sectors. Strong internship-to-PPO conversion ensures most students receive pre-placement offers.
