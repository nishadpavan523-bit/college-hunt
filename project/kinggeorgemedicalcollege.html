About KGMU

King George's Medical University (KGMU), Lucknow is one of India's oldest and most prestigious medical institutions, established in 1905. Formerly known as King George's Medical College (KGMC), it is a State Medical University located in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, accredited with NAAC "A+" Grade. KGMU is recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC), DCI, and INC along with other professional councils. Spread across a 100+ acre urban medical campus, KGMU houses faculties of Medicine, Surgery, Dental Sciences, Nursing, and Allied Health Sciences along with advanced Super Specialty departments covering Cardiology, Neurology, Oncology, Nephrology, and more. The university operates a multi-specialty teaching hospital with thousands of beds, making it a premier destination for medical education and clinical training in North India.

Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Register for NEET-UG
Appear for NEET-UG conducted by NTA. MBBS and BDS admissions are entirely NEET-dependent. Allied Health and Nursing programs may have separate entrance tests.
Step 2 — Participate in Counselling Process
Register for MCC (for MBBS/BDS) or DGME UP State Counselling. Fill in your choice list and select KGMU Lucknow.
Step 3 — Seat Allotment
Seats are allotted based on NEET rank, category, and availability. Accept your allotted seat and report within the given deadline.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Medical Fitness
Submit 10th, 12th marksheets, NEET scorecard, category certificate, and ID proof. Undergo medical fitness examination as required.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Complete fee submission online and confirm enrolment. Collect your student ID and report to the respective faculty on the joining date.
Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Appear for NEET-PG / NEET-MDS / NEET-SS
Register and qualify the relevant PG entrance exam — NEET-PG for MD/MS, NEET-MDS for MDS, NEET-SS for DM/M.Ch programs.
Step 2 — DGME / MCC Counselling Registration
Register for counselling on the official DGME Uttar Pradesh or MCC portal. Select KGMU during preference filling.
Step 3 — Seat Allotment & Reporting
Confirm allotted seat and report to KGMU within the stipulated deadline with all original documents.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Submission
Submit MBBS/BDS degree, internship completion certificate, NEET scorecard, and category documents. Complete online fee payment.

Career Pathways

KGMU graduates are highly sought after across India's healthcare sector. MBBS graduates pursue residency programs, higher specializations (MD/MS), or join government medical services via UPSC/state PSC examinations. PG and Super Specialty graduates join AIIMS, leading private hospital groups like Apollo, Fortis, and Max Healthcare, or take up academic positions. Nursing and Allied Health graduates are placed in leading hospitals, diagnostic centres, and community health programs. The university's internship stipend follows UP Government norms and clinical exposure through its massive teaching hospital gives graduates a significant edge in competitive national examinations and hospital placements.
