About University of Lucknow

University of Lucknow, established in 1921, is one of India's oldest and most prestigious state public universities, located in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Accredited with NAAC "A++" Grade, the university is recognized by the UGC and Government of Uttar Pradesh. It serves as an academic hub with more than 500 affiliated colleges across Uttar Pradesh. The university offers comprehensive programs spanning Arts, Science, Commerce, Management, Law, Engineering, Computer Science, Fine Arts, and Research Studies at undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, certificate, and PhD levels. It is a premier destination for students seeking quality education with a rich intellectual heritage and strong academic excellence.

Undergraduate Admission Process
Step 1 — Register Online
Register through CUET-UG portal or university entrance portal and create your applicant account.
Step 2 — Fill Application Form
Fill the Lucknow University application form and upload academic documents and category certificate.
Step 3 — Appear for Entrance Exam
Appear for CUET-UG, JEE Main, or relevant entrance exam as per your chosen course.
Step 4 — Counselling & Seat Allotment
Participate in university counselling process and get seat allotment based on merit and preferences.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit original 10th, 12th marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate, and ID proof. Complete fee payment.
Postgraduate Admission Process
Step 1 — Register on Portal
Register on the university entrance portal or CUET-PG and create your application account.
Step 2 — Complete Application
Fill application form, select course, and upload degree certificate, marksheets, and category documents.
Step 3 — Submit National Test Score
Submit GATE (M.Tech), CAT/MAT (MBA), UGC-NET score, or appear for university entrance test.
Step 4 — Merit List & Interview
Participate in personal interview round after merit list declaration.
Step 5 — Final Enrollment
Document verification, fee payment, and enrollment completion for confirmed admission.

Placement Notes

University of Lucknow has a strong placement record across its diverse faculties. B.Tech and BCA graduates are recruited by leading IT companies including TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL, and Deloitte. MBA and M.Com graduates find opportunities in banking, management consulting, and financial services. The university's extensive network of 500+ affiliated colleges enables a wide reach in placement activities. The Training & Placement Cell coordinates with companies for campus recruitment. Graduates also pursue government service exams and academic research positions. The university's location in Lucknow provides access to multiple corporate hubs in North India.
