About University of Mysore

The University of Mysore, established in 1916 by the erstwhile Mysore Maharaja Krishnaraja Wadiyar IV, is the sixth oldest university in India and the oldest in Karnataka. Located on the picturesque Manasagangotri campus spanning ~700 acres in Mysuru — the cultural capital of Karnataka — it is one of India's most scenic and heritage-rich universities. Accredited with NAAC A+ Grade and recognized as a UGC Category-I University, UoM offers a comprehensive range of programs across Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Education, Engineering, and Management. It houses 43 postgraduate departments and serves as the affiliating university for over 200 colleges across the Mysuru, Mandya, Chamarajanagar, and Kodagu districts. The university is celebrated for its vibrant research culture, rich cultural heritage, and deep academic tradition in humanities and sciences.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register at UoM Admissions Portal
Visit the official University of Mysore admissions website (uni-mysore.ac.in). Create an account and select the program — UG, PG, LLB, MBA, MCA, or PhD. Note that PG admissions are largely through the PGCET (Karnataka State) while PhD uses the University's own Research Entrance Test (RET).
Step 2 — Ensure Valid Entrance Score / Eligibility
PG programs require a valid PGCET (Karnataka Postgraduate Common Entrance Test) score. MBA requires PGCET / KMAT / CAT. Law programs require KLEE. For Science PG, IIT JAM scores may also be accepted. For PhD, candidates need a valid UGC-NET / SLET or must appear in the UoM Research Entrance Test (RET).
Step 3 — Fill Application & Upload Documents
Complete the online application with academic transcripts, entrance exam scorecards, category certificate (SC/ST/OBC), domicile certificate, ID proof, recent photograph, and relevant experience certificate (for MBA/PhD). Check document requirements specific to each department.
Step 4 — PGCET Counselling / Departmental Selection
PG admissions primarily occur through centralized KEA-PGCET counselling for Karnataka candidates. Seats are allotted based on PGCET rank, category, and preference choices. Some departments conduct their own additional written tests and interviews, especially for specialized programs like MSc Biotechnology.
Step 5 — Research Entrance Test + Interview (PhD)
PhD candidates who do not hold UGC-NET/SLET appear in UoM's Research Entrance Test (RET). Shortlisted candidates then attend an interview before the Doctoral Committee of the respective department. The research proposal, subject knowledge, and research aptitude are assessed.
Step 6 — Seat Acceptance, Document Verification & Enrollment
Accept the allotted seat online within the stipulated time. Visit the university/department for document verification with originals — degrees, marksheets, entrance scorecard, category and domicile certificate. Pay tuition and hostel fees and complete enrollment for the academic term (typically July–August).
