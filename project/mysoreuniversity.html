About University of Mysore

University of Mysore (UoM), established in 1916, is one of the oldest and most prestigious universities in India, situated in the culturally rich city of Mysuru, Karnataka. It is a State University recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and accredited with NAAC A+ grade. Spread over a magnificent 750-acre campus that includes the stunning Manasagangothri campus with iconic Gothic architecture, it is home to 40+ postgraduate departments and research centres spanning Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Management, and Education. The university has over 550 affiliated colleges across 4 districts of Karnataka. UoM is celebrated for its heritage, academic excellence, and contribution to regional and national education for over a century.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Check Eligibility & Notification
Check the official UoM website for admission notifications for the desired UG / PG / PhD program and confirm eligibility criteria.
Step 2 — Register Online
Register on the UoM Admission Portal (admission.uni-mysore.ac.in) or Karnataka PGCET / UGCET portal for respective programs.
Step 3 — Appear in Entrance Exam
Appear in Karnataka PGCET (for PG), UGCET (for UG professional courses), CLAT/KLEE (for Law), or UoM Department Entrance Test as applicable.
Step 4 — Merit List & Counselling
Download the merit list / counselling schedule from the UoM website and participate in online / offline counselling for seat allotment.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th, 12th, UG marksheets/degree, caste certificate (if applicable), ID proof, and passport photographs.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay the prescribed fee online/offline and complete the enrollment process at the respective department to confirm admission.
