About SRM University Delhi-NCR

SRM University Delhi-NCR (formerly SRM University Haryana) is a private state university established in 2013 at Rajiv Gandhi Education City, Sonepat, Haryana. The university holds a prestigious NAAC Grade A+ accreditation and is approved by UGC, BCI, NCHMCT, DSIR, and AIU. Spread over a 47.89-acre campus, it offers programmes across Engineering & Technology, Management, Law, Science, and Humanities. With 925+ companies visiting for placements and a highest package of ₹50 LPA, SRM Delhi-NCR is among the leading private universities in the NCR region.

Admission Process
UG Admission: Apply via SRMHCAT (SRM's own entrance), JEE Main / CLAT / CUET or merit. Official portal: srmuniversity.ac.in
Step 1 — Register for Entrance Exam
For B.Tech: Register for SRMJEEE (SRM's own exam) or use JEE Main scores. For Management/Law: CUET / CLAT / LSAT / AILET or SRMHCAT. For B.Sc./BA/B.Com: Merit / CUET. Apply at srmuniversity.ac.in.
Step 2 — Appear in Entrance Examination
Appear in the relevant entrance exam. Eligibility: 10+2 with minimum 50% in relevant subjects. For B.Tech: PCM required. For Law: 10+2 from any recognised board.
Step 3 — Counselling & Course Selection
SRM publishes merit lists / rank cards. Participate in online counselling to select your programme and specialization. Multiple rounds may be held for vacant seats.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment & Document Verification
Allotted programme displayed on portal. Carry 10+2 marksheet, identity proof, category certificate if applicable, and entrance exam scorecard for document verification.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Booking
Pay the admission fee as per programme and category. Hostel booking is optional. Single room: ~₹3,35,000; Double sharing: ~₹2,25,000. Mess charges are extra as per actual.
Step 6 — PG / PhD Admission (Separate Process)
PG: Apply with GATE / CAT / XAT / CUET-PG scores or SRMHCAT. MBA requires personal interview. PhD: SRMHCAT entrance test (70%) + interview (30%); UGC-NET / GATE / CSIR-NET holders may be preferred.

Career Pathways

SRM Delhi-NCR graduates are well-placed across IT, banking, consulting, and management sectors. B.Tech graduates are predominantly recruited by top IT and tech companies like Amazon, Birlasoft, and Mindtree. MBA graduates are absorbed by consulting and banking majors like Deloitte, HDFC Bank, and Axis Bank. Law graduates pursue litigation, corporate law, and legal advisory roles. With 1,400+ paid internship opportunities and 925+ visiting companies, students get extensive industry exposure from the second year onward.
