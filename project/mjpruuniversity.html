About MJPRU Bareilly

Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Rohilkhand University (MJPRU), formerly known as Rohilkhand University, was established in 1975 in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh. It is one of the largest and most prominent affiliating universities in UP, overseeing around 587 affiliated colleges and serving more than 5 lakh students. The university is accredited with the prestigious NAAC A++ Grade, the highest accreditation in India, and is recognised by UGC as a Category-1 University. MJPRU offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral and diploma programmes across faculties including Engineering & Technology, Pharmacy, Management, Science, Arts, Education, Law and Social Sciences. It is ranked by NIRF and is recognised for its strong research activities through IQAC and dedicated research centres.

Admission Process
📅 Admissions Open: Apply online at mjpru.ac.in · Admissions conducted annually for UG, PG and PhD programmes
Step 1 — Register on the Official Admissions Portal
Visit mjpru.ac.in and navigate to the Admissions section. Register with a valid email ID and mobile number. Select your desired programme and fill in personal and academic details.
Step 2 — Fill the Application Form & Upload Documents
Complete the application form with academic qualifications, category details and course preference. Upload scanned copies of marksheets, certificates, photograph and signature.
Step 3 — Submit Entrance / Merit Scores
For B.Tech/M.Tech: submit UPSEE/JEE/GATE scores. For MBA/MCA: submit CAT/MAT/CUET-PG scores. For B.Sc./B.A./B.Com./M.A./M.Sc./M.Com.: admission is merit-based on qualifying exam marks. For PhD: MJPRU PhD Entrance Test (UGC-NET/JRF qualified may be exempted).
Step 4 — Pay Application Fee
Pay the non-refundable application fee online through the portal. Fee varies by programme. Keep the payment receipt for future reference.
Step 5 — Counselling / Interview
Engineering and professional programme admissions require state-level counselling (UPSAC/AKTU counselling). PhD admissions involve a written test followed by a personal interview conducted by the departmental committee.
Step 6 — Merit List & Seat Confirmation
Final merit list is published on the university portal. Shortlisted candidates must pay the programme fee within the stipulated time to confirm their seat. Hostel allotment is done separately.

Placement Notes

MJPRU's Training & Placement Cell actively conducts skill development programmes, mock interviews, aptitude training and industry visits. Placements are strongest in the Engineering and Pharmacy departments, with leading companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro and IBM visiting campus regularly. Management graduates find opportunities in banking, finance, consulting and marketing. The university also facilitates internship opportunities for students across departments. Students from Arts, Science and Commerce streams primarily move toward government jobs, higher education (PG/PhD) or competitive examinations.
