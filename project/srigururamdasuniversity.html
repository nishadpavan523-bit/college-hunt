About SGRDUHS

Shri Guru Ram Das University of Health Sciences is a leading private health sciences university established in 2016 in Amritsar, Punjab. The university is recognized by UGC (University Grants Commission) and accredited by all major regulatory bodies including National Medical Commission (NMC), Dental Council of India (DCI), Indian Nursing Council (INC), Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and other allied health regulatory authorities. SGRDUHS offers comprehensive education in Medical Sciences, Dental Sciences, Nursing, Physiotherapy, Paramedical Sciences and Allied Health Sciences. The university runs multiple undergraduate, postgraduate, super-specialty, diploma and doctoral programs with a strong focus on clinical practice, research and community healthcare. The campus is strategically located at Vallah, Mehta Road in Amritsar with modern facilities for medical education and training.

Admission Process
📋 Multi-Tier Admission Process: SGRDUHS follows NEET-based admission for UG (MBBS) and NEET-PG for postgraduate programs. Allied health courses follow merit-based selection. All admissions are processed through official university portal at sgruhs.edu.in
Step 1 — Register & Apply for NEET Exam
For UG MBBS: Register for NEET-UG exam conducted by NTA (neet.nta.ac.in). For PG programs: Register for NEET-PG conducted by NTA. Create account on official SGRDUHS admission portal at sgruhs.edu.in
Step 2 — Appear in NEET Entrance Exam
NEET-UG: Offline exam covering Biology (Physics, Chemistry, Zoology & Botany), English from 10+2 syllabus. NEET-PG: For postgraduate medical specializations. Allied health courses: Merit-based selection from 10+2 documents.
Step 3 — Rank Declaration & Counselling
NTA releases NEET result and all India merit list. Register on SGRDUHS admission portal. Participate in All India Counselling for government/private seats. State-wise counselling for reserved categories.
Step 4 — College Choice & Seat Allotment
Choose program preference (MBBS, B.Sc. Nursing, BPT, Allied Health courses) and campus location. Seat allotment displayed in portal based on merit and choice order.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Report to allotted college with original documents: NEET admit card, score card, 10+2 marksheet, caste certificate (if applicable), domicile proof, passport-sized photographs, and identity proof.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allocation
Pay admission and tuition fees as per category through SGRDUHS portal. Hostel allocation (separate boys/girls) based on availability. Complete hostel registration for on-campus accommodation.
