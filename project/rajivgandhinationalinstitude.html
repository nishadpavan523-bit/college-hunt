About RGNUL

Rajiv Gandhi National University of Law (RGNUL), Punjab is a premier National Law University established in 2006, located on Sidhuwal–Bhadson Road, Patiala, Punjab. Recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and approved by the Bar Council of India (BCI), and accredited with NAAC "A" Grade, RGNUL is among India's leading institutions for legal education and research. Spread across a fully residential campus of approximately 50 acres, the university specializes in Constitutional Law, Corporate Law, Criminal Law, Intellectual Property Law, International Law, and Public Policy. RGNUL offers UG, PG, Ph.D., PG Diploma, and Certificate Programs in law and related disciplines.

Admission Process – B.A. LL.B. (Hons.)
Step 1 — Register for CLAT UG
Register on the official CLAT Consortium portal (consortiumofnlus.ac.in) and complete the application form with required details and fee.
Step 2 — Appear in CLAT Examination
Appear for the Common Law Admission Test (CLAT) UG conducted by the Consortium of NLUs. The exam tests English, GK, Legal Reasoning, Logical Reasoning, and Quantitative Techniques.
Step 3 — Participate in CLAT Counselling
Based on your CLAT score, participate in the centralised CLAT Counselling process managed by the Consortium of NLUs and indicate your preference for RGNUL.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment through CLAT Consortium
Seats are allotted based on merit, category, and preference. Accept the allotted seat and pay the seat acceptance fee within the stipulated time.
Step 5 — Document Verification & Fee Payment
Report to RGNUL for physical document verification. Submit 10th and 12th marksheets, CLAT scorecard, category certificate, ID proof, and photographs. Complete fee payment and enrolment formalities.
Admission Process – LL.M.
Step 1 — Register for CLAT PG
Register on the CLAT Consortium portal for the CLAT PG examination. Ensure your LL.B. degree details are correctly submitted.
Step 2 — Appear in CLAT PG
Appear for the CLAT PG exam, which tests Constitutional Law, Jurisprudence, and other core law subjects.
Step 3 — Counselling & Seat Allotment
Participate in CLAT PG counselling. Seat allotment is done based on CLAT PG merit and category. Accept the allotment for RGNUL.
Step 4 — Verification & Fee Payment
Complete document verification with LL.B. degree, marksheets, CLAT PG scorecard, and category certificates. Pay the requisite fee and complete enrolment.

RGNUL's dedicated Placement Cell facilitates campus recruitment by leading law firms, corporate legal departments, banks, and consulting organisations. Being an NLU, RGNUL graduates are highly sought after by India's top-tier law firms for corporate, litigation, and advisory roles. The university's strong alumni network and emphasis on moot court training, legal research, and internships significantly enhance employability across the legal and policy ecosystem.
