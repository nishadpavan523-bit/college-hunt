About JGND PSOU

Jagat Guru Nanak Dev Punjab State Open University (JGND PSOU) is the first State Open University of Punjab, established in 2019 under Punjab State Legislature Act No. 19 of 2019. Recognised by the University Grants Commission under Sections 2(f) and 12(B), the university was founded to provide accessible, flexible, and affordable higher education through Open and Distance Learning (ODL) and online modes. The university particularly focuses on reaching learners in rural areas, working professionals, women, differently-abled learners, and other underserved groups across Punjab and beyond.

Admission Process (UG / PG)
Step 1 — Visit the University Admission Portal
Navigate to the official admission portal at psou.ac.in and create your applicant account with a valid email and phone number.
Step 2 — Complete Online Registration
Fill in the online application form with your personal, academic, and category details as required.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of marksheets, degree certificates, identity proof (Aadhaar), passport photographs, and category certificates (if applicable).
Step 4 — Pay Admission Fees Online
Submit the applicable semester-wise admission fee through the online payment gateway on the portal.
Step 5 — Submit Application & Verification
Submit the completed application for university-side verification. Admission confirmation is issued upon successful document review.
Admission Process (PhD)
Step 1 — Apply Online
Register on the university's PhD admission portal and submit your application along with the research proposal.
Step 2 — Appear for Entrance Test
Appear for the university-conducted PhD Entrance Test. NET/JRF certificate holders may be eligible for relaxation.
Step 3 — Personal Interview
Attend the interview round conducted by the respective school's research committee.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Fee Submission
Submit Master's degree certificates, mark sheets, research proposal, category certificate, and NET/JRF certificate (if applicable). Pay the applicable research fees.
