About Eternal University

Eternal University, located in the serene hills of Baru Sahib, Distt. Sirmaur, Himachal Pradesh, was established in 2008. It is a UGC-recognized private university under Section 2(f) & 12B, spread across a scenic 450-acre campus. The university is primarily focused on girls/women students and follows the motto "World Peace through Value Based Education". It offers programs across engineering, agriculture, sciences, management, arts, nursing, and public health. The university is NAAC accredited and features a unique Gurudwara within its peaceful hill campus.

Admission Process — PG Courses
Step 1 — Fill Online Application
Visit the university portal and complete the online application form with required details.
Step 2 — Upload Documents
Upload academic certificates, ID proof, category certificate, and passport-size photograph.
Step 3 — Merit / Entrance-Based Selection
Selection via merit or accepted entrance scores: CAT / MAT / CMAT / JEE Main / HPCET as applicable.
Step 4 — Fee Payment
Pay the course fee online through the university portal to confirm your seat.
Step 5 — Admission Confirmation
Receive admission confirmation and report to the university for enrolment formalities.
Admission Process — PhD
Step 1 — Apply Online
Submit the PhD application form on the university website along with required documents.
Step 2 — Entrance Examination
Appear for the university-conducted entrance examination for PhD admission.
Step 3 — Research Proposal / Interview
Present a research proposal and attend a panel interview with departmental faculty.
Step 4 — Final Selection
Selected candidates receive an offer letter and complete the enrolment and fee payment process.

Placement Notes

The university's placement cell supports students in securing jobs across IT, ITES, agriculture, healthcare, and management sectors. The highest package recorded is approximately ₹6–7 LPA while the average stands around ₹3 LPA. Key recruiters include HP, PepsiCo, and ZTE. Students from PG and PhD programs also explore opportunities in research institutions, public health organizations, NGOs, and government bodies.
