About Sardar Patel University

Sardar Patel University (SPU), originally named Sardar Vallabhbhai Vidyapeeth, was established on October 30, 1955 in Vallabh Vidyanagar, Anand, Gujarat — a town that itself was founded by visionary educationist Bhaikaka (H. M. Patel). SPU is a State Public University recognized by the UGC and accredited with NAAC Grade A (CGPA 3.11). It is also ranked QS Asia 240 and Times Higher Education Impact Rankings 601–800.

SPU is one of Gujarat's oldest and most respected universities, offering over 200+ courses across 10 faculties and 27+ postgraduate departments. The university has 133 affiliated colleges and a constituent college. Its key academic strengths include Pharmaceutical Sciences, Materials Science, Chemistry, Biotechnology, Business Studies, Computer Applications, Law, and Social Work. SPU's Law faculty has ranked 27th nationally (NIRF) and its management faculty between 45–61 (NIRF). The university prioritizes affordable, quality education for the people of Gujarat with fees among the lowest in the state.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Determine the Relevant Entrance / Eligibility
Identify the admission route: Merit-based (BA/BSc/BCom), ACPC counselling (Engineering/BCA/MCA), CAT/CMAT (MBA), CLAT/University Test (LLB/BA LLB), SLET/NET (PhD), or GSBTC (B.Ed).
Step 2 — Apply Online via SPU Portal
Visit spu.ac.in to fill the online application form for the desired program. Upload scanned documents including marksheets, category certificate, photograph, and required examination scorecards within the specified deadline.
Step 3 — Gujarat ACPC Counselling (for MCA / Tech Programs)
For MCA and technology programs, participate in Gujarat ACPC (acpc.ac.in) counselling process. Fill preferences and lock choices; seat allotment is done based on merit rank and category over multiple rounds.
Step 4 — Merit List / Shortlisting
For merit-based programs (BA/BSc/BCom/BBA), the university publishes merit lists based on qualifying examination marks. Candidates are called for document verification in order of merit rank.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: School/college marksheets, entrance score (if applicable), category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD), domicile certificate, Aadhaar/ID proof, and passport photographs at the respective department or admission office.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay the prescribed (very affordable) semester/annual fees at the university / online and complete enrollment by reporting to the respective PG department or constituent college before the stipulated deadline.

Placement Notes

SPU's Career Development Centre facilitates placements through campus drives, industry interaction programmes, and skill development workshops. Placement numbers are moderate but growing — 72 students placed in 2024 with an average of ₹4.5 LPA. The university's core strengths lie in Pharmaceutical Sciences, Chemistry, Biotechnology, Law, and Management — sectors where Gujarat's industry ecosystem provides strong local opportunities. Many students also opt for competitive exams (UPSC, GPSC, SSC), government jobs, GSET/NET, or further studies, reflecting the broader academic mission of SPU. Its extremely affordable fees make it the go-to choice for quality government university education in central Gujarat.
