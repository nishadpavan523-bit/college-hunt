Admission Process
Step 1 – Visit the Official Admission Portal
Go to the official admission portal at lingayasvidyapeeth.edu.in to begin your application.
Step 2 – Fill the Online Application Form
Complete the online application form with all personal and academic details.
Step 3 – Upload Academic Documents
Upload required academic documents such as marksheets, certificates and ID proof.
Step 4 – Submit Entrance Scores
Provide entrance examination scores (JEE Main, CAT, NATA, GPAT, etc.) if applicable to your program.
Step 5 – Counselling / Interview
Attend counselling or interview round if required for the chosen program.
Step 6 – Pay Admission Fee
Pay the admission fee to confirm your seat in the selected program.
