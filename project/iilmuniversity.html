About IILM University

IILM University, Gurugram is a UGC-recognised private university with roots dating back to 1993, elevated to full university status under the Haryana Private Universities Act in 2018. Located in Sector 53, Gurugram — within the heart of India's corporate hub — IILM offers programmes across Management, Engineering, Liberal Arts, Psychology, Law, Design, Computer Science and Media. The university has produced hundreds of research papers and numerous patents between 2018 and 2024, and boasts a global alumni network of 16,000+ members across the USA, UK, Canada, Europe and Australia. Average packages stand at ₹8–8.6 LPA with top packages reaching ₹24 LPA.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply Online through IILM Admission Portal
Visit iilm.edu.in and register on the admission portal. Fill the application form with personal and academic details. Select your preferred school and programme.
Step 2 — Upload Academic Documents
Upload scanned copies of 10th and 12th marksheets, graduation degree (for PG), entrance scorecard, category certificate, and passport-size photographs.
Step 3 — Appear for Entrance Exam (if applicable)
B.Tech: JEE Main score. BA/BBA LL.B.: CLAT/CUET or IILM Test. B.Des.: Portfolio + Interview. BBA: Merit + Interview. MBA: CAT/XAT/MAT/CMAT/NMAT/GMAT. M.Tech: GATE. LL.M: Merit/Test.
Step 4 — Personal Interview & Profile Assessment
Shortlisted candidates attend a Personal Interview for BBA, MBA, B.Des. and law programmes. The process evaluates aptitude, communication skills and career goals.
Step 5 — Merit List & Document Verification
IILM releases merit lists based on entrance scores and interview performance. Carry original documents: marksheets, entrance scorecard, ID proof, and category certificate for verification.
Step 6 — Fee Payment, Hostel Allotment & Orientation
Pay the course fee online. BBA: ₹13.6 Lakh total · B.Tech: ₹10.8–12 Lakh total · BA/BBA LL.B.: ₹17 Lakh total · MBA: ₹9–12.3 Lakh total. Hostel allotment and orientation follow enrolment.
