About Bangalore University

Bangalore University (BU), established in 1964, is a premier State Public University located in the heart of India's Silicon Valley — Bengaluru, Karnataka. Spread across a sprawling 1,100-acre Jnana Bharathi Campus, BU is one of the largest affiliating universities in Asia. The university is accredited by NAAC with an A++ grade (CGPA 3.75) — the first university in Karnataka to achieve this distinction — and is recognized by UGC, AICTE, AIU, and ACU.

BU houses 43 Postgraduate Departments, a Postgraduate Centre at Kolar, 3 constituent colleges, and over 665 affiliated colleges (of which 115 offer PG courses). The university is structured into six faculties — Arts, Science, Commerce & Management, Education, Law, and Engineering. Its motto, "Gyanam, Vignana Sahitam", meaning "Knowledge along with Wisdom", reflects its commitment to holistic academic excellence and research. BU is ranked #65 by NIRF 2025 under the University category.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Register on UUCMS / BU Admission Portal
Visit the official BU admissions portal at bangaloreuniversity.ac.in or the UUCMS (Unified University and College Management System) portal. Create an account using your mobile number and email ID. Registration is free for most courses.
Step 2 — Fill Online Application Form
Complete the online application with personal details, academic qualifications, category information (SC/ST/OBC/Category-I/EWS/PwD), Karnataka domicile status, and preferred course or department. Pay the nominal application fee (₹100 – ₹500) online.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload scanned copies of 10th and 12th marksheets / 2nd PUC marks card, graduation marksheets (for PG), KCET / PGCET / KMAT / CMAT scorecards (as applicable), category certificate, income certificate, domicile certificate, ID proof, and passport photographs.
Step 4 — Merit List / Entrance Examination
For UG Arts, Commerce, and Science programs, admission is merit-based on 2nd PUC / 12th marks. B.E. / B.Tech admissions via KCET through affiliated engineering colleges. MBA and MCA admissions through PGCET / KMAT. Merit lists are published on the BU UUCMS portal.
Step 5 — Counselling / Document Verification
Shortlisted candidates are called for physical document verification at the university or affiliated college. Bring originals of all uploaded documents: marksheets, category certificate, domicile proof, transfer certificate, migration certificate (if applicable), and gap certificate.
Step 6 — Fee Payment and Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed tuition fee online via the UUCMS portal or at the college fee counter. Submit the signed admission form and required documents to confirm your seat. Collect your enrollment number and admission confirmation receipt for the academic year.

Placement Notes

Bangalore University's placement ecosystem benefits enormously from its location in Bengaluru — India's IT capital. MBA, MCA, ME/M.Tech, and LLM students receive the strongest placement support, with a 60%+ placement rate for professional courses. Placement is facilitated by both university departments and individual affiliated college placement cells. As per NIRF 2025 data, 1,441 PG-level students were placed with a median salary of ₹4.5 LPA. Government sector recruitment through KPSC, UPSC, SSC, and banking exams remains a dominant pathway for a large segment of graduates, particularly in Arts and Science streams.
