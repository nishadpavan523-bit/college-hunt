About NSU

National Sanskrit University (NSU), formerly known as Rashtriya Sanskrit Vidyapeetha, is a prestigious Central University located in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh. Established in 1961, it is one of India's foremost institutions dedicated to the study, research, and promotion of Sanskrit and allied classical disciplines. The university holds a coveted NAAC A+ Grade accreditation and is recognised by the UGC and the Ministry of Education, Government of India. NSU offers a wide spectrum of programmes — from traditional Sanskrit Shastri and Acharya programmes to modern disciplines like Computer Science and Mathematics — making it a unique bridge between ancient knowledge systems and contemporary education.

Admission Process
UG Admission: Apply via CUET-UG (cuet.samarth.ac.in) for most UG programmes. Some admissions are on merit. NSU portal: nsktu.ac.in
Step 1 — Register for CUET-UG / CUET-PG
UG admissions: Register and appear for CUET-UG conducted by NTA at cuet.samarth.ac.in. PG admissions: Register for CUET-PG. Diploma/Certificate: Apply directly on the NSU portal for merit-based seats.
Step 2 — Appear in the Entrance Examination
Appear in CUET-UG or CUET-PG on the scheduled date. The test covers domain-specific subjects and language proficiency. Sanskrit background is assessed for B.A. Shastri and M.A. Sanskrit aspirants.
Step 3 — Check Merit List / Score Card
After result declaration, NSU releases programme-wise merit lists on nsktu.ac.in based on CUET scores or academic merit. Check your rank and eligibility for counselling rounds.
Step 4 — Document Verification & Counselling
Attend counselling as per schedule. Carry 10+2 or graduation marksheets, caste certificate (if applicable), CUET score card, domicile proof, and passport-size photographs for verification.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allocation
Pay the programme fee as per category. Apply for hostel accommodation separately. Separate hostels are available for boys and girls with mess and dining facilities on campus.
Step 6 — PhD Admission (Separate Process)
PhD: NSU conducts its own entrance test followed by a personal interview. UGC-NET JRF holders may receive a direct interview call. Apply at nsktu.ac.in during the PhD notification window.
