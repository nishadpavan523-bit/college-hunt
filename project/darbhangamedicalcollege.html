About DMCH

Darbhanga Medical College and Hospital (DMCH) is one of the oldest and most prestigious government medical institutions in Bihar. Originally established as the Temple Medical School in 1925, it was upgraded to a full-fledged medical college in 1946 through the efforts of the Darbhanga royal family, particularly Maharaja Rameshwar Singh and Maharaja Kameshwar Singh. Affiliated with the Bihar University of Health Sciences and recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC), DMCH serves as a major tertiary-care teaching hospital for North Bihar and the Mithila region. It provides undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and medical training programs along with extensive healthcare services to millions of patients from Darbhanga, Madhubani, Samastipur, Saharsa, Supaul, and nearby districts.

Admission Process – MBBS
Step 1 — Appear for NEET-UG
Qualify the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET-UG) conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA). NEET-UG is mandatory for all MBBS admissions.
Step 2 — All India Quota Counselling (MCC)
15% of seats are filled through All India Quota (AIQ) counselling conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC). Register and participate in MCC counselling rounds.
Step 3 — Bihar State Counselling
85% of seats are filled through Bihar State Medical Counselling. Register with the Bihar Combined Entrance Competitive Examination Board (BCECEB) and participate in state counselling rounds.
Step 4 — Seat Allotment & Reporting
Upon seat allotment, report to DMCH with original documents including 10+2 marksheets, NEET scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), and identity proof.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Complete fee payment as per government-regulated fee schedule and complete enrolment formalities at the institution.
Admission Process – MD/MS (PG)
Step 1 — Qualify NEET-PG
Appear and qualify NEET-PG conducted by the National Board of Examinations (NBE). NEET-PG is mandatory for all MD/MS admissions.
Step 2 — National & State Counselling
Participate in MCC PG counselling for All India Quota seats and Bihar State counselling for state quota seats based on NEET-PG rank.
Step 3 — Document Verification & Joining
Submit MBBS degree certificate, internship completion certificate, NEET-PG scorecard, and other required documents. Complete joining formalities at DMCH.
