About AIIMS Delhi

All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS) Delhi is a premier medical institution and Institute of National Importance, established in 1956 under the AIIMS Act. Located in New Delhi, it is recognized by the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Government of India, and is consistently ranked #1 Medical College in India by NIRF. AIIMS Delhi encompasses a Medical College, Super Speciality Centre, Nursing College, and multiple research centres within its 213-acre hospital complex. It is recognized by the National Medical Commission (NMC) and is among the most affordable and prestigious medical institutions in the country.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify Entrance Examination
Appear and qualify in the relevant entrance exam: NEET-UG for MBBS, INI-CET for MD/MS/MDS, INI-SS for DM/M.Ch, or AIIMS Entrance for Nursing and Paramedical.
Step 2 — Register at AIIMS Examination Portal
Visit the official AIIMS examination portal and register with valid email and mobile number to initiate the admission process.
Step 3 — Participate in Counselling
Based on your rank and score, participate in the centralized online counselling process conducted by MCC or AIIMS for seat allotment.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, degree certificates (for PG), NEET/INI-CET scorecard, category certificate, ID proof, and passport photographs.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is done based on merit, category, and preference. Candidates are notified through the official portal.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the nominal admission fee online and complete all enrolment formalities to confirm your seat at AIIMS Delhi.

Placement Notes

AIIMS Delhi focuses on medical training, internships, residency, and research rather than traditional corporate placements. Almost all MBBS graduates pursue MD/MS, research, or government medical services. Senior Residents receive stipends of ₹1.1–1.5 Lakh+ per month. AIIMS Delhi graduates are highly sought after in India and abroad, with strong opportunities in clinical practice, academics, and medical research.
