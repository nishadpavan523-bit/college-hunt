About GRAU

Guru Ravidas Ayurved University (GRAU), established by the Government of Punjab in 2011, is located at Kharkan, Una Road, Hoshiarpur, Punjab. Recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC), GRAU functions as the affiliating and regulatory authority for Ayurvedic, Homoeopathic and Unani colleges across Punjab. It is one of the few specialized AYUSH universities in North India dedicated to promoting and regulating quality AYUSH education. The university operates under the chancellorship of the Governor of Punjab and covers disciplines of Ayurveda, Homoeopathy and Unani at UG, PG and PhD levels through affiliated colleges statewide.

Admission Process (UG)
Step 1 — Qualify NEET-UG
Appear and qualify in NEET-UG, the mandatory national entrance exam for BAMS, BHMS, and BUMS admissions in all affiliated colleges.
Step 2 — Register for Punjab AYUSH Counselling
Register on the official state counselling portal and fill the online application form with required course preferences.
Step 3 — Upload Required Documents
Upload mandatory documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, NEET scorecard, transfer certificate, character certificate, domicile certificate, category certificate (if applicable), Aadhaar card, and photographs.
Step 4 — Participate in Counselling Rounds
Attend counselling rounds based on NEET merit rank. Choose preferred colleges from affiliated AYUSH colleges across Punjab.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment, Fee Payment & Reporting
Accept allotted seat, pay the required fees online, and report to the college with all original documents for final admission confirmation.
Admission Process (PG)
Step 1 — Qualify AIAPGET
Appear for the All India AYUSH Post Graduate Entrance Test (AIAPGET), the mandatory national entrance exam for MD Ayurveda and MD Homoeopathy.
Step 2 — Participate in Counselling
Participate in AIAPGET counselling for seat allotment in PG programs at affiliated AYUSH colleges under GRAU.
Step 3 — Document Verification
Submit UG degree certificate, internship completion certificate, AIAPGET scorecard, registration certificate, and identity proof for verification.
Step 4 — Fee Submission & Admission Confirmation
Complete fee payment and confirm admission at the allotted institution within the stipulated deadline.
