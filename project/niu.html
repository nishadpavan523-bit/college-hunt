About NIU

Noida International University (NIU) is a UGC-approved private university established in 2010, located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh. Spread across an approximately 75-acre residential campus, NIU offers over 150 programs across undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral levels. The university caters to disciplines spanning Engineering, Management, Law, Medical Sciences, Journalism, Agriculture, Nursing, Sciences, and Liberal Arts. NIU is recognized by multiple statutory bodies including BCI, COA, PCI, and INC, and hosts students from multiple countries, reflecting its international exposure and diverse campus culture.

UG Admission Process
Step 1 — Online Application
Visit the official NIU website (niu.edu.in) and fill out the online application form for your desired program.
Step 2 — Merit / Entrance Evaluation
UG admissions are based on 10+2 merit. Some professional programs (B.Pharm, B.Sc. Nursing) may require NEET; B.Arch requires NATA score.
Step 3 — Document Submission
Upload 10th & 12th marksheets, transfer certificate, category certificate (if applicable), and ID proof to the admissions portal.
Step 4 — Merit List & Seat Confirmation
Merit list is published on the university website. Confirm your seat by paying the admission fee within the stipulated time.
Step 5 — Scholarship Assessment
Scholarships are awarded based on academic performance. Apply for applicable scholarships (merit, sports, EWS, government schemes) at the time of admission.
PG / PhD Admission Process
MBA — Apply via Online Portal
Submit CAT / MAT / CMAT scores or appear for the NIU institute-level entrance exam. Group Discussion and Interview may be part of the selection.
M.Tech — GATE Score / Merit
Apply with a valid GATE score in your relevant engineering discipline, or via merit in cases where GATE is not mandatory.
M.Sc. / M.A. / M.Com / LL.M. — Merit
Admission based on graduation merit. Apply through the online portal with academic credentials and relevant documents.
PhD — Entrance Test + Interview
Apply via the official portal. Appear for the PhD entrance test followed by an interview before the departmental selection committee. Merit list published after evaluation.
