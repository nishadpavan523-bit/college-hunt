About VNSGU

Veer Narmad South Gujarat University (VNSGU), established in 1967 and named after the celebrated Gujarati poet-reformer Veer Narmad, is the primary State University for South Gujarat, headquartered in Surat, Gujarat. It is a UGC-recognized, NAAC B++-accredited affiliating university, overseeing more than 200+ affiliated colleges across Surat, Tapi, Navsari, and Dangs districts. VNSGU offers programs spanning Arts, Commerce, Science, Engineering, Law, Education, Management, Home Science, and Pharmacy. It is recognized under sections 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act. The university plays a pivotal role in providing affordable higher education to students from South Gujarat's textile-industrial and tribal belt regions.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for GUJCET / JEE Main (B.E./B.Tech), ACPC Gujarat process, GLSAT (Law), MAT/CMAT (MBA), GPAT (M.Pharm), VNSGU PET (PG Arts/Science/Commerce), GATE (M.Tech), or UGC-NET/CSIR (PhD) as per chosen program.
Step 2 — Register on VNSGU / ACPC / Centralized Portal
For B.E./B.Tech: register on ACPC Gujarat Portal. For UG Arts/Commerce/Science: register through VNSGU's affiliated college admission portal or directly with the respective affiliated college. For PG: register on VNSGU PG Admission Portal.
Step 3 — Fill Choices & Preferences
Fill preferred programs and affiliated colleges on the portal. ACPC handles centralized engineering admissions through multiple rounds based on GUJCET/JEE merit and category.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit 10th & 12th marksheets, entrance exam scorecard, caste certificate (if applicable), domicile certificate (Gujarat domicile for state quota), PwD certificate, income certificate, and ID/address proof.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment & Counselling
Seat allotment is merit-based. Multiple rounds are held. Gujarat state-quota seats are available for domicile candidates. Management and NRI quota seats are available in affiliated engineering colleges.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrollment
Pay prescribed fees online/offline and complete enrollment at the allotted college or university department within the deadline to confirm admission.

Placement Notes

VNSGU's placement activity is primarily driven by its affiliated engineering colleges and management institutes rather than the main campus directly. Surat's booming economy in textiles, diamonds, pharmaceuticals, and IT creates strong local demand for VNSGU graduates. The Placement Cell at university-level departments and affiliated colleges conducts regular campus drives. MBA and B.E. students generally see better placement rates, while Arts and basic Science graduates often pursue higher studies or competitive exams. The MYSY scheme and ACPC-linked placements further supplement career support for economically weaker students.
