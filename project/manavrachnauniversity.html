About Manav Rachna University

Manav Rachna University (MRU) is one of the leading private universities in Haryana and Delhi NCR, located at Sector 43, Aravalli Hills, Faridabad, Haryana. Recognized under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956 and AICTE approved for Engineering programs, MRU is part of the prestigious Manav Rachna Educational Institutions (MREI) group. The university is known for industry-oriented education, research-driven learning, global collaborations, and modern infrastructure. MRU houses 5+ distinct schools including Engineering, Business, Law, Education & Humanities, and Sciences — offering Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Doctoral programs. With a focus on skill-based education, innovation, entrepreneurship, and practical exposure through internships and industry projects, MRU is a top choice for students across the Delhi NCR region.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Visit the Official Admission Portal
Navigate to the Manav Rachna University official admission portal and register online by creating your applicant account.
Step 2 — Fill Application Form & Upload Documents
Complete the online application form and upload your academic documents, ID proof, and recent photographs.
Step 3 — Appear in MRNAT or Applicable Entrance Exam
Appear for MRNAT or submit national-level scores: JEE Main (B.Tech), CLAT (Law), GATE (M.Tech), or CAT / MAT / XAT (MBA) as applicable to your chosen course.
Step 4 — Counselling & Document Verification
Attend the scheduled counselling session. Submit original marksheets, degree certificates, category certificate, entrance scorecard, and ID proof for verification.
Step 5 — Pay Admission Fee & Complete Enrolment
Pay the admission fee online for seat confirmation and complete all enrolment formalities to begin your academic journey at MRU.
