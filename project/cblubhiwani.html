About CBLU

Chaudhary Bansi Lal University (CBLU), Bhiwani is a state public university established in 2014 by the Haryana State Government, named after Bansi Lal — a former Chief Minister of Haryana. Recognised by UGC under 2(f) & 12B and approved by AICTE and PCI, the university serves over 50,000 students across 22 teaching departments and 66 affiliated colleges spread across Bhiwani and Charkhi Dadri. CBLU offers 53 UG and PG programmes in Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Pharmacy, Humanities, Social Sciences, Management, Commerce, Law, Education and Sports Sciences. The university has 18 MOUs with industry partners and awards scholarships worth ₹2 crore annually.

Admission Process — PG Courses (via KU CET)
Step 1 — Register for KU CET / MDU CET
PG admissions to most courses at CBLU are through Kurukshetra University Common Entrance Test (KU CET) or MDU (Maharshi Dayanand University) CET. Register on the respective portal as announced annually.
Step 2 — Appear in Entrance Test
Appear in the KU CET / MDU CET for your chosen PG discipline. MBA additionally accepts CAT/MAT scores. M.Pharm accepts GPAT. Scores are used for merit-based allotment to CBLU.
Step 3 — Apply on CBLU Admission Portal
Visit cblu.ac.in and register. Fill the online application form with academic details and entrance scorecard. Pay application fee online (₹600–₹1,000 depending on course). Last date for 2026-27: 27 May 2026.
Step 4 — Merit List & Counselling
CBLU releases category-wise merit lists based on KU CET/MDU CET scores. Multiple rounds of counselling are held. Check merit lists at cblu.ac.in regularly.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Bring original graduation degree/marksheets, entrance scorecard, category certificate, domicile certificate (Haryana), ID proof, and passport-size photos for in-person verification.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Enrolment
Pay the course fee online. M.Sc.: ₹20,670–61,000 total · MBA: ₹77,700 total · M.A.: ₹12,000–24,600 total. Confirm seat and receive student ID. Hostel allotment (₹25,000–40,000/yr) follows enrolment.

Placement Notes

CBLU has a dedicated Training & Placement Cell that organises campus drives, industry visits and mock interviews for final-year students. The university's 18 MoUs with industry partners provide internship and placement opportunities. Pharmacy and Biotechnology graduates find opportunities in pharma companies and research organisations. MBA graduates enter banking, FMCG and management roles. Government job preparation (HPSC, HSSC, SSC, banking exams) is also a popular career pathway for most CBLU graduates due to its affordable, government-subsidised fee structure and state university status.
