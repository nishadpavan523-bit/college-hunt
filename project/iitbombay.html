About IIT Bombay

Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay or IITB) is one of India's most prestigious Institutes of National Importance, established in 1958 and located in Powai, Mumbai, Maharashtra. It is governed by the Institutes of Technology Act under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. IITB consistently ranks among the Top 3 Engineering Institutes in India (NIRF) and enjoys strong global rankings. The institute offers programs across Engineering, Science, Design, Management, Humanities, and Interdisciplinary Research. Admission to UG programs is through JEE Advanced + JoSAA counselling, while PG admissions are through GATE / JAM / CAT / CEED / UCEED.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Qualify the Relevant Entrance Exam
Appear for JEE Advanced (for B.Tech / Dual Degree), UCEED (for B.Des), GATE (for M.Tech / PhD), IIT JAM (for M.Sc), CAT (for MBA), or CEED (for M.Des) as applicable to your chosen program.
Step 2 — Register on the JoSAA / Official Portal
For B.Tech and Dual Degree: register on JoSAA (josaa.nic.in). For PG programs: register on the IITB admissions portal (portal.iitb.ac.in) or the respective exam authority's counselling portal with your scorecard and personal details.
Step 3 — Fill College & Course Preferences
During JoSAA counselling, fill and lock your choice of IITs and courses in order of preference. Multiple rounds of seat allotment are conducted. For PG, choices are submitted on the respective department portal.
Step 4 — Document Verification
Submit original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, JEE Advanced / GATE / JAM / CAT scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), domicile and ID proof, PwD certificate (if applicable), and passport photographs.
Step 5 — Seat Allotment
Seat allotment is purely based on JEE Advanced / GATE / JAM merit rank and category. For M.Tech, GATE score + written test + interview may apply. Multiple rounds are held until all seats are filled.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the prescribed semester fee online and report to IIT Bombay for physical verification and enrollment within the stipulated deadline to secure your admission.

Placement Notes

IIT Bombay is consistently among India's strongest placement-performing institutes, with hundreds of recruiters across Software, AI, Quant, Consulting, Finance, and Core Engineering sectors. The institute records multiple crore-plus offers every placement season, including 20+ offers above ₹1 Crore. A large number of students receive Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs) through summer internships. The excellent startup ecosystem through SINE and strong alumni network provides further career support. IITB alumni hold leadership positions at global companies and research institutions worldwide.
