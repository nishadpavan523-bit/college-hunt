About University of Allahabad

The University of Allahabad, popularly known as Allahabad University (AU), is one of India's oldest and most prestigious central universities, established in 1887. Located in Prayagraj (Allahabad), Uttar Pradesh, it holds the distinguished status of a Central University recognized by the UGC and the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Accredited with NAAC "A+" Grade, the university is celebrated for its rich academic heritage and offers a wide array of programs across Arts, Science, Commerce, Law, Management, Computer Science, Engineering, and Research Studies. It offers UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate, and Ph.D. programs across multiple disciplines on a vibrant urban residential campus.

Admission Process (UG & PG)
Step 1 — Appear for the Entrance Exam
Register and appear for CUET-UG (most UG programs), JEE Main (B.Tech), CUET-PG / PGAT (PG programs), or GATE (M.Tech) as applicable.
Step 2 — Apply on the University Admission Portal
Register at the Allahabad University admission portal, fill the application form, and upload all required academic documents and certificates.
Step 3 — Counselling & Seat Allotment
Participate in the university counselling process. Seats are allotted based on entrance scores, merit, and category reservations. The university uses separate admission phases and subject selection processes.
Step 4 — Physical Document Verification
Attend in-person document verification with original marksheets, degree certificates, category certificate, entrance scorecard, and valid ID proof.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Enrolment Confirmation
Pay the admission and semester/annual fees online to confirm your enrolment and secure your allocated seat.

Placement Notes

The University of Allahabad facilitates campus placements for its engineering, management, and computer science graduates through its placement cell. B.Tech and MCA graduates are placed in leading IT companies including TCS, Infosys, Wipro, and HCL. MBA graduates secure roles in management and corporate sectors. The university's strong academic reputation also generates significant placements in government services, PSUs, and civil services. Law graduates join courts, legal firms, and the judiciary. A large number of graduates pursue higher studies, research fellowships, and academic careers leveraging the university's legacy and strong alumni network.
