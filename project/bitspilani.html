About BITS Pilani
⚡ Deemed University of the Highest Category — Ranked Among India's Top 25 Universities (NIRF)

The Birla Institute of Technology and Science (BITS), Pilani is one of India's most prestigious and autonomous technical universities, established in 1964 and accorded the status of a Deemed University by the Government of India. Accredited with NAAC A+ Grade and ranked consistently in the NIRF Top 25, BITS Pilani is globally recognised for excellence in Engineering, Sciences, and Management. The institute is uniquely known for its Practice School (PS) programme — one of India's first industry internship models — and the Dual Degree system that lets students combine any two programmes simultaneously. BITS conducts its own national entrance exam, BITSAT, drawing over 3 lakh applicants each year for its four campuses across Pilani, Goa, Hyderabad, and Dubai.

Admission Process — BITSAT
Step 1 — Apply for BITSAT
Register on the official BITS Pilani admissions portal (bitsadmission.com) for BITSAT. BITSAT is BITS's own computer-based entrance exam conducted across 60+ cities. Eligibility: 10+2 or equivalent with PCM / PCB and minimum 75% aggregate marks. Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics are mandatory subjects.
Step 2 — Appear in BITSAT (Online CBT)
BITSAT is a 3-hour online computer-based test with 130 questions from Physics (40), Chemistry (40), English + Logical Reasoning (25), and Mathematics / Biology (45). Correct answers: +3 marks; Wrong: −1 mark. Candidates who complete 130 questions under time can attempt 12 bonus questions.
Step 3 — Board-Based Direct Admission (Special Provision)
Candidates scoring 75% or above aggregate in 12th board AND topping their respective board (1st rank) may be offered direct admission without BITSAT. This is a special provision for outstanding board toppers.
Step 4 — Apply to BITS with BITSAT Score
After BITSAT results, apply to BITS on the admissions portal. Submit BITSAT score, 12th board marks, preferred campus, and programme. Candidates must have 75%+ aggregate in 12th board to be eligible for final admission.
Step 5 — Iteration-Based Seat Allotment
BITS conducts multiple iterations of seat allotment based on BITSAT merit rank, campus preference, and programme choice. Candidates must log in to accept / reject allotment within the given window. Upgrading to better programme / campus is possible in subsequent iterations.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Admission Confirmation
Pay the semester fee (₹5.9 Lakh+/semester) online to confirm admission. Report to the allotted campus with original documents: 10th & 12th marksheets, BITSAT scorecard, category certificate, migration certificate, and passport photos.
