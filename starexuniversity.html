About Starex University

Starex University, established in 2016, is a private university located in Gurugram (Gurgaon), Haryana, spread across a 35-acre green campus on NH-48. The university holds prestigious NAAC 'A' Grade accreditation and is approved by UGC, AICTE, PCI, and BCI. It operates through 11 Schools of Study offering 80+ courses across UG, PG, PhD, Diploma and Integrated programmes. Starex has a placement rate of above 80% with an average CTC of ₹3.5–4.5 LPA for UG graduates. The university is ranked in NIRF 2023–24 under the UG 4-Year category.

Admission Process
Apply Online: Visit www.starexuniversity.com or admission.starexuniversity.co.in to apply. Toll-Free: 1800 1033 123
Step 1 — Visit Official Website
Go to www.starexuniversity.com or admission.starexuniversity.co.in to start your application process.
Step 2 — Register & Fill Online Application Form
Create an account, fill in personal and academic details, and select your preferred course and specialisation.
Step 3 — Upload Documents & Pay Application Fee
Upload scanned copies of 10th, 12th marksheets, ID proof, passport photograph and any relevant certificates. Pay the application fee online.
Step 4 — Appear for Entrance Exam (If Applicable)
B.Tech: JEE Main / HSTES / SUET. MBA: CAT / XAT / MAT. M.Tech: GATE preferred. Law: CLAT / SUET. PhD: NET/GATE or University Entrance Test + Interview. Some courses are merit-based (no entrance exam required).
Step 5 — Document Verification & Seat Allotment
Shortlisted candidates are called for document verification. Original marksheets, transfer certificate, caste certificate (if applicable) and migration certificate are required.
Step 6 — Pay Admission Fee to Confirm Enrolment
Pay the programme fee (category/course-wise) to confirm your seat. Hostel booking can be done simultaneously. Room type options: Double Sharing (Non-AC / AC) or Triple Sharing.

Career Pathways

Starex University has a dedicated Placement Cell that bridges industry-academia connections. B.Tech graduates are placed in IT, core engineering, and tech companies. MBA and BBA graduates target banking, FMCG, finance, and consulting firms. Law graduates pursue corporate law, litigation, and government services. Pharmacy graduates join pharmaceutical and healthcare companies. Hotel management graduates are placed in hospitality chains and food service industry.
