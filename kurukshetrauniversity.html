About Kurukshetra University

Kurukshetra University is one of the premier state universities of India, established in 1956 by the first President of India, Dr. Rajendra Prasad. Located near the famous Brahma Sarovar in Kurukshetra, Haryana, the university started with only the Department of Sanskrit and has grown into a major multidisciplinary institution. It holds a prestigious NAAC A++ Grade accreditation and is UGC Recognized. KUK offers UG, PG, Diploma, Certificate and Ph.D. programs across Arts, Science, Commerce, Engineering, Law, Education, Management and Pharmacy. The university has more than 290 affiliated colleges and spreads across a lush 473-acre campus with academic blocks, sports complexes, hostels, research centres and digital learning facilities.

Admission Process
ℹ️ Admission Portal: Apply via KUK official admission portal (kuk.ac.in). Professional programs require qualifying national/state-level entrance exams. Centralized counselling is conducted for most programs.
Step 1 — Register on the Admission Portal
Visit kuk.ac.in and register on the official university admission portal. Create a new account with a valid email ID and mobile number.
Step 2 — Fill Online Application Form
Fill in the online application form with personal, academic and category details. Choose your preferred program (UG / PG / Ph.D.) and specialization.
Step 3 — Upload Academic & Category Documents
Upload scanned copies of 10+2 marksheet, graduation marksheet (for PG), caste certificate, domicile/category certificate and entrance exam scorecard (JEE/GATE/CAT/NET as applicable).
Step 4 — Counselling & Seat Allotment
Participate in centralized counselling conducted by the university. Seat allotment is based on merit list / entrance rank and category. Choose preferred program and campus.
Step 5 — Document Verification
Attend document verification at the allotted department/college. Carry all original documents along with photocopies for physical verification.
Step 6 — Fee Payment & Hostel Allocation
Pay the admission fee online as per your program and category. Apply for hostel accommodation separately. ~25 hostels available for boys, girls and international students on campus.

Career Pathways

KUK graduates are well-regarded across the IT, government, legal and education sectors. Professional courses such as engineering, management and computer applications generally have better placement opportunities. B.Tech and MCA graduates are frequently recruited by TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCL and Deloitte. Law graduates pursue judicial services, advocacy and corporate law. Arts and Science graduates often go into teaching, civil services, research and the public sector. MBA graduates find opportunities in banking, finance and corporate management.
