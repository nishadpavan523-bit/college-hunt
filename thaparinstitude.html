About Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology

Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (TIET), commonly known as Thapar University, is one of India's premier deemed universities established in 1956 in Patiala, Punjab. Accredited with NAAC "A+" Grade and recognized by UGC and AICTE, the institute is renowned for its excellence in Engineering, Technology, Management, Computer Applications, Sciences, Artificial Intelligence, and Biotechnology. With a sprawling 250-acre campus, TIET boasts global collaborations with institutions such as Trinity College Dublin and the University of Queensland, making it one of the most internationally connected engineering universities in India.

Admission Process
Step 1 — Apply through Official Portal
Register on the TIET official admission portal at thapar.edu and fill the application form with accurate details.
Step 2 — Submit Entrance Exam Scores
Upload JEE Main (B.E.) / CAT / XAT / NMAT / CMAT (MBA) / GATE (M.Tech) or relevant examination scores as applicable.
Step 3 — Upload Documents
Submit academic marksheets, category certificates, ID proof, and passport-size photographs through the portal.
Step 4 — Check Merit List & Counselling
Monitor the official merit list. Attend counselling or interview rounds if applicable for your program.
Step 5 — Fee Payment & Seat Confirmation
Pay the admission fee online via the university portal to confirm your seat and complete enrolment formalities.
